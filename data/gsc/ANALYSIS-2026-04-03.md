# Duplicate URL (trailing slash)

`/en/dashashwamedh-ghat-ganga-aarti-timing` (55k impr) and `/en/dashashwamedh-ghat-ganga-aarti-timing/` (11k impr) both appear separately. `next.config.js:171` has `trailingSlash: false`. But the **frontmatter `canonical:` field of `content/en/dashashwamedh-ghat-ganga-aarti-timing.md` contains a trailing slash**, contradicting the framework. Result: split signals.

Audit all content files for `canonical:` values ending in `/` and strip — they should match `trailingSlash: false`.

# Top 5 recommended improvements, in order

1. **Rewrite titles for the 6 "leaking" pages** to be less snippet-like. Take the literal answer out of the title and force a click. Example for `dashashwamedh-ghat-ganga-aarti-timing`:
   - Now: "Ganga Aarti Time Varanasi 2026: 5:45 PM Winter / 6:45 PM Summer"
   - Try: "Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh: Exact Time, Where to Stand, How to Beat the Crowd"
   The exact minute should still be in the page body for ranking, but pulling it out of the title forces users to click for the rest.

2. **Kill the `-2025` slugs in our own content/internal links/sitemap.** Redirects are correct; what's wrong is that **`docs/CONTENT-TRANSLATION-AUDIT.json` and likely the sitemap still reference `assi-ghat-aarti-timings-2025.md`**. Grep all source for `-2025` slugs and replace with `-2026` so we stop emitting old canonicals. Then re-submit the sitemap.

3. **Fix canonical trailing-slash mismatches.** Search `content/**/*.md` for `canonical: "https://www.kashitaxi.in/...//"` patterns (trailing `/`) and remove. At minimum: `content/en/dashashwamedh-ghat-ganga-aarti-timing.md` (confirmed).

4. **Apply the bike-rentals pattern to other commercial pages.** The site has tour/taxi/booking pages that should hit similar CTRs. Compare `/en/morning-boat-ride-varanasi-price` (1.32% CTR, pos 4.59) head meta vs. `/bike-rentals-varanasi` — the bike page leads with price in title; the boat page does not.

5. **Decide on US/desktop strategy.** 118k US impressions and 362k desktop impressions are real, but at <0.6% CTR they convert to almost no revenue. Either (a) write 1–2 trip-planning pillar pages aimed at the US/UK searcher, or (b) consciously deprioritize and don't be surprised by the CTR gap.
