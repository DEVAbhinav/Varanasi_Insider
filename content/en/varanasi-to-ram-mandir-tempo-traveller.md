## Ram Mandir Security & Documentation Checklist

- Carry original ID (Aadhaar/Passport) for each adult—mandatory at frisking.
- Bags larger than 12\" × 12\" are not permitted inside the complex. We provide lockable containers inside the Traveller.
- Liquid offerings are restricted; pack sealed sweets/fruits instead.
- Phones are allowed but photography near sanctum is prohibited. Keep tripod/selfie sticks in vehicle.
