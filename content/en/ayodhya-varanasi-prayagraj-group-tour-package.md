### Day 3: Varanasi to Prayagraj — Sangam, Snan & Sacred Stops

![Prayagraj — the Sangam convergence and Bade Hanuman Ji](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkntlqbwr/image/upload/kashitaxi/kashitaxi/prayagraj.jpg)
*The Triveni Sangam — where Ganga (lighter water) and Yamuna (greenish tint) visibly diverge. The mythical Saraswati joins underground.*

**122 km drive from Varanasi via NH-19 (~2 hr 20 min).** Good 4-lane divided highway, 2–3 toll plazas.

#### What the Prayagraj Stop Covers (4–5 hours for half-day; 8–10 hours for full day)

**Core spiritual stops (every group does these):**

1. **Triveni Sangam — Boat Ride + Snan (60–90 min)**
   Boatman rows to the exact point where the Ganga and Yamuna colours visibly split. Pilgrims perform holy dip (snan) and kalash puja. The Rigveda says: *"Those who bathe where the two rivers, white and dark, flow together, rise up to heaven."* Ashes of Gandhi (1949) and Vajpayee (2018) were immersed here.
   - **Shared boat:** ₹100–200/person. **Private boat:** ₹500–1,500 (negotiable, seasonal).
   - **Panda (priest) touts are persistent** at the ghats. Our pre-arranged guide manages this so the group doesn't get hustled.
   - Best time: early morning (5:30–7:00 AM) for light and calm water.

2. **Bade Hanuman Ji Temple (20–30 min)**
   200–300m walk from Sangam ghat. Houses a unique **20-foot reclining (lete hue) Hanuman murti** — the only one of its kind in North India, partially below ground level. During monsoon, Ganga floodwaters rise to "touch Hanuman's feet" — a devotional event. Believed to be svayambhu (self-manifested). Free entry, 5 AM–12 PM / 3 PM–10 PM. **Crowded Tuesdays and Saturdays.**

3. **Akshaya Vat — Immortal Banyan Tree (30–45 min)**
   Sacred fig tree inside the Patalpuri Temple complex within the Allahabad Fort compound. Mentioned in the Ramayana — tradition says Rama rested under this tree. Emperor Jahangir reportedly cut it and hammered red-hot iron onto the stump to kill it — it regrew within a year. Access has improved significantly post-Maha Kumbh 2025 (previously required military permission). **~6 AM–6 PM.** Some inner fort areas remain restricted (Indian Army Ordnance Depot since 1857).

**Optional stops (full-day groups):**

4. **Anand Bhawan — Nehru Museum (45 min–1.5 hrs)**
   The historic Nehru family home. Congress history exhibits, freedom struggle artifacts, Jawahar Planetarium. Adjacent Swaraj Bhavan also open. **Hours:** 9:30 AM–5:00 PM, Tue–Sun. **Closed Mondays.** Entry: ~₹20 (Indians), ~₹100–150 (foreigners).

5. **Khusrau Bagh** — Mughal garden with 3 sandstone mausoleums (Khusrau, Shah Begum). Free entry, well-maintained. 30 min.

| Model | Best For | Schedule |
| --- | --- | --- |

### Day 4: Ayodhya Main Darshan Day

![Ayodhya — Ram Janmabhoomi and the temple circuit](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkntlqbwr/image/upload/kashitaxi/kashitaxi/blogAyodhya.jpg)
*Ram Janmabhoomi darshan is the emotional peak of the circuit — schedule it first, the rest follows.*

**Key Ayodhya stops with practical details:**

1. **Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple (07:00 – 09:30)** — Inaugurated January 22, 2024. Entry FREE (trust warns against scam claims). Darshan in 2-hour windows, 7 AM–9 PM. **No phones, cameras, leather inside.** Lockers at Gate 3. Average 1–1.5 lakh daily visitors. 7 AM weekday slot = shortest queue (30–60 min). Weekend/festival: 2–4+ hours. The Nagara-style temple has 366 columns, zero iron/steel — entirely Bansi sandstone with 10,000 copper plates.

2. **Hanuman Garhi (09:30 – 10:15)** — **76 steep steps** to the fort-shaped temple. 6-inch Bal Hanuman on Anjani's lap, silver garbha griha, panoramic views. Free, ~5 AM–10 PM. **Elderly advisory:** no lift — recommend base-level darshan for mobility-limited pilgrims.

3. **Kanak Bhawan (10:15 – 10:45)** — "Sone Ka Ghar" with gold-crowned Ram-Sita idols. Free, ~8 AM–12 PM / 4 PM–9 PM.

4. **Sita Ki Rasoi + Dashrath Mahal (10:45 – 11:30)** — Symbolic royal kitchen with stone utensils + King Dasharatha's palace complex. Free.

5. **Nageshwarnath Temple (11:30 – 12:00)** — One of Ayodhya's few non-Rama sites. Ancient Shiva shrine, founded (per legend) by Kush. Free.

6. **Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi (~6:30 PM)** — Diyas floating on the Saryu, devotional chants, 30–45 min. More intimate than Ganga Aarti. Best vantage: central ghats.

Ayodhya can become tiring if the group tries to “finish everything.” The best package protects the emotional core of the day: Ram Mandir darshan first, then the rest.

## FAQ: Ayodhya Varanasi Prayagraj Group Tour Package

### What is the ideal duration for a group doing Ayodhya, Varanasi, and Prayagraj?

For most groups, **4 nights / 5 days** is the cleanest balance between devotion and fatigue management.

### Can we do Prayagraj as only a short stop?

Yes. Some groups want a Sangam-focused stop and then continue to Ayodhya. Others need an overnight. The right choice depends on age profile and departure planning.

### Is this package suitable for 20+ pilgrims?

Yes. In fact, this is where the package becomes commercially strong because transport coordination and per-head economics improve when the group is planned properly.

### Do you include meals in the package?

Meal structure is customizable. Breakfast is the easiest default. Many pilgrimage groups also choose a stricter sattvic meal plan so timing stays controlled.

### Can our group arrive in Varanasi and depart from Ayodhya?

Yes. That is one of the strongest formats for this package because it reduces unnecessary backtracking.

### What is the best time for this 3-city circuit?

October to March for best weather across all three cities. November is peak — Dev Deepawali in Varanasi and Kartik Purnima at Sangam align powerfully. March–April has Ram Navami crowds in Ayodhya — book 3–4 weeks ahead and expect 20–40% price surcharge.

### How long does the Prayagraj stop need?

A half-day (4–5 hours) covers the spiritual core: Sangam boat ride with snan, Bade Hanuman Ji Temple, and Akshaya Vat. A full day adds Anand Bhawan museum, Khusrau Bagh, and a relaxed pace. Most pilgrimage groups choose the half-day model and continue to Ayodhya the same day.

### Is the Sangam snan physically demanding?

The boat ride is comfortable. The snan (holy dip) requires stepping off the boat into waist-deep water at the confluence. Our boatmen and guides assist. For elderly or non-swimmers, kalash puja (symbolic offering) from the boat is an alternative — spiritually equivalent without the physical dip.
