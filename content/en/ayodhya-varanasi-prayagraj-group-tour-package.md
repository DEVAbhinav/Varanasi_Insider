## Recommended 4N/5D Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrive in Varanasi, Reset, Evening Ganga Aarti

- airport or station pickup
- hotel check-in and freshen-up buffer
- short briefing for the group leader
- evening Dashashwamedh or river-view Aarti plan based on group age and crowd tolerance

For large groups, we usually avoid forcing everyone into one crushed standing zone. Boat or reserved-view planning is safer and calmer.

### Day 2: Kashi Vishwanath, Kal Bhairav, South Varanasi, Optional Sarnath

- early darshan planning for Kashi Vishwanath
- Kal Bhairav and Annapurna sequence where appropriate
- breakfast and rest
- Sankat Mochan, Durga Kund, Tulsi Manas, BHU New Vishwanath
- optional Sarnath in the afternoon if the group still has energy

This day is where most bad package operators over-pack the itinerary. We keep it disciplined because the circuit still has Prayagraj and Ayodhya ahead.

### Day 3: Varanasi to Prayagraj, Sangam, Continue to Ayodhya or Overnight Prayagraj

Two models work here:

1. **Fast moving group:** Varanasi -> Prayagraj stop -> Ayodhya same day  
2. **Comfort-first group:** Varanasi -> Prayagraj overnight -> Ayodhya next morning

The right choice depends on:

- age profile
- reporting discipline
- how much time the group wants at Sangam
- departure city and ticket timing

For senior-heavy groups, the overnight Prayagraj model is usually stronger.

### Day 4: Ayodhya Main Darshan Day

- Ram Janmabhoomi focus
- Hanuman Garhi
- Kanak Bhawan
- Dashrath Mahal and lighter heritage stops depending on pace
- Saryu Aarti in the evening

Ayodhya can become tiring if the group tries to “finish everything.” The best package protects the emotional core of the day: Ram Mandir darshan first, then the rest.

### Day 5: Final Prayers and Departure

Departure can be arranged as:

- **Ayodhya out**
- **Lucknow out**
- **Varanasi return**

For many groups, **VNS in / AYJ or LKO out** is the cleanest commercial and operational pattern.

## FAQ: Ayodhya Varanasi Prayagraj Group Tour Package

### What is the ideal duration for a group doing Ayodhya, Varanasi, and Prayagraj?

For most groups, **4 nights / 5 days** is the cleanest balance between devotion and fatigue management.

### Can we do Prayagraj as only a short stop?

Yes. Some groups want a Sangam-focused stop and then continue to Ayodhya. Others need an overnight. The right choice depends on age profile and departure planning.

### Is this package suitable for 20+ pilgrims?

Yes. In fact, this is where the package becomes commercially strong because transport coordination and per-head economics improve when the group is planned properly.

### Do you include meals in the package?

Meal structure is customizable. Breakfast is the easiest default. Many pilgrimage groups also choose a stricter sattvic meal plan so timing stays controlled.

### Can our group arrive in Varanasi and depart from Ayodhya?

Yes. That is one of the strongest formats for this package because it reduces unnecessary backtracking.
