## What Is Pisach Mochan Kund (Vimala Teerth)?

Pisach Mochan Kund is an ancient sacred tank set **inland**, away from the main riverfront. Its name literally points to *liberation from the pisach (restless spirit) state*. Families come here specifically when a death was **unnatural or premature**, when last rites could not be completed properly, or when repeated hardship in the living family is attributed to an unsettled ancestor.

Because it is inland, it has one very practical advantage during July–September: **it stays accessible when the Ganga is in flood and the ghats are submerged.**

### The Belief System (Pitra Dosh)

In this tradition, a person who dies an **unnatural or premature death** — suicide, murder, a violent accident, drowning — may not attain a peaceful transition and is believed to remain trapped in a restless **pret / pisach yoni**. That unsettled ancestor is thought to create **Pitra Dosh** — a spiritual blockage that manifests as repeated hardship, obstruction, or misfortune in the living bloodline. The remedial rites here are intended to **sever those malevolent attachments, complete what the last rites could not, and pacify the restless spirit**, so the family line can move forward unobstructed.

You do not need to "prove" anything or believe in a specific way — the purohit simply performs the prescribed rite correctly on your family's behalf.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Pisach Mochan Kund and why do people visit it?**
Pisach Mochan Kund, also called Vimala Teerth, is an inland sacred tank in Varanasi visited by families dealing with unnatural deaths — accident, suicide, drowning, or violence. The rites here are believed to free a departed soul from a restless (pret/pisach) state and lift the resulting Pitra Dosh on the living.

**What is Narayan Nag Bali puja?**
Narayan Nag Bali is a two-part remedial rite: Narayan Bali performs the last rites for a soul denied a proper cremation or peace, and Nag Bali atones for the symbolic killing of a serpent. It is commonly performed for unnatural deaths and to relieve Pitra Dosh, guided by a specialist purohit.

**How is Pisach Mochan different from Shradh at Tulsi Ghat?**
Standard Shradh and Tarpan at Tulsi or Kedar Ghat are general annual rites for a natural death. Pisach Mochan Kund is specifically for unnatural or premature deaths and severe Pitra Dosh, using rites like Narayan Nag Bali and Tripindi Shradh. Many families do both.

**How much does Narayan Nag Bali or Tripindi Shradh cost in Varanasi?**
These specialist rites typically range ₹5,000–₹11,999 depending on scale, number of ancestors, and whether havan is included. We connect you with a verified purohit and quote the dakshina in writing beforehand.

**How long does the ritual take and how should we prepare?**
Most remedial rites run 3–5 hours and are best started at dawn. Carry the deceased's name, gotra, and cause/date of passing if known. We arrange samagri, a calm setting, and flood-safe transport since Pisach Mochan is inland.

**Can this rite be combined with Gaya Pind Daan?**
Yes. Families often perform the remedial rite at Pisach Mochan and then extend to Gaya for Pind Daan and Prayagraj for Tarpan. See our Kashi–Gaya–Prayag Pind Daan tour for itinerary and vehicle options.
