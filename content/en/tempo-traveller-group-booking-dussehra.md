- **Communication Issues:** Hard to coordinate via phone during noisy **Ravana Dahan events**
- **Higher Costs:** 3 separate cars cost more than 1 **Tempo Traveller Dussehra booking**

## FAQ: Tempo Traveller Group Booking

**Q: How far in advance should I book for Dussehra?**  
A: At least 48-72 hours for guaranteed availability. Festival dates (Oct 2-3) are high demand, so earlier booking ensures you get your preferred vehicle size.

**Q: Can we make stops for food/shopping during the trip?**  
A: Yes, within the booked duration. Popular stops include prasad shopping, traditional sweets, or dinner after festival events. Driver can suggest good places.

**Q: What if some group members want to leave early?**  
A: We can arrange split pickup times or drop-offs, but it requires advance planning. Generally, keeping everyone together works best for festival events.

**Q: Is the driver included in the passenger count?**  
A: No, driver has a separate seat. A 12-seater means 12 passenger seats plus driver's seat.

**Q: Can we bring festival purchases/prasad in the vehicle?**  
A: Absolutely! Our vehicles have ample luggage space and roof carriers for festival shopping, prasad, ritual items, etc.

**Q: Do you provide child seats?**  
A: We can arrange child seats with advance notice. All vehicles have seat belts for safety during festival travel.

**Q: What's included in the quoted price?**  
A: Vehicle, driver, fuel, waiting time, and basic maintenance. Excludes parking fees (if any), tolls for outstation trips, and driver meals for full-day bookings.
