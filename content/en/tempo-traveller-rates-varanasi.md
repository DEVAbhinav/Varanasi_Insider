## How Tempo Traveller Pricing Usually Works in Varanasi

One thin rate table is never enough because travellers compare three very different things under the same search:

- **airport transfer**
- **local city package**
- **outstation hire**

That is why the same vehicle may look “cheap” in one quote and “expensive” in another. The structure of the booking matters more than the headline number.

## Local vs Outstation vs Airport: The Real Difference

### Airport Transfer

Best when the job is simple: pickup, luggage loading, and direct drop. Airport pricing is usually easier to understand because the route is short and the waiting window is limited.

### Local Package

This works when your group needs one vehicle for **city darshan, temple hopping, lunch stops and return**. The question here is not just distance. It is whether the package fits your actual stop count and waiting time.

### Outstation Hire

This is where the fare logic changes most. For Ayodhya, Prayagraj, Vindhyachal, Bodh Gaya or longer circuits, you need to account for:

- per-km charging or fixed package structure
- daily minimum km
- tolls and parking
- driver allowance
- state taxes on multi-state runs

## What Usually Pushes the Rate Up

- premium seating layouts
- holiday and festival demand
- overnight halts
- multi-city circuits
- extra luggage or support gear
- unusually long waiting at one stop

The right question is not “what is the cheapest traveller?” It is “which quote fits our trip without surprise add-ons later?”
