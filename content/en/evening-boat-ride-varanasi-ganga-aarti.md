# Evening Boat Ride Varanasi: Watch Ganga Aarti from the Water (Differentiated 2026 Planning Guide)

> **TL;DR — book before the aarti rush:**
>
> - **Shared bench** → from ₹250 per seat
> - **Private rowboat** → around ₹3,000
> - **Seats vanish ~40 min before the Ganga Aarti** → board early for the central arc
> - **Photographers** → prep ISO 2000 and f2.8 for flame detail
>
> Micro-tip: carry a thin cushion — the wooden planks bruise during a 60-minute hold.

> **What's covered here:** Boat types, seat classes, boarding timing, river alignment, and photography settings for watching Ganga Aarti from the water. For the ceremony's full timing and crowd strategy, see the [Dashashwamedh timing guide](/en/dashashwamedh-ghat-ganga-aarti-timing).

## 3. Recommended Camera & Phone Settings (On‑Water)
- Mirrorless: ISO 2000, f/2.8, 1/80s (increase shutter once lamps fully lit).  
- Smartphone Night Mode: Lock exposure early; avoid overcompensating for flame brightness.  
- Stabilization: Brace elbows to knees; rowboat micro‑rock reduces blur vs motor vibration.  

## 4. Fare Negotiation Workflow (Practical Script)
1. Ask boatman: “Shared or private? Capacity?”  
2. Request base fare quote; counter at 70–75% if private.  
3. Confirm: duration (≥45 min ceremony + 10 min drift), number of passengers, life jackets.  
4. Pay small deposit only if leaving to get snacks (UPI or ₹100 cash).  
5. Reconfirm position 15 min pre‑start; minor repositioning still possible.

## 6. Safety & Compliance (On Water)
- Mandatory life jackets (enforce politely if missing).  
- No standing during lamp ignition (stability + courtesy).  
- Keep mobile lanyard / wrist strap to avoid river drops.
