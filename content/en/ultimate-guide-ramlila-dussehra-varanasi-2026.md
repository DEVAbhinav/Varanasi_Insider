## Important distinction: Ramnagar and Nati Imli

Ramnagar Ramlila is the long-form moving performance staged in Ramnagar, across the Ganga from central Varanasi. Nati Imli is a locality in Varanasi city with a separate famous Bharat Milap observance. Earlier pages on this site incorrectly treated Nati Imli as an area of Ramnagar and combined the two route plans.

Use the correct destination when arranging transport:

- For **Ramnagar Ramlila**, confirm the night's Ramnagar episode and location.
- For **Nati Imli Bharat Milap**, travel to Nati Imli in Varanasi city and follow the city's event-day traffic plan.

## How to plan without relying on an invented timetable

### Ramnagar Ramlila

1. Get the current daily programme from a district, tourism or organiser notice.
2. Confirm the episode location locally because the production uses multiple outdoor settings.
3. Ask about the current police diversion and last permitted vehicle point.
4. Keep the return pickup flexible and save the driver's details offline.

### Dussehra

The calendar date is confirmed, but individual Ravana Dahan venues, times and access controls can change. Check the district or police notice instead of assuming every ground starts at the same hour.

### Nati Imli Bharat Milap

The expected date is 21 October. Do not rely on the old page's claims about official wristbands, a Dhobi Ghat shuttle, fixed barricade times or a guaranteed royal-arrival schedule; no supporting official notice was found.

## Crowd and transport advice

- Expect walking beyond the last motorable point.
- Choose a clear group meeting place before entering a crowd.
- Carry water, essential medicines and a charged phone.
- Do not pay for a “VIP pass” unless the organiser or district publishes it.
- Follow police diversions even if a saved map suggests another route.
- Reconfirm commercial fares and inclusions in writing rather than relying on dated web-page prices.
