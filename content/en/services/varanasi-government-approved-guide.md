## 10. Practical tips so the guided day feels easy

### Dress & carry

- Modest clothing for temples (shoulders/knees covered)  
- Slip-off footwear  
- Small backpack only; leave excess at hotel when possible  
- Water, basic meds, phone power bank  
- Cash for tiny purchases; keep valuables minimal in dense lanes  

### Temple reality check

- Security rules and cloakrooms can change  
- Phones/bags may be restricted in inner zones  
- Official paid faster-access options exist; tout markups are not “official VIP”  
- For current assisted-darshan thinking, see [Kashi Vishwanath VIP / Sugam help](/en/services/kashi-vishwanath-vip-darshan-booking)

### Etiquette that matters

- At cremation ghats: quiet, no pyre photos, no drama  
- Ask before photographing people, priests, or sadhus  
- Follow the guide’s lead in narrow ritual spaces  
- Donate only when **you** choose — not under pressure  

### Pace like a local

Varanasi is not a mall crawl. Build one major sacred block, one river block, one rest block. That simple rhythm beats seven rushed pins on Google Maps.
