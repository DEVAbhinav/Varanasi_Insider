# Kashi Taxi Driver Partners: Meet the Faces Behind Your Ride

Our guests trust the people behind the wheel. This page is for the drivers who make every pickup, boat transfer, and outstation run smooth.

## Why This Page Exists
- Celebrate drivers with names, photos, and route specialties.
- Show guests the humans and training behind every safe ride.
- Give partners a single place to update profiles and feel proud of their work.

## Driver Photo Wall (Coming Alive)
- Each partner gets a headshot, first name, years driving, primary routes (airport, Tent City, outstation), and languages.
- Badges: police-verified, winter fog expert, women-first trips, night airport, tempo traveller.
- If you want your photo here, WhatsApp it to **94503 01573** with your name and routes.

## How We Select and Train Drivers
- **Verification:** Police check + Aadhaar revalidation every 12 months.
- **Ridecraft:** Route drills for airport → Ravidas Ghat, Tent City jetty, Assi/Dashashwamedh drops.
- **Safety:** Ghat-side etiquette, luggage handling, silent-word protocol, seniors-first assistance.
- **Winter & Fog Readiness:** Buffer timing, live flight tracking, diversion handling via Lucknow/Prayagraj.
- **Guest Care:** Language pointers, calm driving, zero honking near ghats, bottled water readiness.

## Partner Benefits
- Fair, on-time payouts; no surprise deductions.
- Priority assignments for verified and badge-complete drivers.
- Night pickup allowance for late flights and festival windows.
- Referral bonus when you bring another verified driver.
- Access to tempo traveller, Innova, and premium sedan jobs as you level up.

## Routes & Specializations
- **Airport & Station:** VNS arrivals, late-night trains, luggage-first handling.
- **Tent City & Jetty Drops:** Ravidas Ghat coordination and boat timing alignment.
- **Outstation:** Prayagraj, Ayodhya, Bodh Gaya, Lucknow, and Vindhyachal loops.
- **City Tours:** Ganga Aarti timing, sunrise boats, Sarnath afternoons, silk market parking.

## How Drivers Update Their Profile
- Send **photo + name + years driving + languages + top 3 routes** to WhatsApp **94503 01573**.
- Add any certifications (first aid, defensive driving) for extra badges.
- We publish updates weekly so your profile stays current.

## Guest Promise (What Travelers See)
- Verified driver name and photo before pickup.
- Vehicle plate, make, and cleanliness checklist.
- Live tracking link and on-call dispatcher.
- Option to request drivers with specific badges (women-first, fog expert, tempo traveller).
