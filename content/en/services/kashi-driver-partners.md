# Meet Your Verified Kashi Taxi Drivers

This page helps travelers understand who drives with Kashitaxi.in and what standards we follow before assigning a ride.

## Why This Page Is Useful for Travelers
- Know that your driver is verified and trained.
- Understand route specialization before booking.
- Request drivers by language, comfort needs, and trip type.
- Travel with better confidence for airport pickups, ghat visits, and outstation trips.

## What We Verify Before Driver Assignment
- Police and identity verification with periodic revalidation.
- Route training for airport, ghat drop points, station transfers, and outstation corridors.
- Safety behavior checks: calm driving, luggage handling, and senior-friendly support.
- Vehicle hygiene and maintenance checks.
- Operational reliability for late-hour travel and seasonal conditions.

## Driver Profiles You Can Request
- Airport and railway transfer specialists.
- Ghat-access drivers familiar with Godowlia, Maidagin, Assi, and lane drop rules.
- Outstation drivers for Prayagraj, Ayodhya, Bodh Gaya, and Vindhyachal routes.
- Family-friendly drivers experienced with elders and luggage-heavy travel.
- Women-safety trained drivers for solo female travelers (subject to availability).

## What Travelers Receive Before Pickup
- Driver name and phone number.
- Vehicle number and model.
- Pickup point confirmation and ETA.
- Guidance for last-mile walking zones where direct car access is restricted.
- Live coordination support on call or WhatsApp.

## How to Share Your Ride Preferences
Send these details on WhatsApp **+91 94503 01573**:
- Travel date and pickup time
- Route and key stops
- Number of passengers and luggage count
- Any language or comfort preference
- Elder-support or special assistance needs
