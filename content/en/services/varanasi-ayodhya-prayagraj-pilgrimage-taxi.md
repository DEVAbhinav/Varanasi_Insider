## Kashi Prayag Ayodhya Yatra: The Sacred Triangle Circuit Guide

### 1. Kashi (Varanasi) – The Eternal City

**Significance**: Moksha (liberation) kshetra; Lord Shiva's abode; Ganga bathing for ancestral peace.

**Key Darshan Points We Facilitate**:
- Kashi Vishwanath Temple (VIP darshan coordination available)
- Manikarnika Ghat (ancestral ritual observation)
- Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple
- Durga Temple (Durga Kund)

**Optimal Timing**: Pre-dawn (04:30–06:30) for temple access before crowd surge.

### 2. Prayagraj (Allahabad) – Triveni Sangam & Sacred Confluence

**Distance from Varanasi**: 125 km | **Travel Time**: 2.5–3 hrs

**Significance**: Confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, Saraswati (invisible); ritual bathing for sin cleansing; Kumbh Mela host city.

**Key Darshan Points**:
- Triveni Sangam (boat ride for ritual bath – ₹800–₹1,500 depending on season)
- Hanuman Temple (Bade Hanuman Ji, reclining posture)
- Akshayavat (sacred banyan tree inside Patalpuri Temple complex)
- Allahabad Fort (permit required; external view standard)

**Timing Guidance**: 
- Best Sangam bath window: 06:00–09:00 (calm water, fewer boats)
- Avoid midday heat (11:00–15:00) for ghat activities

**Varanasi to Prayagraj Taxi Options**:
- **Same-Day Return**: Varanasi → Prayagraj → Varanasi (8–9 hrs total; ₹5,500 Sedan / ₹7,500 Innova)
- **Overnight Stay**: Drop Prayagraj hotel, return pickup next day (₹3,500 one-way + ₹3,800 return)

### 3. Ayodhya – Ram Janmabhoomi & Ram Mandir Darshan

**Distance from Varanasi**: 210 km | **Travel Time**: 4.5–5 hrs  
**Distance from Prayagraj**: 165 km | **Travel Time**: 3.5–4 hrs

**Significance**: Birthplace of Lord Ram; Ram Mandir (newly inaugurated 2024); Ramayana pilgrimage epicenter.

**Key Darshan Points**:
- **Ram Janmabhoomi Complex** (new Ram Mandir; expect 2–4 hr queue during peak; VIP darshan available via official channels)
- Hanuman Garhi (hilltop temple; 76 steps)
- Kanak Bhawan (Sita-Ram temple; ornate architecture)
- Saryu River Aarti (evening; 18:30 in winter)

**Darshan Timing Strategy**:
- Early arrival (05:00–06:30): Shortest queue at Ram Janmabhoomi
- Evening: Hanuman Garhi + Saryu Aarti combined visit

**Varanasi to Ayodhya Taxi Circuit Options**:
- **Varanasi → Ayodhya → Varanasi (Same Day)**: 10–12 hrs; ₹7,800 Sedan / ₹10,500 Innova
- **Via Prayagraj Circuit (2 Days)**: Varanasi → Prayagraj (overnight) → Ayodhya → Varanasi (₹14,500–₹18,000 full package)

## Practical Pilgrimage Tips

### Temple Dress Codes
- **Kashi Vishwanath**: No shorts; women encouraged to wear dupatta
- **Ram Janmabhoomi**: Modest attire; electronics restricted (phones allowed but cameras often prohibited)
- **Sangam**: Change of clothes recommended for post-bath; vehicle provides privacy curtain

### Ritual Timing Windows
- **Pind Daan (Gaya)**: 06:00–10:00 optimal; requires 2–3 hrs; pandas (priests) guide for fee (₹1,100–₹5,100)
- **Kashi Evening Aarti**: 18:45 (winter) / 19:15 (summer)
- **Ram Janmabhoomi**: 04:30 temple opens; 05:00–07:00 least crowded

### Health & Comfort
- Nov–Feb: Pleasant (12–22°C); light woolens sufficient
- Carry glucose/electrolytes for long darshan queues
- Footwear left outside temples; slip-ons recommended
