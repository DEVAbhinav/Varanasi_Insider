## Sugam vs Regular Darshan

- **Regular (general) darshan** → free, but can mean a long queue on busy days.
- **Sugam Darshan** → paid, faster-access separate line; typically saves significant queue time when running.
- **Neither guarantees a fixed time** → access still depends on crowd, security and temple rules.

On **Mahashivratri, Shravan Mondays and other peak days**, Sugam is often changed or suspended and heavy crowd control applies. See our [Mahashivratri crowd survival guide](/en/kashi-vishwanath-shivaratri-crowd-survival-guide) for those dates.
