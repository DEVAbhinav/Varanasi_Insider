## Day-by-Day Itinerary (4N/5D)

**Day 1 — Arrive Varanasi:** Pickup, hotel, evening Ganga Aarti from a boat. Overnight Varanasi.

**Day 2 — Varanasi:** Sunrise boat ride, [Kashi Vishwanath darshan](/en/services/kashi-vishwanath-vip-darshan-booking) (Sugam option), Sarnath. Overnight Varanasi.

**Day 3 — Bodh Gaya:** Drive to Bodh Gaya; Mahabodhi Temple, Bodhi Tree, Great Buddha Statue. Overnight Bodh Gaya.

**Day 4 — Ayodhya:** Drive to Ayodhya via route; Ram Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Saryu Aarti. Overnight Ayodhya.

**Day 5 — Prayagraj → return:** Triveni Sangam snan & boat, Anand Bhavan. Return to Varanasi for departure.
