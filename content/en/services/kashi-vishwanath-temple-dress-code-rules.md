## What to Wear at Kashi Vishwanath Temple

- **Men:** kurta-pyjama, dhoti, or trousers with a shirt.
- **Women:** saree, salwar-kameez, or a modest suit.
- **Avoid:** shorts, very short dresses, revealing or beachwear.
- **Special pujas** (e.g. sanctum abhishek) may **require traditional dress** — confirm when booking.

## What to Carry into Kashi Vishwanath (and What's Not Allowed)

- **Carry minimal** — the less you bring, the faster the cloakroom.
- **Leave in cab / cloakroom:** phone, camera, electronics, leather items, large bags.
- **Usually allowed:** flowers/prasad offerings per temple norms; keep money & documents secure.
