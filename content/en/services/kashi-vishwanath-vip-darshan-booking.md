## Temple Rules & Practical Tips (Please Read)

- **Mobiles, cameras, leather, and bags** are usually not allowed inside the sanctum — use the cloakroom. We guide you on this.
- **Dress modestly.** Traditional wear is appreciated; many devotees prefer it for aarti.
- **Peak days** (Mahashivratri, Shravan Mondays, Dev Deepawali week) have special crowd rules and possible suspension of special darshan — we advise honestly.
- **Official schedules and rates change.** For the definitive aarti timings and ticket prices, verify on the temple's official portal **shrikashivishwanath.org** before travel.
