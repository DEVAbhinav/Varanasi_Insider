Yes. Festival blackout dates only pause the Flash ₹690 sedan but our standard fare grid stays unchanged. Quotes issued on WhatsApp include a “Surge Shield” line so drivers cannot add extras on arrival.

**Q2. What if my flight is delayed beyond the 30-minute buffer?**  
We automatically track your flight. Delays under 90 minutes are free. After 90 minutes we either (a) keep the same driver at ₹100/15-minute standby or (b) re-dispatch the next available car with zero rebooking fee.

**Q3. Can I split payment or pay digitally?**  
UPI, card-on-file, or cash on drop. Invoices list GST and surge shield so expense teams accept them.

**Q4. How do you prevent touts from hijacking my ride?**  
Driver sends a selfie + plate photo on WhatsApp before you exit. We also share an OTP that must match before the journey begins, eliminating handoff scams.

Sedans add only ₹150 after 9 PM (₹1,699 cap for Innova Crysta). Tempo Travellers already include driver allowance, so there is no hidden “night handling” markup—exactly what the fare grid displays.

**Q6. Can I book straight from the airport to Assi Ghat or Dashashwamedh?**
