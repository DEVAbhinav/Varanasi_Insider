## Navratri Stay Strategy (High-Demand Period)

**Book Early:** 15–30 days prior.
**Split Strategy:** Day 1 stay near temple → Day 2 move to Mirzapur (rest + recovery).
**Late-Night Rooms:** Confirm 24h access if planning post-midnight darshan.
**Power & Water:** Verify generator + hot water at dharamshala (some ration supply).
**Avoid Touts:** Deal directly with reception/trust contacts; ask for receipt.

> Pro Tip: Keep a small sling bag only—large luggage storage is limited in lane-side dharamshalas.

## FAQs: Staying in Vindhyachal

> **What is the closest stay to the temple?**  
> Several small guest houses and B R Palace (≈50 m) are among the closest; Surjidevi Trust is also very near.

> **Is online booking reliable for dharamshalas?**  
> Mostly no—rely on direct phone or local assistance; verify authenticity of any payment request.

> **Can I get AC during peak summer?**  
> Limited AC inventory near temple—book early or use Mirzapur hotels.

> **Is parking available near dharamshalas?**  
> Very limited—use railway area hotels or Mirzapur for secure vehicle parking.

> **Do stays provide prasad or meal packages?**  
> Some arrange basic thali; fasting meals usually separate and need prior request.
