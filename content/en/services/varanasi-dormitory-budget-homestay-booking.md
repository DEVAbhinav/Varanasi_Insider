## Which Area Should You Stay In? (60-Second Guide)

- **Near Kashi Vishwanath / old city** → shortest walk to darshan & Ganga Aarti, but narrow lanes, limited vehicle access. Best for able-bodied pilgrims who want to be in the heart of Kashi.
- **Assi Ghat side** → calmer, good cafes, easy sunrise-boat access, decent vehicle reach. Great all-round choice for families.
- **Cantt / Sigra / Lanka** → smoothest cab access, more space and comfort, a short drive to the ghats. Best for elders, groups, and comfort-first travellers.
