## Day-by-Day Itinerary (3N/4D)

**Day 1 — Arrive Varanasi (Kashi):** Airport/station pickup, hotel check-in. Evening [Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat](/en/dashashwamedh-ghat-ganga-aarti-timing) from a boat. Overnight Varanasi.

**Day 2 — Varanasi darshan → drive to Ayodhya:** Sunrise [boat ride](/en/morning-boat-ride-varanasi-price), [Kashi Vishwanath darshan](/en/services/kashi-vishwanath-vip-darshan-booking) (Sugam option), Sarnath. Afternoon drive to Ayodhya. Overnight Ayodhya.

**Day 4 — Prayagraj Sangam → return:** Triveni Sangam boat & snan, Bade Hanuman, Anand Bhavan. Drive back to Varanasi for departure (airport/station drop).
