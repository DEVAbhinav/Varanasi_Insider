## Where These Stays Are (Old-City Lanes)

The temple sits in a dense web of pedestrian lanes near **Godowlia** and **Dashashwamedh Ghat**. Stays here are the closest to darshan but come with narrow lanes, stairs, and no vehicle access to the door — you walk the last 200–500 metres.

- **Right in the lanes** → shortest walk to darshan & aarti; simplest rooms; best for able-bodied pilgrims.
- **Godowlia crossing** → still very close, slightly easier to reach, more room choice.
- **Assi Ghat / Cantt side** → a short cab ride out; better for families, elders and anyone wanting a car to the door.
