# Varanasi Instrument Market Guide 2026

**Why this matters:** Tourist trap drums & export-grade shortcuts flood shelves. This insider guide filters authentic craft from decorative stock so you invest in playable, stable instruments.

## 1. Quick Price Bands (2026 Reality)
| Instrument | Student Usable | Mid/Performing | Pro / Custom | Export-Only Tourist Junk (Avoid) |
|------------|----------------|---------------|--------------|----------------------------------|
| Tabla Pair | ₹6–9K | ₹12–18K | ₹22–40K+ (concert) | <₹5K hollow / synthetic skins |
| Shehnai | ₹4–7K | ₹9–15K | ₹18–35K (master bore) | Painted toy units ₹1–2K |
| Sitar | ₹12–18K | ₹22–35K | ₹40–70K (well-seasoned) | Thin top gourd + laminated ₹8–10K |
| Harmonium | ₹10–14K | ₹16–26K | ₹30–45K (concert, scale changer) | Fiber box bright lacquer |
| Sarangi (rare) | ₹18–28K | ₹35–55K | ₹60K+ (seasoned aged wood) | Tourist carved front <₹12K |

## 3. Authenticity Checklist (Tabla Example)
| Component | Inspect | Acceptable | Reject |
|----------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Dayan Wood | Grain tight / rings even | Sheesham / hardwood weighty | Light, porous, hollow sound |
| Syahi (black spot) | Concentric layers, matte | Even sustain tap | Flaking, glossy paint feel |
| Bayan Rim | Smooth, no burr | Slight uniform bevel | Sharp edge / gaps |
| Tuning Blocks | Hard grain, tight fit | Even spacing 8–10 | Mixed random woods |
| Strap | Thick leather | Minimal stretch | Vinyl / cracking |

## 4. Humidity & Seasonal Prep
Varanasi = river humidity swings; request maker to:
- Provide spare gatta (wood wedges) sizes + note positions
- Mark reference pitch (A=440 vs preferred classical reference)
- Apply final talc / anti-stick only at pickup (not a day early)

## 5. Negotiation Ethic
| Situation | Suggested Approach |
|-----------|--------------------|
| Fair mid-range quote | Max 10–12% soften |
| Obvious tourist inflation (2×) | Offer documented benchmark table |
| Custom build slot | Pay 30–40% deposit only |
| Add-ons (bag, cover) | Bundle discount 5–8% |

Avoid driving price below artisan dignity; underpaid makers = corner-cut materials.

## 6. Sitar Specific Checks
- Joint between tabli (top) & main gourd: no visible glue bleeds
- Jawari surface even, not aggressively filed shiny
- Fret tie: uniform tension (test slide audible squeak absence)
- Sympathetic string holes smooth (no fray risk)

## 7. Shehnai Evaluation
| Aspect | Good | Poor |
|--------|------|------|
| Bore Finish | Smooth internal | Rough tool chatter |
| Reed (jivala) | Responsive low breath | Over-stiff, squeals |
| Tuning | Stable across 2 octaves | Sharp high register |
| Cork Seals | Uniform compression | Air leaks bubbling test |

Bring small tuner app; test Sa→Pa→Sa glides.

## 8. Harmonium Reality
Myths: “Scale changer always better.” Not true if bellows airflow uneven. Check for:
- Continuous bellows cycle (no suction collapse)
- Reed banks tuned ±5 cents
- Key dip uniform (2–3 mm)
- No metallic buzzing at mid C gentle attack

## 9. Ethical Purchasing
Support shops that:
- Let apprentices observe sale process (knowledge transmission)  
- Disclose sourcing (skins ethically procured)  
- Offer repair orientation 10–15 mins  

## 10. Packing & Transport
| Method | Risk | Mitigation |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Checked Flight (sitar) | Crack / bridge shift | Hard case + bubble wrap + “FRAGILE SITAR – TOP LOAD” tag |
| Cabin (tabla dayan) | Security query | Print airline percussion policy |
| Courier Domestic | Moisture warp | Desiccant + double box |

## 11. Rapid Decision Flow (If Time < 1 Hour)
1. Pick 2 vetted shops (hotel / local musician reference)  
2. Narrow to 2 instruments each → comparative test sustain & tonal bloom  
3. Photograph defects → negotiate  
4. Lock purchase + quick humidity briefing  
5. Obtain maker contact + 3 month tune support promise
