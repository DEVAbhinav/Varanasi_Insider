## What is Nag Nathaiya?

Nag Nathaiya, also known locally as a Lakkha Mela, is the traditional reenactment of Lord Krishna subduing the serpent Kaliya. At Tulsi Ghat, the child portraying Krishna enters the river and appears on a multi-hooded serpent effigy while devotees watch from the ghat and authorised boats.

## Viewing and safety

- Tulsi Ghat offers the closest atmosphere but becomes extremely crowded.
- An authorised boat may offer a clearer view, subject to the river-police navigation plan.
- Use only licensed operators and insist on a life jacket for every passenger.
- Avoid overcrowded boats and improvised mid-river transfers.
- Choose a regroup point before entering the ghat crowd.
- Follow police barricades; vehicle and boat access may change during the event.

## Getting there

Assi is the most practical outer approach to Tulsi Ghat. Expect to walk the final stretch if traffic controls are active. Confirm the exact drop and pickup points only after the event advisory is published.
