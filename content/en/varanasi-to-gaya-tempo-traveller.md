## Gaya & Bodh Gaya 2-Day Tempo Traveller Flow (Recommended)

### Day 1 – Varanasi ➝ Gaya (Pind Daan)

- **4:30 AM:** Depart Varanasi; packed breakfast served inside vehicle.
- **8:30 AM:** Check into pre-booked dharamshala/hotel (AC & non-AC options).
- **9:00 AM:** Meet vetted Gayawal priest at Vishnupad Temple. Ritual kit provided (pind, darbha grass, til, cloth).
- **11:30 AM:** Perform Pitru Tarpan on Falgu ghat; driver watches footwear/bags.
- **12:30 PM:** Simple satvik lunch arranged near temple (leaf platter, limited spices).
- **2:30 PM:** Visit Mangla Gauri Shakti Peeth (steep climb assistance included).
- **5:00 PM:** Rest/evening bhajan at lodging; optional Darbhanga Ghat sunset visit.

### Day 2 – Bodh Gaya & Return

- **6:00 AM:** Short meditation at Mahabodhi Temple; watch monks’ morning rituals.
- **7:30 AM:** Great Buddha statue, international monasteries (Thai, Japanese, Tibetan).
- **10:00 AM:** Breakfast at Bodh Gaya (clean cafes like Bodhi Tree).
- **11:30 AM:** Depart for Varanasi via Dobhi – Sasaram; lunch at Sher Shah Suri plaza.
- **5:30 PM:** Reach Varanasi before evening Aarti (if requested driver can drop directly at ghat).

> **Tip:** Carry photocopies of ancestor names/gotra for priest records. We supply waterproof folders to keep them safe near the river.
