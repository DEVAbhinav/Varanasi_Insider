# Sarnath Temple Timings: Opening Hours, Friday Closure & Visit Plan

> **Quick answer:** Plan the main Sarnath visitor area for **8:00 AM–6:00 PM**. The **Archaeological Museum is 10:00 AM–5:00 PM and closed Fridays**. These are published planning hours, not a guarantee for every temple or special date.

This page answers only the timing questions. For the history of Dhamek Stupa, the Ashoka Pillar, museum highlights and Buddhist temples, use the [Sarnath complete guide](/en/sarnath-complete-guide).

## Sarnath Opening Hours at a Glance

| Place | Planning hours | Weekly closure | Important note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main Sarnath visitor area | 8:00 AM–6:00 PM | Check locally for special closures | Some monuments are described as sunrise-to-sunset; 8 AM is the conservative arrival time |
| Archaeological Museum | 10:00 AM–5:00 PM | Friday | Do not build a Friday itinerary around the museum |
| Mulagandha Kuti Vihar | Varies by prayer schedule | No single site-wide schedule | Check at the temple on arrival |
| International Buddhist temples | Varies by institution | Varies | Lunch breaks and prayer access differ |

Official tourism information currently lists the Sarnath Museum at 10 AM–5 PM with Friday closure. Older travel pages—including an earlier version of this page—often repeat 9 AM or old ticket information. Those claims have been removed.

## Best Time of Day to Visit

For most visitors, **8:00–11:30 AM** is the most practical window:

1. Walk the outdoor archaeological area after 8 AM while it is cooler.
2. Enter the museum after it opens at 10 AM.
3. Finish at Mulagandha Kuti Vihar or another nearby temple.

In hot months, avoid making a long outdoor walk the centre of a midday visit. In winter, fog can soften early-morning visibility, although the cooler temperature is comfortable for walking.

## Friday Plan Without the Museum

The museum closure does not mean all of Sarnath is closed. On Friday, focus on:

- Dhamek Stupa and the archaeological remains
- the original Ashoka Pillar site
- Mulagandha Kuti Vihar
- Chaukhandi Stupa
- one or two international Buddhist temples, subject to their own opening hours

Do not advertise or promise access to every temple: each institution controls its own prayer and visitor schedule.

## How Long Should You Allow?

| Time available | Sensible plan |
|---|---|
| 2 hours | Archaeological area plus one temple; add the museum only if queues and opening hours allow |
| 3–4 hours | Archaeological area, museum and Mulagandha Kuti Vihar |
| Half day | Add Chaukhandi Stupa and selected international temples |

Trying to include every monastery usually creates more driving and waiting than insight. A focused half day is better than a checklist.

## Practical Half-Day Itinerary

- **8:00 AM:** arrive and explore the archaeological area
- **9:30 AM:** walk toward the museum and take a short break
- **10:00 AM:** visit the Archaeological Museum, except Friday
- **11:00 AM:** visit Mulagandha Kuti Vihar
- **12:00 PM:** depart for Varanasi or continue to selected temples

Allow 30–45 minutes each way from central Varanasi in normal traffic, with extra buffer during festivals or road diversions.

## Tickets and Same-Day Verification

Ticket prices and booking systems change. Use the official counter or current official ticketing information rather than an old ₹5, ₹100 or “combo ticket” figure copied from another guide. Also verify special-date closures before making a long journey.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the current published Sarnath visitor hours?

The general Sarnath visitor area is listed as open from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Individual monuments and temples may follow their own daylight or prayer schedules.

### What time does Sarnath Archaeological Museum open?

The museum is listed as open from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM and closed every Friday. Arrive well before closing.

### Is Sarnath Museum open on Friday?

No. Plan the archaeological area and temples instead, checking their separate schedules.

### How much time is needed for Sarnath?

Three to four hours is comfortable for the principal archaeological area, museum and Mulagandha Kuti Vihar. Allow a half day if adding more temples.
