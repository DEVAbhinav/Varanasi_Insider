## Important tips before you lock the fare

1. **One-way vs round-trip:** One-way if you overnight in Bodhgaya. Same-day return if the car waits at the temple.
2. **Check inclusions:** Confirm tolls, Bihar entry/green tax and driver allowance are inside the quote — not “plus tax at border.”
3. **Plan stops:** Route passes Jaunpur / Sasaram belts; build one meal halt each way.
4. **Peak days:** Buddha Purnima and winter pilgrimage weeks — book the cab early.

## Places to visit in Bodh Gaya (so the cab day works)

### 1. Mahabodhi Temple Complex (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
- **Main temple:** 55 m pyramidal spire built in the 5th–6th century
- **The Bodhi Tree:** direct descendant of the original tree under which Buddha attained enlightenment
- **Vajrasana (Diamond Throne):** the exact spot of enlightenment
- **Timings:** about 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM, open all days · **Entry:** free
- **Duration:** allow 2–3 hours minimum

### 2. Great Buddha Statue
80 ft tall, inaugurated by the Dalai Lama in 1989. A meditation garden surrounds it — best visited at sunrise or sunset for light and quiet.

### 3. International monasteries
Bodhgaya hosts monasteries representing Buddhist traditions worldwide, each with distinct architecture:
- **Thai Monastery** — golden architecture
- **Japanese Temple** — Zen garden and meditation hall
- **Tibetan Monastery** — colourful prayer wheels and thangkas
- **Bhutanese, Chinese & Vietnamese Monasteries** — each with its own style

### 4. Sujata Kuti & Stupa
Village where Sujata offered kheer to Buddha before enlightenment. About 7 km from the main temple in a peaceful countryside setting — add only if energy allows.

### 5. Dungeshwari Cave (Mahakala Caves)
Where Buddha meditated for 6 years before enlightenment. About 12 km from Bodhgaya on a scenic hilltop — better with an overnight stay, not a rushed same-day add-on.

## Buddhist etiquette & what to pack

**Temple etiquette:** cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes in temple zones, walk clockwise around stupas, keep quiet in meditation areas, don’t point feet at Buddha images.

**Pack:** easy-off walking shoes, water, sunscreen/hat, light layer for dawn starts.

**Meditation windows:** 5–7 AM or 5–7 PM at Mahabodhi if you overnight.
