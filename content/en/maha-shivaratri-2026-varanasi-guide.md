```

MAIN AREAS:
🕉️ Sanctum Sanctorum: Contains the sacred Jyotirlinga
🏛️ Vishwanath Corridor: New infrastructure (2021)
👥 Darshan Halls: Multiple levels for crowd management
🛒 Shopping Complex: Religious items and offerings
🍽️ Food Courts: Prasad and vegetarian meals

CAPACITY & CROWDS:

## 🎭 Shiv Baraat: The Divine Wedding Procession

### Understanding the Spectacle
The **Shiv Baraat** is a unique carnival-like procession representing Lord Shiva's wedding party, featuring elaborate costumes, music, and theatrical performances.

```
🎪 SHIV BARAAT PHENOMENON:

CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
💒 Represents: Shiva's marriage to Parvati
🎭 Participants: Dressed as gods, ghosts, and celestial beings
🎵 Atmosphere: Loud music, dancing, celebration
🔥 Performances: Fire-eaters, acrobats, traditional arts
👻 Unique Elements: Aghori sadhus, ghost costumes

PROCESSION DETAILS:
📍 Multiple Routes: Several processions through city
⏰ Peak Time: 6:00-9:00 PM
👥 Participants: 10,000+ in various processions
🎶 Music: Traditional drums, modern sound systems
📸 Photography: Spectacular but challenging in crowds
```

```

Route 1: Traditional Central Route

🏛️ Destination: Kashi Vishwanath Temple
⏰ Timing: 6:00-8:00 PM
👥 Crowd Level: EXTREMELY HIGH

Route 2: Asi Ghat to Dashashwamedh

🛣️ Path: Along ghats and parallel streets

👥 Crowd Level: HIGH

Route 3: Cantonment Area Route
🚩 Start: Various starting points
🛣️ Path: Through newer city areas

⏰ Timing: 7:00-9:00 PM
👥 Crowd Level: MEDIUM-HIGH

Multiple Neighborhood Routes:
