## Monuments to Visit on Varanasi to Agra Taxi Tour

### 1. Taj Mahal (UNESCO) on Varanasi to Agra Tour

**Timings**: 6:00 AM - 6:30 PM (closed Fridays)  
**Entry Fee**:
- Indians: ₹1,100 (includes shoe covers, water bottle)
- SAARC/BIMSTEC: ₹740
- Foreigners: ₹1,300
- Children under 15: Free

**Best Time to Visit**: Sunrise (6:30-7:30 AM) - golden light, fewer crowds

**Important Facts**:
- Built: 1631-1653 (22 years)
- Material: White Makrana marble
- Inlay work: 28 types of precious stones
- Workers: 20,000+ artisans
- Architect: Ustad Ahmad Lahori
- Height: 73 meters (main dome)
- Symmetry: Perfect bilateral symmetry

**Duration**: 2-3 hours minimum

### 2. Agra Fort (UNESCO) for Varanasi to Agra Travelers

**Timings**: 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM (daily)  
**Entry Fee**: ₹650 (Indians), ₹850 (foreigners)

**Highlights**:
- Red sandstone fort (1565-1573)
- Jahangir Mahal, Khas Mahal
- Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace)
- Diwan-i-Am, Diwan-i-Khas
- View of Taj Mahal from fort
- Shah Jahan imprisoned here (last years)

**Duration**: 2 hours

### 3. Fatehpur Sikri (UNESCO) on Agra Taxi Circuit

**Location**: 40 km from Agra (1-hour drive)  
**Timings**: Sunrise to sunset  
**Entry Fee**: ₹610 (Indians), ₹760 (foreigners)

**Highlights**:
- Abandoned Mughal capital (built 1571)
- Buland Darwaza (largest gateway in world)
- Jama Masjid, Salim Chishti's tomb
- Panch Mahal (5-story palace)
- Perfect Mughal architecture

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

### 4. Itmad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj) Stop

**Timings**: 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM  
**Entry Fee**: ₹310 (Indians), ₹510 (foreigners)

**Highlights**:
- First marble tomb in India
- Intricate marble inlay work
- Prototype for Taj Mahal design
- Peaceful, fewer crowds

**Duration**: 1 hour

### 5. Mehtab Bagh (Taj Sunset Point)

**Timings**: 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM  
**Entry Fee**: ₹300 (Indians)

**Highlights**:
- Best sunset view of Taj Mahal
- Across Yamuna River
- Perfect for photography
- Peaceful garden

**Duration**: 1 hour
