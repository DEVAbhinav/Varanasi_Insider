### Best Time for an Agra Taxi Tour

- **For Taj Mahal**: October to March, especially sunrise.
- **Best months**: November to February.
- **Avoid if possible**: April to June, when Agra can be extremely hot (35-48°C).
- **Monsoon**: July to September is humid and the marble areas can get slippery.

**Important:** Taj Mahal is **closed on Fridays**.

## Places to Visit in Agra on a Varanasi to Agra Taxi Tour

### 1. Taj Mahal (UNESCO)

**Timings**: 6:00 AM - 6:30 PM (closed Fridays)
**Entry Fee**:

- Indians: ₹1,100 (includes shoe covers, water bottle)
- SAARC/BIMSTEC: ₹740
- Foreigners: ₹1,300
- Children under 15: Free

**Best time**: Sunrise (6:30-7:30 AM). It is cooler, the light is better, and the crowd is smaller.

**Useful facts:**

- Built: 1631-1653 (22 years)
- Material: White Makrana marble
- Inlay work: 28 types of precious stones
- Workers: 20,000+ artisans
- Architect: Ustad Ahmad Lahori
- Height: 73 meters (main dome)
- Symmetry: Perfect bilateral symmetry

**Duration**: 2-3 hours minimum

### 2. Agra Fort (UNESCO)

**Timings**: 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM (daily)
**Entry Fee**: ₹650 (Indians), ₹850 (foreigners)

**Highlights:**

- Red sandstone fort (1565-1573)
- Jahangir Mahal, Khas Mahal
- Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace)
- Diwan-i-Am, Diwan-i-Khas
- View of Taj Mahal from fort
- Shah Jahan imprisoned here in his last years

**Duration**: 2 hours

### 3. Fatehpur Sikri (UNESCO)

**Location**: 40 km from Agra (1-hour drive)
**Timings**: Sunrise to sunset
**Entry Fee**: ₹610 (Indians), ₹760 (foreigners)

**Highlights:**

- Abandoned Mughal capital (built 1571)
- Buland Darwaza
- Jama Masjid, Salim Chishti's tomb
- Panch Mahal (5-story palace)
- Mughal architecture at its best

**Duration**: 2-3 hours

### 4. Itmad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj)

**Timings**: 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM
**Entry Fee**: ₹310 (Indians), ₹510 (foreigners)

**Highlights:**

- First marble tomb in India
- Intricate marble inlay work
- Prototype for Taj Mahal design
- Peaceful and usually less crowded

**Duration**: 1 hour

### 5. Mehtab Bagh

**Timings**: 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM
**Entry Fee**: ₹300 (Indians)

Across the Yamuna River, this is the easiest place for a sunset view of the Taj Mahal.

**Duration**: 1 hour

## Varanasi to Agra Taxi Travel Tips

- **Book 2-3 days if you can:** It gives enough time for Taj sunrise, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri, and local markets.
- **Avoid Friday:** Taj Mahal is closed.
- **Photography:** No tripods inside Taj Mahal; handheld camera is allowed.
- **Dress and shoes:** Wear modest clothes and shoes that are easy to remove.
- **Tickets:** Book online when possible to reduce queue time.
- **Food:** Try Mughlai food and Agra petha.
- **Carry:** Camera, sunscreen, sunglasses, winter jacket, water bottle, power bank, cash, and valid ID proof.
- **Security:** Food/drinks, smoking, touching marble with oily hands, and large bags are restricted at monuments.
