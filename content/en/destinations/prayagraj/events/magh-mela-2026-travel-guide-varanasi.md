# Prayagraj Magh Mela 2026: dates and travel recap

## Magh Mela is not Maha Kumbh

Prayagraj Maha Kumbh ran from **13 January to 26 February 2025**. There was no Maha Kumbh in 2026; Magh Mela was the annual event. See the [Government of India Maha Kumbh 2025 schedule](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2092233&lang=2&reg=48).

## Planning a future mela trip

Do not reuse a 2026 parking pass, coloured sticker, shuttle route or drop point for another edition. Before travelling:

- Use that year's official bathing calendar.
- Check Prayagraj district and traffic-police notices.
- Confirm the last permitted vehicle point and walking distance.
- Keep medicines, warm clothing and a clear regroup point for older travellers.

Varanasi to Prayagraj is normally about 120–125 km by road, but mela-day journey time depends on the pickup point and official diversions.
