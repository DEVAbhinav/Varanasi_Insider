# Varanasi to Prayagraj Magh Mela 2026 taxi archive

> **Archive notice, updated 3 September 2026:** Magh Mela 2026 ran from **3 January to 15 February** and has ended. The former active booking offers, urgent-dispatch premium, fixed snan-day departure times and claimed discounts have been removed.

Verified calendar: [Akashvani / News on AIR — Magh Mela 2026](https://newsonair.gov.in/magh-mela-begins-at-prayagraj-sangam-with-paush-purnima-holy-bath/)

## What remains useful

Varanasi and Prayagraj are normally connected by NH-19. A regular transfer should be quoted using the current pickup point, vehicle, toll/parking terms and exact destination.

For a future mela, do not assume an old route, special pass, parking zone or shuttle still applies. First obtain:

- The new official bathing calendar
- The current Prayagraj traffic and parking notice
- The last permitted vehicle point
- Written fare, cancellation and waiting-time terms

## 2026 dates for historical reference

The main dates were 3 January (Paush Purnima), 14 January (Makar Sankranti), 18 January (Mauni Amavasya), 23 January (Basant Panchami), 1 February (Maghi Purnima) and 15 February (Maha Shivaratri/closing day).
