# Prayagraj mela travel for seniors and families

> **Correction notice, updated 3 September 2026:** Magh Mela 2026 has ended. The earlier page used unsupported claims about train occupancy, platform queues, visitor totals, fare surges, HEPA filters, medical equipment and doorstep access to camp gates. Those claims have been removed.

For the historical dates, see the [corrected Magh Mela 2026 archive](/en/city/prayagraj/events/magh-mela-2026-travel-guide-varanasi). For a future event, use the new official calendar and district traffic plan.

## Safety checklist

- Ask the traveller's clinician about cold exposure, long walks and bathing if relevant.
- Carry regular medicines, prescriptions, identification and an emergency contact.
- Confirm whether the vehicle can accommodate a mobility aid; never assume a wheelchair or oxygen supply is included.
- Ask the district or organiser about accessible routes and the last permitted drop point.
- Expect walking and allow time for rest, toilets and changing clothes.
- Choose a visible regroup point and keep one responsible adult with each vulnerable traveller.
- Get the vehicle registration, driver's phone number, total fare and cancellation terms in writing.
- Use only organiser- or police-designated shuttles and routes.

## Taxi versus train

Neither mode is automatically safer for every traveller. Compare the actual train reservation, station assistance, road restrictions, walking distance, medical needs and group size. A taxi cannot bypass police barricades or guarantee a drop at the Sangam.

## Before booking a future mela transfer

Confirm the event year, official bathing date, vehicle type, seat belts, luggage space, accessible drop point, waiting terms and return pickup. Avoid any service promising unofficial passes, guaranteed inner-zone access or unsupported medical facilities.
