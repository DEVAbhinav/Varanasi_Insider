## The Train Rush Reality for Varanasi to Prayagraj Magh Mela 2026 Trips

### What Official Sources Don't Highlight About Magh Mela Trains

Indian Railways operates special "Magh Mela Express" trains with 20–30 additional runs per day. Capacity looks huge on paper, but 100+ million pilgrims across 45 days overwhelms scheduling.

### Typical Varanasi to Prayagraj Train Journey Experience (Not Marketing Hype)

1. **Booking chaos:** Reservation portals crash; manual ticketing queues stretch 3–4 hours at Varanasi Junction.
2. **Overcrowding:** Sleeper compartments built for 72 people cram 150+; aisles and restrooms stay blocked.
3. **Platform gridlock at Allahabad:** 50,000+ pilgrims descend simultaneously, making exits unsafe for seniors or kids.
4. **Taxi shortage:** Auto-rickshaw fares surge 300–400% from Allahabad station to Sangam; waits exceed one hour.
5. **Hygiene plunge:** Shared toilets host 100+ users daily, raising infection risk during peak winter season.
6. **Luggage vulnerability:** Offerings and bags stay unattended in racks – petty theft spikes every Magh Mela.
7. **Delayed return:** Return trains often leave past midnight, forcing exhausted pilgrims to navigate dark platforms.

**Net result:** A supposed 3-hour journey balloons into 12–14 hours of stress.
