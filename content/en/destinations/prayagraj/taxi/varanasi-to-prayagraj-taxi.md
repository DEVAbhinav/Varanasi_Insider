The Varanasi to Prayagraj route remains open year-round. However, during monsoon (July-September), occasional waterlogging in low-lying areas may add 15-20 minutes to travel time. ## Places to Visit in Prayagraj

If you have half a day or more in the city, build a simple circuit around these highlights:

- **Triveni Sangam**: Sacred confluence of the Ganga, Yamuna, and the mythical Saraswati. Take a boat ride and, if time allows, attend a short Ganga aarti.
- **Allahabad Fort (view from outside)**: Mughal-era fort built by Emperor Akbar at the riverfront.
- **Akshay Vat**: The legendary "immortal" banyan tree inside the fort complex, accessible on specific routes.
- **Hanuman Mandir (Bade Hanuman Ji)**: Famous riverside temple with a reclining Hanuman idol.
- **Khusro Bagh**: Historical garden complex with Mughal tombs and shaded walkways.
- **Anand Bhavan**: Ancestral home of the Nehru family, now a museum.

Plan temple visits early in the morning or post-lunch to avoid heavy queues at peak aarti times.
