## Varanasi to Nepal Border Documents & Requirements

### For Indian Citizens

**Required documents (any one):**

- Passport (preferred, valid).
- Voter ID card.
- Driving license with photo.
- Government-issued photo ID.

**Visa:** Not required under the Indo-Nepal Treaty of Peace and Friendship, 1950.

**Vehicle entry:**

- Original vehicle registration (RC).
- Driving license.
- Pollution certificate.
- Insurance valid in Nepal.
- No permit needed for cars; free temporary import.

### Visa Guide for Foreign Nationals

**Required documents:**

- Valid passport with 6+ months validity.
- Passport photo (2 copies).
- Nepal visa.

**Nepal visa:**

- Available on arrival at all borders.
- Fees:
  - 15 days: $30 USD.
  - 30 days: $50 USD.
  - 90 days: $125 USD.
- Payment: USD cash preferred; Indian rupees accepted at exchange rate.
- Processing time: 30 minutes - 2 hours.

**Visa exemption:** Citizens of India and Maldives do not need a visa.

- Keep passport/ID and vehicle papers ready.
- Arrive early; 8 AM - 2 PM is usually fastest.
- Avoid weekends if your schedule allows.
- Change some INR to NPR at the border.

- Nepal is 15 minutes ahead of India.

## Varanasi to Nepal Road Trip Travel Tips

### Before You Leave Varanasi

- Check weather; Nepal can be cooler than Varanasi.
- Carry layers for cold mornings and nights in the mountains.
- Keep USD cash for visa if you are a foreign national.
- Indian rupees are widely accepted (NPR 100 = INR 62 approx).
- Check Nepal coverage in your travel insurance.
- Inform your bank about international travel.

### What to Pack

- Valid passport/ID with originals and photocopies.
- Passport photos (2-3 copies).
- Light woolens, even in summer for Pokhara/Kathmandu.
- Comfortable walking shoes.
- Sunscreen and sunglasses for high altitude.
- Basic medicines.
- Power adapter (same as India, 220V).
- Reusable water bottle.

### In Nepal After Arrival

- **Language:** Nepali, similar to Hindi; English widely spoken.
- **Currency:** Nepalese Rupee (NPR).
- **Food:** Dal Bhat, Momos, Thukpa and Newari cuisine.
- **Tipping:** Not mandatory but appreciated; 10% at restaurants.
- **Altitude:** Kathmandu 1,400m, usually mild.
- **Safety:** Very safe country with low crime.
- **Shopping:** Bargain in markets except fixed-price stores.
