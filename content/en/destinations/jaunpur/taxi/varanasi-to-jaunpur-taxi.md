## What to See in Jaunpur

1. **Atala Masjid (1408)** - the architectural template for the Sharqi style, with a towering ornate gateway.
2. **Jama Masjid (Bari Masjid)** - the largest Sharqi mosque, raised on a high plinth.
3. **Shahi Bridge (Akbari Bridge, 1564)** - a still-used Mughal-era stone bridge across the Gomti, lined with shops.
4. **Shahi Qila (Fort)** - a 14th-century fort with a Turkish bath and views of the river.
5. **Lal Darwaza Masjid** - a smaller, graceful mosque from the Sharqi golden age.

**Tastes of Jaunpur:** the town is famous for **imarti** (a thicker, richer jalebi) and traditional **itr** (perfume). Budget 20 minutes for a sweet-shop stop.

## Best Route & Timing

**Varanasi → NH731 → Jaunpur** is a quick, low-traffic drive that also serves as the first leg of the Ayodhya road. Leave by **8:00-9:00 AM**, cover the monuments through the late morning, have lunch, and you are back in Varanasi by mid-afternoon. **October to March** offers the most comfortable sightseeing weather.
