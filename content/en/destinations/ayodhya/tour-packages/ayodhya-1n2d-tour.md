## Overview

The 1 night / 2 days Ayodhya package delivers a concentrated Ramayana pilgrimage. Perfect for devotees, families, and weekend travellers, the plan covers Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Sita Ki Rasoi, and the soulful Saryu aarti—without compromising rest or safety.

## Highlights at a Glance

- **Duration:** 2 days / 1 night with centrally located 3-star stay.
- **Best for:** Ram bhakts, senior citizens, families on a weekend spiritual break.
- **Accessibility:** Private AC fleet with vetted drivers, locker assistance, and escort support where needed.
- **Guidance:** Local expert who navigates temple etiquette, queues, and storytelling.

## Day 1 – Arrival, Main Temple Circuit, Saryu Aarti

**Morning / Afternoon Arrival**

- Pickups from Ayodhya Airport, Ayodhya Junction Railway Station, or by road from Lucknow (140 km) / Varanasi (200 km).
- Hotel check-in, welcome drink, briefing, and lunch stop.

**Primary Temple Darshan (1:00 – 5:30 PM)**

1. **Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir**  
   Explore the consecrated grand temple honouring Lord Rama’s birthplace. We assist with e-darshan slot booking during peak dates and manage locker storage for phones.
2. **Hanuman Garhi**  
   Climb 76 steps to the iconic hilltop shrine of Lord Hanuman—guardian of Ayodhya.
3. **Kanak Bhawan**  
   Glimpse the gold-adorned Rama–Sita idols and colourful Ramayana murals.
4. **Sita Ki Rasoi**  
   Symbolic kitchen of Goddess Sita with traditional utensils preserved for devotees.
5. **Dashrath Mahal (Ramkot)**  
   Walk through the historic compound believed to be King Dashrath’s residence.

**Evening on the Saryu (5:30 – 8:00 PM)**

- **Ram Ki Paidi** – witness serene ghats, pilgrims taking holy dips, and the devotional ambience.
- **Saryu Evening Aarti** – a gentler, contemplative river worship with lamps, chants, and disciplined devotees.
- **Ram Ki Paidi Light & Sound Show** – optional multimedia retelling of Ramayana (ticketed; 45–60 minutes).
- Dinner (hotel restaurant or popular local bhojanalaya) and overnight stay.

## Day 2 – Sunrise Rituals, Heritage Loop, Departure

**Optional Sunrise Call (6:00 – 7:30 AM)**

- Return to Ram Ki Paidi for dawn serenity, photography, and early temple darshan.

**Breakfast (8:00 – 9:00 AM)**

- Hotel buffet or local favourites—poori-halwa, kachori-sabzi, kulhad chai.

**Morning Heritage Trail (9:00 AM – 1:00 PM)**

- **Nageshwarnath Temple** – Ancient Shiva shrine built by Kush, open till 10:00 PM.
- **Guptar Ghat** – Sacred spot where Lord Rama is believed to have taken Jal Samadhi.
- **Ramkatha Park** – Landscaped park with Ramayana sculptures and open-air theatre.
- **Tulsi Smarak Bhawan Museum** – Manuscripts, paintings, and exhibits devoted to Goswami Tulsidas.
- **Janaki Mahal / Local Markets** – Shop for Ram Lalla idols, rudraksha beads, religious literature, and sweets.

**Lunch & Checkout (12:30 – 1:30 PM)**

- Enjoy sattvic thali at Ram Rasoi Bhojanalaya or dine at the hotel before checkout.

**Departure Options (Afternoon onwards)**

- Drop at Ayodhya Airport or Railway Station.
- Continue by road to Lucknow (3 hours) or Varanasi (4–5 hours).
- Extend stay for an extra night or add Prayagraj / Lucknow modules.

## Best Time to Visit

- **Peak (Oct–Mar):** Pleasant 8°C–25°C, major festivals like Diwali, Ram Navami, Vivah Panchami. Book hotels 10–15 days ahead.
- **Shoulder (Apr, Sep):** Moderate weather, standard pricing, 5–7 day lead time.
- **Off-season (May–Aug):** Hot or monsoon, lighter crowds, 30–40% discounts with 2–3 day notice.

## Food Guide

- Sattvic thali (no onion/garlic) at Ram Rasoi Bhojanalaya.
- Aloo tikki chaat, kachori-sabzi, and poori-halwa at market stalls (₹40–₹100).
- Lassi, thandai, and kulhad chai for quick hydration.
- Laddu, peda, and other prasad-friendly sweets as take-home gifts.

## Travel Pointers

- Dress modestly (shoulders and knees covered) and carry a scarf/dupatta.
- Wear easy-to-remove footwear; socks help when walking barefoot.
- No mobiles/cameras inside certain temple zones—use locker services.
- Keep ₹5,000–₹8,000 cash in small denominations for donations and markets.
- Drink only bottled water; carry sanitizer, tissues, sunscreen, hat, and basic medicines.
