## Package Essentials

- **Duration:** 2 days / 1 night with centrally located 3-star or better stay.[^duration]
- **Suited for:** Ram devotees, weekend pilgrims, families, cultural travellers reconnecting with the Ramayana epic.[^audience]
- **Core inclusions:** Ram Janmabhoomi darshan assistance, Hanuman Garhi climb, Kanak Bhawan, Saryu Aarti, optional light & sound show.

## Day 1 · Arrival & Sacred Core

**Arrivals** – Pickup from Ayodhya Airport (Maharishi Valmiki International) or Ayodhya Junction; optional transfers from Lucknow (140 km) or Varanasi (200 km).[^^arrival]

**Hotel check-in** – Verified 3-star properties with quick darshan access; welcome drink, briefing, lunch slot.[^stay]

**Temple Trail (13:00 – 17:00)**

1. **Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple** – Structured darshan with locker guidance, security protocol briefing and post-prasad photography stops.[^ramjanm]
2. **Hanuman Garhi** – 76-step ascent to cave temple honouring Lord Hanuman; panoramic Ayodhya views.[^hanuman]
3. **Kanak Bhawan & Sita Ki Rasoi** – Gold-plated Rama–Sita idols, symbolic royal kitchen with traditional utensils.[^kanak]
4. **Dashrath Mahal (Ramkot)** – Historical palace footprint narrating Rama’s childhood legends.[^dashrath]

**Teatime break** – Street chai in kulhads, samosa/pakora tasting near temple lanes.[^teatime]

**Saryu Evening (17:30 – 20:00)**

- **Ram Ki Paidi ghats** – Sunset ambience, devotees taking holy dips, photography pointers.[^saryu]
- **Saryu Aarti** – Serene 30-minute ritual with diyas and Sanskrit chanting (arrive 20 mins early).[^^aarti]
- **Light & Sound Show (optional)** – Ramayana narrated via lights & audio, starts ~19:00 (ticketed).[^^lss]

**Dinner & Overnight** – Sattvic thali or Punjabi spread at preferred restaurant; overnight at hotel.

## Day 2 · Extended Circuit & Departure

**Sunrise option (06:00 – 07:30)** – Morning darshan repeat, Ram Ki Paidi sunrise, or yoga/meditation session.[^sunrise]

**Breakfast (08:00 – 09:00)** – Hotel buffet or local poori-halwa, kachori-sabzi, jalebi from trusted stalls.[^breakfast]

**Heritage & Cultural Sites (09:00 – 12:30)**

- **Nageshwarnath Temple** – Ancient Shiva shrine linked to Kush, son of Rama.[^nagesh]
- **Ramkatha Park** – Ramayana sculptures, landscaped gardens, family-friendly halt.[^ramkatha]
- **Tulsi Smarak Bhavan Museum** – Manuscripts, Ramcharitmanas exhibits, cultural library.[^tulsi]
- **Guptar Ghat** – Believed spot of Rama’s Jal Samadhi on Saryu river.[^guptar]

**Market & Food Crawl (12:30 – 14:00)**

- Souvenir hunt: Ram idols, Rudraksha malas, scriptures, local sweets (peda, laddu).[^market]
- Food favourites: Aloo tikki chaat, kulhad lassi, thandai.[^foodtrail]

**Lunch, Checkout & Departure (14:00 onwards)** – Tailored drop at airport/rail or onward transfer to Lucknow/Varanasi.

## Booking Checklist

1. Reserve 10–15 days ahead for festivals (Ram Navami, Diwali, Deepotsav); 5–7 days in shoulder months.[^booking]
2. Carry original ID proof for security at Ram Janmabhoomi; mobiles not permitted inside (lockers provided).
3. Dress modestly; covered shoulders/knees mandated, socks recommended for multi-temple visits.
4. Share dietary preferences (Jain, sattvic, vegan) for curated dining stops.
5. Notify mobility constraints to plan assistance for Hanuman Garhi steps.

## Best Time to Visit

- **Peak (Oct–Mar):** Pleasant 8°C–25°C, major festivals like Diwali, Ram Navami, Vivah Panchami. Book hotels 10–15 days ahead.
- **Shoulder (Apr, Sep):** Moderate weather, standard pricing, 5–7 day lead time.
- **Off-season (May–Aug):** Hot or monsoon, lighter crowds, 30–40% discounts with 2–3 day notice.

## Food Guide

- Sattvic thali (no onion/garlic) at Ram Rasoi Bhojanalaya.
- Aloo tikki chaat, kachori-sabzi, and poori-halwa at market stalls (₹40–₹100).
- Lassi, thandai, and kulhad chai for quick hydration.
- Laddu, peda, and other prasad-friendly sweets as take-home gifts.

## Travel Pointers

- Dress modestly (shoulders and knees covered) and carry a scarf/dupatta.
- Wear easy-to-remove footwear; socks help when walking barefoot.
- No mobiles/cameras inside certain temple zones—use locker services.
- Keep ₹5,000–₹8,000 cash in small denominations for donations and markets.
- Drink only bottled water; carry sanitizer, tissues, sunscreen, hat, and basic medicines.
