## Why Plan This as a 2-Day Trip

Naimisharanya is a true outstation pilgrimage, not a day-trip. The round trip is roughly **740 km**, so trying to do it in a single day means 15+ hours in the car with little time for darshan. The comfortable plan:

- **Day 1:** Depart Varanasi early, drive via Lucknow (a good lunch and rest point), reach Naimisharanya by evening, attend the Chakra Tirth aarti, and stay overnight.
- **Day 2:** Morning darshan of the main shrines, then return to Varanasi, or continue to **Ayodhya** to extend the pilgrimage.

We price this as a **2-day return (1 night)** because it gives you the route the site deserves.

## What to See in Naimisharanya

1. **Chakra Tirth** - the holy tank at the centre of the tirtha, where Lord Vishnu's chakra is said to have fallen; the water is believed to be continuously fed from below.
2. **Vyas Gaddi** - the seat where Sage Vyas is said to have composed the Puranas.
3. **Lalita Devi Temple** - a revered Shakti Peeth.
4. **Hanuman Garhi** - the reclining Hanuman shrine, unusual and beloved.
5. **Pandav Kila & Dadhichi Kund** - linked to the Mahabharata and the sage Dadhichi's sacrifice.
6. **Misrikh (Dadhichi Kund)** - ~10 km away, often visited in the same circuit.

## Best Time to Travel

**October to March** is ideal. **Ekadashi, Amavasya, and the month of Phalgun** see large gatherings at Chakra Tirth. Summers are hot on this route; if travelling May-June, plan an early Day-1 start to beat the heat.
