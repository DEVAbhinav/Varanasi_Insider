## Places to Visit in Lucknow

### Top Historical & Cultural Sites

**1. Bara Imambara Complex**
- **Built**: 1784 by Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula
- **Main attractions**: 
  - Bhulbhulaiya (labyrinth with 489 identical doorways)
  - Asfi Mosque
  - Baoli (stepwell)
- **Timings**: 6:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- **Entry fee**: ₹25 (Indians), ₹500 (foreigners)
- **Duration**: 2-3 hours
- **Best time**: Early morning or late afternoon

**2. Chota Imambara (Hussainabad Imambara)**
- Beautiful chandeliers and mirrors
- Tombs of Nawabs
- **Timings**: 6:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- **Entry fee**: ₹50
- **Duration**: 1 hour

**3. Rumi Darwaza**
- 60-feet high gateway
- Awadhi architecture
- **Best for**: Photography (external viewing free)

**4. British Residency**
- 1857 rebellion ruins
- Museum with artifacts
- **Timings**: Sunrise to sunset
- **Entry fee**: ₹25
- **Duration**: 1-2 hours

**5. Lucknow Zoo (Nawab Wajid Ali Shah Zoological Garden)**
- Large zoo with diverse animals
- Train ride available
- **Timings**: 9:30 AM - 5:00 PM (closed Fridays)
- **Entry fee**: ₹60 (adults), ₹20 (children)

**6. Ambedkar Memorial Park**
- Modern architectural complex
- Beautiful statues and structures
- **Entry**: Free
- **Best time**: Evening (illuminated)
