## Why Was Allahabad Renamed to Prayagraj?

**Historical Context**:
- **Prayagraj** = Ancient Sanskrit name (mentioned in Vedas, Puranas)
- **Meaning**: "Prayag" (place of sacrifice) + "Raj" (king) = "King of sacred places"
- **Allahabad** = Name given during Mughal emperor Akbar's reign (1583)
- **Renaming**: Part of restoring ancient Hindu names to cities

**Both names refer to the same holy city**, home to:
- Triveni Sangam (Ganga-Yamuna-Saraswati confluence)
- Kumbh Mela (every 12 years, 2026 Mahakumbh upcoming!)
- Allahabad Fort (Akbar Fort)
- Anand Bhawan (Nehru family home)
- Allahabad University (oldest university in UP)
