## Places to Visit in Patna

### Top Historical & Cultural Sites

**1. Patna Museum**
- **Established**: 1917
- **Highlights**: 
  - Didarganj Yakshi (Mauryan masterpiece)
  - Ashoka's Lion Capital
  - Buddhist & Jain sculptures
  - Fossil gallery
- **Timings**: 10:30 AM - 4:30 PM (closed Mondays & govt. holidays)
- **Entry fee**: ₹50 (Indians), ₹500 (foreigners)
- **Duration**: 2-3 hours
- **Must-see**: Mauryan gallery

**2. Golghar (Round House)**
- Built in 1786 by Captain John Garstin
- 29-meter high granary
- 145 steps to panoramic view of Patna
- **Timings**: 9:00 AM - 5:30 PM
- **Entry fee**: ₹15
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes
- **Best time**: Sunset for city views

**3. Mahavir Mandir**
- Most visited Hanuman temple in North India
- 8 million visitors annually
- Unique prasad distribution system
- **Timings**: 4:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- **Best time**: Early morning or evening aarti
- **Duration**: 1-2 hours (including queue)

**4. Takht Sri Patna Sahib**
- Birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh
- One of five Sikh Takhts (seats of authority)
- Beautiful white marble gurdwara
- **Timings**: 3:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- **Entry**: Free (head covering required)
- **Langar**: Free community meal available

**5. Kumhrar Archaeological Park**
- Mauryan palace ruins (3rd century BCE)
- 80-pillar hall excavation
- Ancient stupas and water tanks
- **Timings**: 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- **Entry fee**: ₹25
- **Duration**: 1-2 hours

**6. Gandhi Maidan**
- Historic ground (55 acres)
- Site of major political rallies
- Morning/evening walk popular
- **Best time**: Early morning or evening

**7. Patna Ghat (Gandhi Ghat)**
- Ganges riverfront
- Morning aarti
- Boat rides available (₹50-100)
- **Best time**: Sunrise or sunset
