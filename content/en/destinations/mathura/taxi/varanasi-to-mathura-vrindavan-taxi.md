## Why a Multi-Day Braj Yatra

Braj is not one town but a cluster - Mathura, Vrindavan, Govardhan, Gokul, Barsana, and Nandgaon - spread over 50+ km. Doing justice to it needs time, and the long approach from Varanasi makes a single day impractical. A typical comfortable plan:

- **Day 1:** Varanasi → Agra (overnight). Optionally see the **Taj Mahal** at sunset.
- **Day 2:** Agra → Mathura (Krishna Janmabhoomi, Dwarkadhish) → Vrindavan (Banke Bihari, Prem Mandir, ISKCON); overnight in Vrindavan.
- **Day 3:** Govardhan parikrama / Barsana, then begin the return to Varanasi (or break the return at Agra/Prayagraj).

Because Agra sits squarely on this route, **combining Braj with a Taj Mahal visit** is the most popular and best-value way to use the kilometres.

## What to See in Braj

**Mathura**
- **Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi** - the temple complex at Krishna's birthplace.
- **Dwarkadhish Temple** - the city's most ornate active temple.
- **Vishram Ghat** - the central ghat on the Yamuna for aarti.

**Vrindavan**
- **Banke Bihari Temple** - the emotional heart of Vrindavan (note the unique darshan style).
- **Prem Mandir** - a vast marble temple, stunning when lit in the evening.
- **ISKCON (Krishna Balaram Mandir)** and **Radha Raman** - serene and welcoming.

**Around Braj**
- **Govardhan Hill** - the 21-km parikrama linked to Krishna lifting the hill.
- **Barsana & Nandgaon** - Radha's and Krishna's villages, famous for Lathmar Holi.

## Best Time to Travel

**October to March** is most pleasant. **Janmashtami** (August/September) in Mathura and **Holi** in Barsana/Vrindavan are spectacular but extremely crowded - book early and expect heavy traffic. Summers are very hot across this plain.
