## Best Time for Varanasi Local Sightseeing Package

### By Season

**Winter (October - March):** ✅ **BEST TIME**
- Pleasant weather (10-25°C)
- Clear skies for photography
- Major festivals (Dev Deepawali, Maha Shivratri)
- Ideal for sunrise boat rides without cold discomfort

**Summer (April - June):** ⚠️ **HOT**
- Extreme heat (30-45°C)
- Start tours early (5:00-6:00 AM)
- Carry water, sunscreen, hats
- Afternoon temple visits challenging

**Monsoon (July - September):** ⚠️ **UNPREDICTABLE**
- Heavy rainfall (July-August peak)
- Some ghats submerged
- Roads can be slippery
- Sarnath remains accessible

### By Time of Day

**Early Morning (5:00 AM start):**
- ✅ Avoid temple crowds
- ✅ Perfect for sunrise boat rides
- ✅ Cool weather for walking
- ✅ Witness morning Aarti at Assi Ghat

**Mid-Morning (8:00 AM start):**
- ✅ Comfortable start time
- ⚠️ Temples start getting crowded (9:00 AM onwards)
- ✅ Good for Sarnath visit (museum opens 9:00 AM)

**Afternoon (12:00 PM start):**
- ⚠️ Hot weather (avoid April-June)
- ✅ Can include evening Ganga Aarti
- ✅ Less temple crowds

**Evening (3:00 PM start):**
- ✅ Pleasant weather
- ✅ Perfect for evening Aarti package
- ⚠️ Limited time for Sarnath (museum closes 5:00 PM)
