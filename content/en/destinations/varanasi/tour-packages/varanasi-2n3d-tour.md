## Overview

The 2 nights / 3 days Varanasi package is the most comprehensive way to enter Kashi’s rhythm without rushing. Ideal for first-time visitors seeking layered storytelling, spiritual seekers, photographers, and families who want recovery time between rituals.

## Package Profile

- **Duration:** 3 full days / 2 nights with curated 3*/4* hotels.
- **Best for:** Travellers who want both iconic sights and lesser-known cultural dives.
- **Mobility:** Private AC fleet with professional drivers and bottled water refills.
- **Support:** Local guide team, concierge chat, emergency response desk.

## Daily Breakdown

### Day 1 – Arrival, Orientation, Sunset Aarti

- Airport/rail pickup, check-in, welcome briefing, and rest.
- **Afternoon (2:00 – 5:30 PM)**
  - Baba Kaal Bhairav Temple to seek city entry blessings.
  - Kashi Vishwanath, Annapurna, and Vishalakshi shrines with Sugam Darshan arrangements.
  - Old city walking tour through silk weaving lanes, flower bazaars, local snack stalls.
- **Evening (5:30 – 8:30 PM)**
  - Golden-hour private boat ride across Assi, Dashashwamedh, and Manikarnika ghats.
  - Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti with premium viewing (boat or reserved ghat seats).
  - Dinner options: hotel buffet, rooftop café, or curated street food crawl.

### Day 2 – Sunrise Rituals, Temple Circuit, Sarnath & Culture

- **Pre-dawn (5:00 – 8:00 AM)**
  - Subah-e-Banaras sunrise boat ride, Assi Ghat morning aarti, yoga practitioners, devotional baths, and respectful cremation ghats view.
  - Old city morning walk for tea stalls, markets, and optional revisit of Kashi Vishwanath.
- **Breakfast (8:00 – 9:30 AM)** at hotel or legendary kachori-jalebi stops.
- **Late Morning (10:00 AM – 1:00 PM)**
  - Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple.
  - Durga Kund Temple and neighbouring Tulsi Manas Temple (Ramcharitmanas murals).
  - Banaras Hindu University: New Vishwanath Temple, Bharat Kala Bhavan museum, leafy campus drives.
- **Lunch + Rest (1:00 – 2:00 PM)**
- **Sarnath Excursion (2:00 – 5:00 PM)**
  - Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, Mulagandhakuti Vihara, Archaeological Museum (closed Fridays), 80-foot Buddha statue, and monastic ruins.
- **Evening Cultural Lane (5:30 – 7:30 PM)**
  - Banarasi silk weaving demonstration and showroom visit.
  - Optional Assi Ghat sunset, cultural performances, or café downtime.
- **Dinner & Overnight**

### Day 3 – Heritage Add-ons, Shopping, Departure

- **Morning Options (8:00 – 12:00 PM)**
  - **Ramnagar Fort & Museum:** Vintage cars, royal armoury, riverside views, astronomy clock.
  - OR additional temples (Tridev, Nageshwar, local kula devta shrines).
  - OR guided yoga/meditation session on the ghats.
- **Late Morning (10:00 – 1:00 PM)**
  - Shopping trail through Thatheri Bazaar, Vishwanath Gali, and Gol Ghar for silk sarees, brassware, rudraksha beads, incense, and sweets.
  - Food stops: Blue Lassi Shop, Banarasi paan parlours, tamatar chaat sellers.
- **Lunch (1:00 – 2:00 PM)**, hotel check-out, buffer to freshen up.
- **Departure (3:00 PM onwards)** transfer to airport or railway station.

## Best Season & Lead Time

- **Peak (Oct–Mar):** Festive Varanasi, 10–15 day advance booking advised.
- **Shoulder (Apr, Sep):** Mild weather, 5–7 days notice works.
- **Off-season (May–Aug):** Hot/monsoon but 30–50% savings with 2–3 day lead time.

## Premium Enhancements

- Decorated premium boats or balcony seating for Ganga Aarti.

## Culinary Roadmap

- Street chaat at Godowlia, tamatar chaat, and lassi tastings.
- Day 2 breakfast at Ram Bhandar; Day 2 evening Blue Lassi signature flavours (₹100–₹110).
- Banarasi paan at Vishwanath Gali, seasonal malaiyo (Nov–Feb).

## Travel & Safety Tips

- Pack modest clothing, comfortable footwear, sunscreen, power bank, and basic medicines.
- Drink only branded bottled water; carry sanitizer and mosquito repellent.
- Avoid photographing cremation rites; respect temple etiquette.
- Carry cash ₹10,000–15,000 with small denominations for donations and markets.
