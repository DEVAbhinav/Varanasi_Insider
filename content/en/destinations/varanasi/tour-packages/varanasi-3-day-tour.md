Ideal for spiritual seekers, families, and photographers. *See the day-by-day breakdown below for detailed timings and add-ons.*

## Why Three Days?

Stretching the itinerary over 72 hours gives breathing room to absorb Kashi’s layered rituals, heritage pockets and culinary scene without rush. You experience sunrise and sunset Aartis, temple clusters, Sarnath archaeology, Ramnagar royalty, silk ateliers, and curated cultural performances.[^overview]

- **Ideal for:** Spiritual seekers, families, photography enthusiasts, travellers craving unhurried pacing.[^audience]
- **Accommodation:** Choice of standard, deluxe, luxury properties with CP/MAP/AP meal plans.[^stay]

## Day 1 · Orientation & Evening Aarti

- Airport/rail pickup, hotel check-in, briefing over welcome drink.[^arrival]
- **Kaal Bhairav Temple** to seek entry permission into Kashi."[^^kaal]
- **Kashi Vishwanath + Annapurna + Vishalakshi temples** with Sugam Darshan facilitation.[^kv]
- **Old City Heritage Walk** through bustling galis, silk looms, brass workshops, flower markets, street snacks.[^oldcity]
- **Sunset Private Boat & Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti** with premium seating and narrative commentary.[^boat][^aarti]

## Day 2 · Sunrise Rituals, Temples, Sarnath & Culture

**Pre-dawn** – Subah-e-Banaras boat ride covering Assi Ghat morning aarti, Manikarnika from water, yoga cohorts, golden hour photography.[^subah]

**Temple loop** – Sankat Mochan, Durga Kund, Tulsi Manas Temple, BHU’s New Vishwanath temple, Bharat Kala Bhavan museum.[^temples]

**Breakfast** – Kachori-sabzi, jalebi, or hotel buffet; optional food walk upgrade.[^food]

**Sarnath immersion** (afternoon) – Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, Mulagandhakuti Vihara, 80-ft Buddha statue, Archaeological Museum (closed Friday) and nearby monastery ruins.[^sarnath]

**Cultural evening** (optional add-ons) – Private classical music recital, Kathak performance, yoga session, food walk across Godowlia lanes.[^culture]

**Dinner & overnight** – Tailored restaurant reservations or in-hotel dining.

## Day 3 · Forts, Markets & Farewell

- **Ramnagar Fort & Museum** – Vintage cars, arms gallery, astronomical clock, riverside vistas.[^ramnagar]
- **Silk weaving atelier visit** – Meet master weavers, understand brocade patterns, guided shopping to avoid tourist traps.[^silk]
- **Shopping crawl** – Banarasi sarees, brass idols, wooden toys, spices, sweets across Vishwanath Gali, Thatheri Bazaar, Golghar.[^shopping]
- **Culinary wrap** – Blue Lassi signature flavours, Banarasi paan, tamatar chaat, Malaiyo (Nov–Feb).[^^culinary]
- Leisure buffer for extra temples or photography before checkout and assisted departure transfer.

## Best Time & Booking Notes

- **Peak (Oct–Mar):** Pleasant 5°C–25°C, festivals galore. Book 10–15 days ahead; expect 20–40% rate lift.[^season]
- **Shoulder (Apr, Sep):** Moderate crowds and pricing; reserve 5–7 days ahead.[^shoulder]
- **Off-season (May–Aug):** Hot or rainy but discount-friendly; 2–3 day notice suffices.[^off]
- Declare interest in add-ons (VIP darshan, performances, yoga, photography guide) during booking to ensure slots.
- Share ID proofs for temple fast-track and flight/train details for seamless transfers.
