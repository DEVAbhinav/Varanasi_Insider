This route exists because most pilgrims visiting one city regret not adding the other. Since the Ram Janmabhoomi temple inauguration in January 2024, Ayodhya has transformed from a half-day stop into a full-day destination. Twin Aarti experiences — Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti (evening Day 1) and Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi (evening Day 2) — bookend the drive and create a spiritual arc that standalone tours can't match.[^overview]

- **Distance:** 200–220 km between cities via NH19/NH27 (4–5 hrs drive).[^distance]
- **Duration:** 3 days / 2 nights (1 night Varanasi + 1 night Ayodhya).
- **Perfect for:** Pilgrim families, Ram–Shiva devotees, festival travellers, and first-time visitors to UP's holy circuit.[^audience]

## Day 1 · Welcome to Kashi — Temples & Twilight Aarti

### Arrival & Check-in (12:00 – 13:30)

Fly into Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS) or arrive by rail at Varanasi Junction / Cantt. Chauffeured pickup in a sanitized AC vehicle, hotel check-in, briefing over welcome drink, and a quick lunch stop.[^arrival]

### Afternoon Temple Circuit (13:30 – 16:45)

#### Kaal Bhairav Temple (13:30 – 14:15)

![Kaal Bhairav Temple — where every Kashi pilgrimage begins](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkntlqbwr/image/upload/kashitaxi/kashitaxi/varanasi-kal-bhairav-p.jpeg)
*The Kotwal (divine police chief) of Kashi — tradition says you must seek his permission before entering the city's sacred geography.*

The guardian deity of Kashi. The pilgrimage sequence begins here — no journey into the city starts without his permission. One of the rare Hindu temples where **alcohol is offered** as part of the ritual.[^kaal]

**Entry:** Free. **Hours:** 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM. **Time needed:** 30–40 minutes.

#### Kashi Vishwanath Complex (14:30 – 16:00)

![Kashi Vishwanath — the Ganga Dwar entrance](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkntlqbwr/image/upload/kashitaxi/kashitaxi/kashi-vishwanath-ganga-dwar.jpeg)
*Ganga Dwar entrance to Kashi Vishwanath — one of twelve Jyotirlingas, rebuilt 1780 by Maharani Ahilyabai Holkar.*

The spiritual axis of Varanasi. The circuit covers three temples within the KV Corridor:[^kv]

1. **Kashi Vishwanath Temple** — One of twelve Jyotirlingas. Daily footfall: 10,000–15,000 pilgrims.
2. **Annapurna Temple** — Goddess of nourishment, famous for the annadan tradition.
3. **Vishalakshi Temple** — One of 51 Shakti Peethas.

**Practical notes:**
- **Sugam Darshan (fast-track):** ₹300. Reduces queue from 20–120 min to 10–15 min.
- **Security:** No mobile phones, leather items, or cameras. Lockers ₹20–50. Use **Gate 4** for shortest queue.
- **Queue reality:** Weekday afternoons: 20–40 min. Weekends: 60–120 min.
- **KV Corridor accessibility:** Accessible pathways suitable for elderly visitors and wheelchairs.

#### Old City Heritage Walk (16:00 – 16:45)

30-minute stroll from KV through Vishwanath Gali (silk shops, flower garlands), Thatheri Bazaar (brass artisans), and a quick food stop — kachori-sabzi at Ram Bhandar (₹25–35) or lassi at Blue Lassi Shop (₹60–120).[^oldcity]

## Day 3 · Ayodhya Darshan & Departure

### Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple (07:00 – 09:30)

The emotional and spiritual peak of the circuit. The Ram Mandir was inaugurated on January 22, 2024, with the Dhwajarohan (flag-hoisting) ceremony completed November 25, 2025.[^ramjanm]

**Architecture:** Nagara/Maru-Gurjara style, 110m × 72m × 49m, 366 columns — built entirely from Bansi Paharpur sandstone fused with 10,000 copper plates. No iron or steel. Designed by the Sompura family (15 generations of temple architects).

**The deity:** Ram Lalla — a 5-year-old infant Rama carved from 60-million-year-old Shaligram rocks sourced from Nepal's Kali Gandaki river. On Ram Navami, the **Surya Tilak** phenomenon directs a sunbeam onto the deity's forehead through a mirror-and-lens apparatus.

**Darshan process:**
- **Entry:** FREE. The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra trust explicitly warns that no fee is charged — beware of anyone claiming otherwise.
- **Darshan windows:** 7 slots daily, 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM (2-hour blocks).
- **Best slot:** 7:00 AM weekday for shortest queue (30–60 min). Weekends and festivals: 2–4+ hours.
- **Average daily footfall:** 1–1.5 lakh visitors.
- **Security:** Extensive screening. **No mobile phones, cameras, leather items, or bags.** Lockers available at Gate 3. Plan to arrive 30 min before your slot.
- **Dress code:** Modest clothing covering shoulders and knees. Head cover recommended for women.
- **Accessibility:** Ramps and lifts available. Pilgrim centre serves 25,000 visitors.

**Aarti schedule:** Mangal Aarti 4:00 AM, Shringar Aarti 6:00 AM, Shayan Aarti 10:00 PM. Free aarti passes bookable at online.srjbtkshetra.org.

**Our concierge role:** We handle locker logistics, queue strategy, best-time-of-day advice, and post-darshan navigation. You focus on the experience.

### Ayodhya Temple Circuit (09:30 – 13:00)

#### Hanuman Garhi (09:30 – 10:15)

The fort-shaped temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman, perched above the town with circular corner ramparts. The climb is **76 steep steps** — the view from the top is worth it if mobility allows.[^hanuman]

**What's at the top:**
- 6-inch Bal (child) Hanuman seated on mother Anjani's lap
- Silver-carved garbha griha with 3 ornate doors
- Hanuman Chalisa inscribed on interior walls
- Vijay Stambh (victory pillar)
- Panoramic views over Ayodhya

**Entry:** Free. **Hours:** ~5:00 AM – 10:00 PM. **Time needed:** 30–45 minutes.

**Elderly advisory:** The 76 steps are genuinely challenging — no lift alternative. We recommend elderly travellers do darshan from the base entrance instead. The guide assists with prasad coordination.

#### Kanak Bhawan (10:15 – 10:45)

"Sone Ka Ghar" (House of Gold) — gifted by Kaikeyi (Ram's stepmother) to Ram and Sita per the Ramayana tradition. Refurbished by King Vikramaditya and later by Bhanu Kunwari (Orchha queen).[^kanak]

**Highlight:** Gold-crowned Ram-Sita idols in the sanctum — among the most ornate deity dressings in Ayodhya.

**Entry:** Free. **Hours:** ~8:00 AM – 12:00 PM, 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM. **Time needed:** 20–30 minutes.

#### Sita Ki Rasoi & Dashrath Mahal (10:45 – 11:30)

Two adjacent heritage stops in the Ramkot area:

- **Sita Ki Rasoi** — The symbolic royal kitchen. Stone utensils on display — rolling pin, griddle, mortar — representing Sita's domestic life in the palace. Adjacent to Kanak Bhawan. Free entry, 10–15 minutes.
- **Dashrath Mahal** — Temple/palace complex at the site of King Dasharatha's royal court. Part of the Ramkot heritage circuit. Free entry, 15–20 minutes.[^dashrath]

#### Nageshwarnath Temple (11:30 – 12:00)

One of the few **non-Rama sites** in Ayodhya — an ancient Shiva shrine. Legend says Kush (Ram's son) lost his armlet in the Saryu; a nag kanya (snake princess) returned it; they married; Kush built this temple for her. The present structure dates to 1750 AD. It's the presiding deity of Ayodhya, and the Mahashivaratri crowd here rivals any temple in the city.[^nagesh]

**Entry:** Free. **Time needed:** 20–30 minutes.

### Optional Extensions (if departure timing allows)

- **Ram Ki Paidi morning stroll** — The renovated riverfront is peaceful before the afternoon crowds.
- **Guptar Ghat** — Where, per tradition, Lord Rama took jal samadhi (entered the Saryu). A quieter pilgrimage site.
- **Tulsi Smarak Bhavan** — Museum dedicated to Goswami Tulsidas, with manuscripts and artifacts related to the Ramcharitmanas.[^optional]

## Booking Intelligence

1. **Peak season (Oct–Mar):** Book 10–15 days ahead. Both cities fill during festivals.
2. **Ram Navami (Mar/Apr):** Ayodhya's biggest day. Book 3–4 weeks ahead. Crowds can reach 5+ lakh.
3. **Off-season (May–Aug):** 2–3 days notice. Hotel discounts of 30–40%.
4. **Late arrivals:** Share flight/train details at booking. We restructure Day 1 timing.
5. **Dietary needs:** Indicate Jain, vegan, sattvic, or allergy requirements. Both cities are overwhelmingly vegetarian.
6. **Mobility constraints:** Mention at booking — Hanuman Garhi has 76 steps, Ram Janmabhoomi has long walking distances, and some Varanasi ghat temples have stairs.
7. **ID proofs:** Digital copies for Sugam Darshan at KV and hotel check-ins in both cities. Aadhaar or passport accepted.

## What to Pack

- **Temple clothing:** Modest, loose cotton layers covering shoulders and knees. Dupatta/scarf for head cover. **No leather** at any temple — including belts, bags, and wallets (leave in car).
- **Footwear:** Slip-on sandals — you'll remove them at 12+ temples across both cities.
- **For the drive:** Snacks, water, entertainment for the 4–5 hr intercity leg. Neck pillow if you doze.
- **Essentials:** Sunscreen, reusable water bottle, power bank, hand sanitizer, ₹5,000–8,000 cash in small denominations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is 3 days enough for Ayodhya and Varanasi?**
Yes — 3 days comfortably covers both cities. Day 1 covers Varanasi's Kashi Vishwanath temple circuit and Ganga Aarti. Day 2 adds the sunrise boat ride, secondary temples, optional Sarnath visit, and the afternoon drive to Ayodhya with Saryu Aarti. Day 3 covers Ram Janmabhoomi, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, and departure. For Prayagraj as well, extend to 4–5 days.

**How far is Ayodhya from Varanasi?**
200–220 km via NH19/NH27 through Jaunpur and Sultanpur. The drive takes 4–5 hours in a private AC vehicle with one rest stop at Jaunpur (halfway). Road quality is reasonable — national highway throughout.

**What is the Ram Janmabhoomi darshan process?**
Entry is free — no fee is charged (the trust explicitly warns against scam claims). Darshan runs in 2-hour windows from 7 AM to 9 PM daily. Security is extensive: no mobile phones, cameras, leather items, or bags allowed inside. Lockers at Gate 3. Expect 1–2 hour queues on weekdays, longer on weekends. Early 7 AM weekday slot has the shortest wait.

**How much does the Ayodhya-Varanasi 3-day tour cost?**
Budget: ₹8,499–12,000 per person twin sharing (3-star, breakfast). Standard: ₹15,000–20,000 (enhanced hotels, some meals). Deluxe: ₹24,000–29,000 (4-star, all meals, VIP support). Luxury: ₹35,000+ (5-star/heritage, bespoke). Festival surcharge of 20–40% during peak.

**Is the tour suitable for elderly parents?**
Mostly yes with adjustments. Varanasi's KV Corridor has accessible pathways and Sarnath is flat. In Ayodhya, Ram Janmabhoomi has ramps and lifts. The challenge is Hanuman Garhi — 76 steep steps with no elevator. We recommend elderly travellers do darshan from the base. Cantonment hotels in both cities ensure smooth logistics.

**Can I start from Ayodhya instead of Varanasi?**
Yes — the itinerary reverses easily. Start with Ayodhya darshan on Day 1, drive to Varanasi on Day 2 with Ganga Aarti on arrival evening, cover Varanasi temples and sunrise boat on Day 3.

**What should I wear for temple visits?**
Modest loose clothing covering shoulders and knees. No leather items at any temple. Dupatta or scarf for head cover at Kashi Vishwanath. Ram Janmabhoomi has similar rules — no phones, cameras, or leather past the security perimeter.

**Is photography allowed at Ram Janmabhoomi?**
No — mobile phones and cameras are prohibited inside the temple complex. Security is strict. You can photograph from outside the perimeter. Lockers at Gate 3 store devices during darshan.

**What if my schedule only allows 2 days?**
The Varanasi-only [2-day tour](/en/city/varanasi/tour-packages/varanasi-2-day-tour) covers the Kashi core. Adding Ayodhya to 2 days is extremely rushed — you'd skip either sunrise or Sarnath. We strongly recommend 3 days for the twin-city circuit.
