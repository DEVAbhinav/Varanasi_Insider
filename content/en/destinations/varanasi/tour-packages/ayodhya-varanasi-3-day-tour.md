## Overview

A three-day pilgrimage weaving together Ayodhya’s Ram Janmabhoomi energy with Varanasi’s Shaivite aura. Seamless intercity transfers, concierge-backed temple access, and heritage storytelling keep the focus on devotion—not logistics.[^overview]

- **Distance:** 200–220 km between cities (≈5 hrs drive).[^distance]
- **Best for:** Pilgrim families, Ram–Shiva devotees, festival travellers, first-time visitors to UP’s holy circuit.[^audience]

## Routing Options

1. **Varanasi → Ayodhya (popular)** – Arrive in Kashi, end in Ayodhya for Ram Janmabhoomi darshan.[^routing]
2. **Ayodhya → Varanasi** – Preferred when flights/rails land in Ayodhya first.

The base itinerary below follows Option 1.

## Day-by-Day Breakdown

### Day 1 · Welcome to Kashi

- Meet-and-greet at Varanasi airport/rail, hotel check-in, briefing over welcome drink.[^arrival]
- **Kaal Bhairav Temple** – Seek guardian permission before entering Kashi circuit.[^kaal]
- **Kashi Vishwanath + Annapurna** – Sugam Darshan (₹300) assistance, prasad arrangements, locker management.[^kv]
- **Old City Heritage Walk** – Silk looms, handicraft alleys, ritual markets.[^oldcity]
- **Sunset Boat & Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti** – Reserved seating/boat vantage, synchronized lamps, devotional chants.[^aarti]
- Dinner & overnight stay in Varanasi.

### Day 2 · Varanasi Deep Dive + Drive to Ayodhya

**Morning:**

- **Subah-e-Banaras sunrise boat** at Assi Ghat, golden hour photography, morning rituals.[^subah]
- **Breakfast** – Hotel buffet or kachori-sabzi + jalebi trail.[^food]
- **Temple loop:** Sankat Mochan, Durga Kund, Tulsi Manas, BHU’s New Vishwanath Temple.[^temples]
- **Sarnath (optional, schedule permitting):** Dhamek Stupa, museum (Fri closed), 80-ft Buddha.[^sarnath]

**Afternoon transfer:**

- Private AC cab Varanasi → Ayodhya via NH19/NH27 (4–5 hrs) with curated rest stops.[^drive]

**Evening in Ayodhya:**

- Hotel check-in, rest.
- **Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi** – Serene diyas and chants on the riverfront.[^saryu]
- Optional light & sound show narrating Ramayana.[^lss]

### Day 3 · Ayodhya Darshan & Departure

- **Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple** – Guided darshan flow, locker support, best practice briefing.[^ramjanm]
- **Hanuman Garhi** – 76-step climb to cave temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman.[^hanuman]
- **Kanak Bhawan + Sita Ki Rasoi** – Gold-plated Rama-Sita idols, symbolic royal kitchen.[^kanak]
- **Dashrath Mahal, Nageshwarnath Temple** – Royal heritage and ancient Shiva shrine.[^nagesh]
- Optional extensions: Ram Ki Paidi stroll, Guptar Ghat, Tulsi Smarak Bhavan.[^optional]
- Lunch, souvenir shopping (pedas, idols, Rudraksha).[^^shopping]
- Depart from Ayodhya airport/rail **or** return by cab to Varanasi for onward travel.

- Festivals (Ram Navami, Diwali, Dev Deepawali) demand 10–15 day advance confirmation.[^festivals]
