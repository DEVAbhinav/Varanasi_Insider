## Overview

This 2 night / 3 day Kashi–Ayodhya yatra links the new Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir in Ayodhya with the timeless ghats of Varanasi. Ideal for pilgrims, families, and spiritual explorers wanting a compact Uttar Pradesh circuit that honours both Lord Rama and Lord Shiva. The itinerary balances temple time with intercity comfort, curated food stops, and concierge assistance.

## Distance, Time & Route First

I've driven this Varanasi–Ayodhya corridor more times than I can count. A few things most guides won't tell you: the expressway stretch near Sultanpur can add 45 minutes if you hit a sugarcane-truck jam, the Ayodhya city entry gets choked after 10 AM on darshan days, and the Ram Mandir queue works best if you arrive before 7 AM. Keep these in mind when reading the timing blocks below.

## 3-Day Flow (Varanasi First)

### Day 1 – Arrive in Varanasi, Temple Trail, Evening Ganga Aarti

- Airport or rail pickup, hotel check-in, welcome briefing.
- **Afternoon:**
  - Baba Kaal Bhairav Temple to seek Kashi entry blessings.
  - Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga with Annapurna and Vishalakshi temples. Sugam Darshan (₹300) arranged when available.
  - Old city walk through silk lanes, handicraft bazaars, and flower markets.
- **Evening:**
  - Private Ganges boat ride from Assi to Dashashwamedh with views of Manikarnika Ghat.
  - Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti with reserved seating or premium boat anchoring.
- Dinner and overnight stay in Varanasi.

### Day 2 – Sunrise on Ganga, Varanasi Temples, Drive to Ayodhya

- **Early Morning:** Optional Subah-e-Banaras sunrise boat ride, Assi Ghat morning aarti, yoga on the ghats.
- **Breakfast:** Hotel buffet or kachori-sabzi + jalebi stop.
- **Temple Trail (9:00 AM – 12:30 PM):** Sankat Mochan Hanuman, Durga Kund, Tulsi Manas, Banaras Hindu University with New Vishwanath Temple and Bharat Kala Bhavan.
- **Optional:** Short Sarnath detour if you prefer Buddhist heritage (adjusts departure time).
- Lunch, hotel checkout.
- **Drive to Ayodhya (200–220 km / 4–5 hrs):** Smooth highway run with curated rest stops.
- Evening check-in at Ayodhya hotel.
- Optional Saryu evening aarti at Ram Ki Paidi and light-and-sound show.

### Day 3 – Ayodhya Mandir Circuit, Departure

- **Morning temple loop (7:00 AM onwards):**
  - Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir (book online slots during peak seasons).
  - Hanuman Garhi (76 steps, iconic cave temple).
  - Kanak Bhawan and Sita Ki Rasoi.
  - Dashrath Mahal, Nageshwarnath Temple, and heritage lanes.
- **Late Morning Add-ons:**
  - Ram Ki Paidi sunrise, Lata Mangeshkar Chowk, Guptar Ghat.
  - Ramkatha Park, Tulsi Smarak Bhawan Museum, Janaki Mahal depending on pace.
- **Culinary & Shopping:** Sample sattvic thalis, aloo tikki chaat, peda laddus, and browse religious souvenirs.
- **Departure Choices:**
  - Drop at Ayodhya Airport or Railway Station.
  - Or return drive to Varanasi for onward flight/train.
  - Or extend to Prayagraj, Lucknow, or Bodhgaya.

## Seasonal Playbook

- **Peak (Oct–Mar):** Diwali, Dev Deepawali, Ram Navami. Book 10–15 days in advance; expect higher rates.
- **Shoulder (Apr, Sep):** Pleasant weather, moderate crowds, 5–7 day lead time.
- **Off-season (May–Aug):** Hot/monsoon; 30–40% discounts and 2–3 day lead time.

## Food Highlights

- **Varanasi:** Kachori-sabzi, jalebi, tamatar chaat, Blue Lassi (₹100–₹110), Banarasi paan, malaiyo in winter.
- **Ayodhya:** Sattvic thali, poori-halwa, aloo tikki chaat, peda laddus, kulhad chai.
- Recommended eateries: Blue Lassi Shop, Ram Bhandar, Ram Rasoi Bhojanalaya, Brij Bhog Restaurant, Café Saryu View.

## Travel Tips

- Carry modest attire, slip-on footwear, scarf/dupatta, sunscreen, hat, power bank, and basic medicines.
- Drink only bottled/filtered water. Keep sanitizer and tissues handy.
- Mobile phones are restricted inside Ram Janmabhoomi—use locker facilities.
- Respect photography bans at cremation ghats and during sensitive rituals.
- Budget ₹10,000–15,000 cash in small denominations for donations and markets.
