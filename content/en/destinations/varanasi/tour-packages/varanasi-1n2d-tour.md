## Overview

This 1 night / 2 days Varanasi package balances temple darshans, sunrise and sunset boat rides, a Sarnath excursion, and curated food experiences. Designed for weekend travellers, families, and first-time visitors, the program keeps logistics tight while allowing unhurried spiritual encounters.

## Snapshot

- **Duration:** 2 days / 1 night with centrally located 3-star hotel.
- **Ideal for:** Couples, small families, pilgrims wanting both morning and evening Ganga rituals, photography enthusiasts.
- **Fleet choices:** Sedan, Innova Crysta, or tempo traveller with vetted drivers.
- **Guide support:** English-speaking guide for temples, Sarnath, and food walks.

## Day-by-Day Plan

### Day 1 – Arrival, Temples, Sunset Boat + Ganga Aarti

**Morning / Noon**  
Pickup from airport or railway station, hotel transfer, check-in, and rest. Early arrivals can add a casual city orientation walk.

**Afternoon Temple Circuit (2:00 – 5:30 PM)**

- **Kashi Vishwanath Complex** – Jyotirlinga darshan plus Annapurna and Vishalakshi temples. Sugam Darshan tickets arranged when available.
- **Kaal Bhairav Temple** – Seek permission from Kashi’s guardian deity before exploring.
- **Old City Heritage Walk** – Silk weaving workshops, flower markets, handicraft alleys, and street food tasting.

**Evening Experience (5:30 – 8:30 PM)**

- **Private Ganges Boat Ride** – Golden-hour cruise covering Assi to Dashashwamedh, glimpses of Manikarnika Ghat.
- **Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti** – Reserved seating or premium boat positioning for the choreographed ceremony.
- **Dinner & Overnight** – Return to hotel for dinner (optional) and restful night.

### Day 2 – Sunrise Aarti, City Temples, Sarnath, Departure

**Pre-dawn to Breakfast (5:30 – 9:00 AM)**

- **Subah-e-Banaras Boat Ride** – Sunrise over the Ganga, morning rituals, Assi Ghat aarti, yoga sessions, and respectful view of cremation ghats.
- **Old City Walk** – Catch morning markets, chai stalls, and revisit Kashi Vishwanath if desired.
- **Breakfast** – Hotel buffet or local kachori-sabzi + jalebi at Ram Bhandar / Chachi ki Kachori.

**Morning Temple Trail (9:30 AM – 12:00 PM)**

- **Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple** – Beloved shrine founded by Tulsidas.
- **Durga Kund Temple** – Iconic red structure with adjacent pond.
- **Tulsi Manas Temple** – Scripted walls reenact the Ramcharitmanas; combine with BHU stop for New Vishwanath Temple and Bharat Kala Bhavan.

**Sarnath Excursion (12:30 – 3:00 PM)**

Drive 10 km to Buddha’s first sermon site: Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, Mulagandhakuti Vihara, 80-foot Buddha statue, and Archaeological Museum (closed Fridays).

**Afternoon Add-ons (3:30 – 5:00 PM)**

- **Banarasi Silk Weaving Workshop** – Meet master weavers and shop authentic brocades.
- **Local Markets** – Handicrafts, brassware, spices, and temple souvenirs.
- **Optional:** Ramnagar Fort or café downtime near Assi Ghat.

**Departure (5:00 PM onwards)**

Return to hotel, freshen up, check out, and transfer to airport/rail station. Late-night departures can add a second evening stroll or food crawl.

## Seasonal Booking Tips

- **Peak (Oct–Mar):** Festivals like Diwali, Dev Deepawali, and Mahashivratri. Reserve hotels and boats 7–10 days ahead.
- **Shoulder (Apr, Sep):** Moderate crowds and rates; book 3–5 days in advance.
- **Off-season (May–Aug):** Hot or rainy but budget-friendly with up to 30–40% discounts.
