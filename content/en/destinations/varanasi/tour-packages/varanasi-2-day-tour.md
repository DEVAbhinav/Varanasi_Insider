## Package Snapshot

- **Duration:** 2 days / 1 night with curated 3-star+ stay options.[^duration]
- **Perfect for:** Weekend travellers, couples, families, pilgrims after deeper immersion.[^audience]
- **Core experiences:** Evening Ganga Aarti, sunrise Subah-e-Banaras, Sarnath, BHU campus, silk weaving, foodie trail.

## Day-by-Day Game Plan

### Day 1 · Arrival, Temples & Twilight Aarti

**Arrivals** – Fly into Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport or roll in by rail; chauffeured pickup and assisted check-in at vetted hotels (3-star upwards).[^^arrival]

**Afternoon Temple Circuit (14:00 – 17:30)**

- **Kashi Vishwanath Complex** – Sugam Darshan support, Annapurna & Vishalakshi shrines, ritual walkthrough.[^kv]
- **Kaal Bhairav Temple** – Seek permission from the guardian deity of Kashi before continuing exploration.[^kaal]
- **Old City Heritage Walk** – Silk looms, handicraft alleys, flower market photo ops and local snack tasting.[^oldcity]

**Evening River Experience (17:30 – 20:30)**

- **Private Boat Ride** – Sunset cruise covering 84 ghats, cremation briefing handled sensitively.[^boat]
- **Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti** – Reserved ghat/boat seating, synchronized lamps, conch shells, percussion.[^aarti]

**Dinner & Stay** – Return to hotel for dinner (MAP/AP upgrades available) and overnight rest.

### Day 2 · Sunrise Mystique, Sarnath & Cultural Deep Dive

**Subah-e-Banaras (05:30 – 08:00)**

- **Sunrise Boat Ride** – Watch the ghats awaken, yoga practitioners, priests, and morning aarti at Assi Ghat (05:00 summer / 05:30 winter).[^sunrise]
- **Old City Walk** – Narrow galis, shrine hopping, chai and kachori stalls coming alive.[^walk]

**Breakfast (08:00 – 09:00)**

- Hotel buffet or iconic kachori-sabzi + jalebi at Ram Bhandar / Chachi ki Kachori.[^food]

**Temple & Campus Loop (09:30 – 12:00)**

- **Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple** – Tulsidas legacy and laddu prasad experience.[^sankat]
- **Durga Kund & Tulsi Manas Temple** – Shakti energy and Ramcharitmanas inscriptions.[^durga]
- **Banaras Hindu University (BHU)** – New Vishwanath (Birla) Temple, Bharat Kala Bhavan museum, leafy boulevards.[^bhu]

**Sarnath Excursion (12:30 – 15:00)**

- Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, Mulagandhakuti Vihara, 80-ft Buddha statue and Archaeological Museum (Fri closed).[^sarnath]

**Cultural Add-ons (15:30 – 17:00)**

- Banarasi silk weaving studio, artisan interactions, souvenir curation.[^silk]
- Optional Ramnagar Fort detour for royal armory and vintage car museum (time permitting).[^ramnagar]

**Departure (17:00 onwards)** – Assisted hotel checkout, onward drop to airport/rail.

## Booking Tips

1. Peak months (Oct–Mar) book out 7–10 days ahead; shoulder months (Apr, Sep) 3–5 days.
2. Share arrival details for seamless pickup coordination and early room readiness.
3. Indicate dietary preferences (Jain, vegan, sattvic) to tailor meal reservations.
4. Add optional cultural experiences (yoga, food walk, photography tour) at the time of confirmation for guaranteed slots.
