# Chhath Puja 2026 in Varanasi

It also referred to Banaras station by the obsolete “Manduadih (MUV)” identity. The guide now separates verified dates from details that require local confirmation.

Source: [Drik Panchang — Chhath Puja Calendar 2026](https://www.drikpanchang.com/chhath/chhath-puja-calendar.html?time-format=24plushour&year=2026)

The sunset and sunrise offering times depend on the selected locality. Recheck the Varanasi-specific panchang and local organiser notice close to the observance.

## Choosing a ghat

Chhath observances take place at multiple riverfront and neighbourhood water sites. Do not rely on a commercial page's promise that a particular ghat will have easier entry, permanent platforms, parking or a direct vehicle drop. Check:

- Whether the local committee is holding the observance there in 2026
- Barricades, pedestrian entry and emergency exits
- Water-level and river-safety conditions
- Lighting, toilets and accessibility for older travellers

## Transport and station information

Varanasi's principal railway stations include **Varanasi Junction (BSB)**, **Banaras (BSBS)**, **Varanasi City (BCY)** and **Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Junction (DDU)**. Banaras station was formerly called Manduadih; current tickets use **BSBS**, not the old MUV code. The [Indian Railways station list](https://indianrailways.gov.in/railwayboard/uploads/directorate/traffic_comm/Station_Name_8477.pdf) identifies Banaras as BSBS.

For road travel, the last permitted vehicle point may change when the police issue the event plan. A driver cannot guarantee access beyond a barricade. Get the vehicle, fare, waiting terms and pickup point in writing.

## Traffic and boat status

Restrictions are likely near busy observance sites during Sandhya and Usha Arghya, but this page does not invent exact closure times or routes. Check Varanasi district and Traffic Police notices before departure.

Boat operations can be limited or suspended for crowd and water safety. Treat any boat reservation as conditional on the official river order, and verify the operator's licence, boarding point and refund terms.

## Safety checklist

- Arrive with enough time to walk from an outer vehicle point.
- Keep children and vulnerable travellers beside a responsible adult.
- Agree on a visible regroup point before entering the crowd.
- Carry drinking water, essential medicines and a charged phone.
- Stay out of the ritual space between a devotee and the water.
- Follow police, fire-service and river-police directions.
- Avoid deep or slippery water and do not obstruct steps or emergency lanes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the old 28–31 October 2026 schedule valid?

No. The correct four-day sequence is **13–16 November 2026**.

### When should I buy puja supplies?

Markets may become busy, but this page does not claim a guaranteed price pattern. Compare quality and price, retain receipts for large purchases and avoid blocking ritual routes with bulky items.

### Are boats guaranteed to operate?

No. Use the current river-safety order. An operator's confirmation does not override a police suspension.
