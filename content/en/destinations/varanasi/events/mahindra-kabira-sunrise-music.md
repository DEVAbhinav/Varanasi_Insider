# Mahindra Kabira Festival – Morning Music Sessions at Sunrise

**Quick Facts**

- **When:** 6:30 AM on 20 & 21 December 2025
- **Where:** Guleria Kothi steps (boats moored on the Ganges)
- **Entry:** Included with delegate or daily pass (limited stand-by seats for locals)
- **Best For:** Classical music lovers, photographers, meditators craving quiet

> Planning the full festival? Read the [complete Mahindra Kabira Festival guide](/en/city/varanasi/events/mahindra-kabira-festival-2025-varanasi) for tickets, lineup, and hotel zones.

**Dates:** December 20-21, 2025
**Time:** 6:30 AM (approximately 45 minutes)
**Venue:** Guleria Kothi, Varanasi Ghats
**Entry:** Festival delegate pass or daily pass

### The Experience of Raag at Sunrise

Imagine waking before dawn, wrapping yourself in a warm shawl, and making your way to the Ganges as stars fade and darkness lifts. You settle on the ancient steps by the water, and as the first light touches the horizon, a master sitar player's fingers dance across strings, creating *raag* – the classical Indian musical form that responds to time of day and emotional mood.

![Sunrise boat on the Ganges](/images/varanasi-river-sunrise-boat-p.jpeg "Dawn music sessions unfold on the ghats as sunrise paints the river")

This is the essence of the Mahindra Kabira Festival's morning music sessions – a rare convergence of art, spirituality, and nature.

### December 20 Morning Program

**Featured Artists:**
- **Swati Tiwari:** Renowned classical vocalist.
- **Siddharth Chakravarti:** Tabla artist (percussion).
- **Hidayat Husain Khan:** Master sitar player.

The session features morning raags – musical forms specifically composed for dawn. These raags are believed to align with the body's natural awakening rhythms. Swati Tiwari's vocal performance will precede Hidayat Husain Khan's sitar masterclass.

**Hidayat Husain Khan's Legacy:**
Son of the legendary Ustad Vilayat Khan, Hidayat represents one of India's oldest musical dynasties. The Vilayat Khan lineage has shaped sitar tradition for generations. His appearance at the festival is significant – he rarely performs in small venues.

### What Makes December's Morning Ideal

- **Acoustic Perfection:** Winter air carries sound crisply without summer's heat distortion.
- **Spiritual Ambiance:** Early morning ritual energy amplifies the musical experience.
- **Natural Lighting:** Golden sunrise provides visual beauty.
- **Reduced Disturbance:** Fewer tourists mean sacred atmosphere remains intact.
- **Temperature:** Cool morning air keeps you alert and receptive.

### How to Maximize This Experience

**Preparation (Night Before)**
- Sleep well – the 4:45 AM start requires rest.
- Lay out warm clothes, avoid early-morning scrambling.
- Eat light dinner; some prefer fasting before spiritual experiences.
- Set alarm for 4:30 AM.

**Morning Routine**
- Arrive at venue by 6:00 AM.
- Sit toward back if camera use planned (respect performers).
- Silence phone completely.
- Find spot with clear view but doesn't obstruct others.

**During Performance**
- Allow the music to wash over you rather than analyzing.
- Close eyes if it enhances the experience.
- Observe your breath synchronizing with raag's rhythm.
- Notice how your emotions shift with musical phrases.

**After Performance (Equally Important)**
- Sit in silence for few minutes – don't rush away.
- Journal your experience before it fades.
- Engage respectfully with other attendees.
- Purchase CDs or digital recordings if available.

### The Science Behind Morning Raag

Classical Indian musicology categorizes raags by time of day. Morning raags typically:
- Use ascending pitch sequences (aligning with sun's rise).
- Feature soft, meditative notes.
- Include specific note combinations that are believed to energize.
- Follow rhythmic patterns that echo morning bird songs.

**Example Morning Raag:** *Bhairav* (performed in early morning), featuring the note 'ri' which connects to awakening consciousness in Ayurvedic tradition.

### Logistics & Booking

**Cost:**
- Included in 3-day festival pass (₹40,000).
- Daily pass: ₹5,000-8,000.
- Some sessions open to non-ticket holders (arrive early, space permitting).

**Accessibility:**
- Seating on ancient ghat steps (not wheelchair accessible).
- Bring cushion if sitting on stone for 45 minutes is uncomfortable.
- No stairs – level ground access from main ghat.

**Weather Consideration:**
- December mornings range 8-15°C.
- Bring thermal wear, not just blankets (wind off river is cold).
- Risk of fog (very rare in mid-December but possible early month).

### Beyond the Session: Deeper Engagement

**Meet the Musicians (Optional):**
If appropriate opportunities arise, a brief conversation with artists (not during their rest time) can be enriching. Most musicians are humble and appreciate genuine musical interest.

**Learn About Raag:**
The festival includes daytime sessions on raag basics – attending one helps you appreciate morning session nuances.

**Recording & Memory:**
While some official recordings may be available, the live experience is irreplaceable. Rather than filming, consider recording audio on phone – you'll preserve sound while staying present.
