# Banaras Lit Fest 2026 Complete Venue & Travel Guide – Taj Ganges Location, Hotels & Logistics

> **Archive notice:** Banaras Lit Fest 2026 was held **30 January–1 February 2026** and has ended. Venue and programme details can change between editions; check the [official festival website](https://banaraslitfest.org/) before using this guide for a future trip.

Planning your Banaras Lit Fest 2026 attendance (January 30 – February 1)? Beyond the intellectual calibre of speakers and the literary excellence on the program, your experience depends heavily on how smoothly you move through Varanasi.

This guide walks you through Taj Ganges as a venue, nearby accommodation options, airport and railway access, ghat experiences, and practical daily movement strategies so you can focus on sessions instead of worrying about traffic or timings.

> **Snapshot:** BLF 2026 is hosted at Hotel Taj Ganges, Nadesar – a 5-star heritage property about 21 km from Varanasi airport and 1.1 km from Varanasi Junction, with daily programming from 10:00 AM to 9:30 PM.

## Hotel Taj Ganges – The Festival Venue

### Location & Accessibility

- **Full address:** Nadesar Palace Grounds, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh 221002
- **Area type:** Cantonment district – upscale, peaceful, away from old-city congestion
- **Distance from Varanasi Junction railway station:** ~1.1 km (10–15 minutes on foot, 2–5 minutes by auto)
- **Distance from Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport:** ~21 km (about 19–22 minutes by car in normal traffic)
- **Distance from Kashi Vishwanath Temple:** ~3.5 km (10–15 minutes by car)
- **Distance from Dashashwamedh Ghat:** ~2.2 km (around 5–10 minutes by car depending on traffic)

Taj Ganges sits in a greener, quieter part of Varanasi, which means you get a breather from the intensity of the old city while still being 10–15 minutes away from the ghats and temple area by road.

### Why Taj Ganges Works So Well for BLF 2026

Taj Ganges isn’t just another conference hotel. Its selection for India’s premier literary festival reflects a few important advantages:

- **5-star credentials:** 130 rooms and suites, excellent housekeeping, and reliable service standards
- **Heritage grounds:** Located on historic Nadesar Palace land, blending modern comfort with cultural gravitas
- **Natural ambience:** Manicured lawns, quiet pathways and gardens where you can process intense sessions in silence
- **Multiple event spaces:** Halls, breakout spaces and outdoor venues that can run parallel sessions without chaos
- **On-site F&B:** Two restaurants plus a tea lounge – meaning you can eat, meet, and reset without leaving the property

### Amenities That Matter for Festival Attendees

- **High-speed WiFi:** Stable connectivity across the hotel – critical if you’re a media delegate or working between sessions
- **Business centre:** For last-minute manuscript edits, article submissions, or Zoom calls
- **Spa and wellness:** J Wellness Circle, a pool, and a fitness centre to unwind after long days of programming

If budget allows, staying at Taj Ganges itself is the most frictionless choice – zero commute, maximum networking.

## Reaching Taj Ganges from Varanasi Airport

### Scenario 1: Pre-Booked Kashi Taxi (Recommended)

- English-speaking driver meets you at Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport arrivals with a name placard
- Drive to Taj Ganges: ~21 km, usually 19–22 minutes in normal traffic
- Typical cost: ₹2,200–2,800 depending on vehicle (sedan vs SUV)
- Drop directly at hotel or via a quick food/ATM stop if requested in advance

### Scenario 2: Generic Taxi or App Cab

- Airport taxi stand operates through most of the day
- Indicative cost: ₹500–700 one-way, often negotiated and variable
- Ride quality and driver behaviour can be hit-or-miss
- Limited English communication; less helpful if flights are delayed or you are a first-time visitor

### Scenario 3: Hotel Shuttle

- Some hotels, including Taj Ganges, may arrange private transfers
- Cost and availability vary – check at the time of room booking
- Usually very reliable, but you may need to coordinate timings tightly with your arrival

For many delegates, the sweet spot is a pre-booked service like Kashi Taxi: you fix the price in advance, share your flight details, and let the local team handle timing and route choices.

## Local Attractions Near Taj Ganges (For Free Time)

If you’re extending your stay or have free windows between sessions, a few high-yield experiences are easy to integrate.

### Kashi Vishwanath Temple

- Distance: ~3.5 km from Taj Ganges
- Why visit: One of India’s most important Shiva temples and central to Kashi’s spiritual identity
- Best time: Early morning (5:00–7:00 AM) or late evening
- Allow: 1–2 hours including travel and darshan

### Dashashwamedh Ghat

- Distance: ~2.2 km from Taj Ganges
- Highlights: Evening Ganga Aarti, dense ghat life, street food and markets
- Best time: Early morning for a quieter feel, or evening for the aarti
- Allow: 1–2 hours

### Assi Ghat

- Distance: ~3 km
- Highlights: Slightly more relaxed vibe, good for sunrise walks and quieter contemplation
- Best time: 4:30–6:30 AM or sunset hours
- Allow: 1–1.5 hours

### Sarnath

- Distance: ~13 km (25–30 minutes by car)
- Importance: Buddha’s first sermon site; major Buddhist pilgrimage centre with stupas, monasteries, and a museum
- Best fit: Half-day or full-day excursion away from festival days or after BLF

### Markets & Silk Shops

- **Godowlia and old city lanes:** Ideal for kites, sweets, trinkets, and the texture of daily life
- **Banarasi silk showrooms:** For sarees, dupattas, and handloom textiles

If you’re short on time, combining a quick Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh with a short market stroll gives you a compact yet powerful sense of the city.

## Ghat Tours – Perfect for Literary Attendees

Many writers and readers find their deepest Varanasi memories not in halls but on the ghats.

### Subah-e-Banaras (Dawn Experience)

- 5:30–7:00 AM boat ride along the ghats
- Soft morning light, bells, aarti, and everyday rituals unfolding simultaneously
- Powerful sensory context for the sessions you’ll attend later in the day

**Typical Flow:**

1. 5:00–5:15 AM: Taxi pickup from Taj Ganges or nearby hotel
2. 5:30 AM: Board boat at Assi or Dashashwamedh Ghat
3. 5:30–7:00 AM: Boat ride and guided overview of major ghats
4. 7:00–7:30 AM: Return to hotel, breakfast, and freshen up

If you pre-book a local operator or coordinate via your taxi provider, this entire segment can be slotted into a festival morning without missing any sessions.

## Daily Transport Strategy During BLF 2026

### Option A: You’re Staying at Taj Ganges or Very Close By

- No daily commute to venue
- Arrange transport only for ghats, temples, and post-festival sightseeing
- Daily transport budget: ~₹1,000–1,500 if you do one ghat visit or market run per day

### Option B: You’re Staying 8–12 km Away

- Pre-book a taxi for morning and night commutes or even full-day coverage
- Festival core timings: 10:00 AM – 9:30 PM, so a single full-day vehicle often makes sense
- Ballpark: ₹5,200–8,800 total for a 3-day package depending on vehicle type and distance

### Option C: Mix-and-Match Strategy

- Day 1: Private taxi from airport + check-in
- Day 2: Use hotel shuttle or shared options
- Day 3: Dedicated taxi for ghat tour + airport departure

Whatever you choose, the key is **clarity of plan** – fix your hotel first, then lock in how you’ll move between airport, venue, and ghats.

## Practical Visitor Tips for BLF 2026

### Weather & What to Pack

- Late January temperatures: typically 12–20°C
- Mornings and nights can feel chilly, especially on the ghats
- Pack light layers, a warm stole or jacket, and comfortable walking shoes

### Food & Health

- Rely on hotel restaurants and well-reviewed eateries for the first day
- Introduce street food gradually if you’re not used to it
- Carry basic medicines, ORS, and water; Varanasi days are long and stimulating

### Connectivity & Power

- Jio, Airtel and Vodafone generally work well around the festival area
- Carry a power bank; you’ll likely be using your phone for photos, notes, and networking
- Keep a universal adapter if you’re coming from abroad

### Culture & Etiquette

- Remove shoes in temples; dress modestly for religious sites
- Ask before photographing people, especially on the ghats
- Expect cows, dogs, and sudden processions in the streets; patience goes a long way
