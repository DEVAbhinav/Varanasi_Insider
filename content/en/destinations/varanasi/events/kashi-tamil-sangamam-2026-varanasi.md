# Kashi Tamil Sangamam: verified 2025 recap and 2026 status

> **Correction notice, updated 3 September 2026:** Kashi Tamil Sangamam 4.0 was a **2025 event**, not a December 2026 event. No dates for a new 2026 edition have been officially announced. Earlier information on this page incorrectly shifted the 2025 programme into 2026.

## What is confirmed

Kashi Tamil Sangamam 4.0 began in Varanasi on **2 December 2025**. The Government of India described it as a cultural and educational exchange connecting Tamil Nadu and Kashi, with more than 1,400 delegates expected in organised groups.

The related Tamil-learning programme in Varanasi schools ran from **2 to 15 December 2025**. Those dates must not be presented as a 2026 visitor programme.

**Primary source:** [Press Information Bureau — Kashi Tamil Sangamam 4.0](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2192810&lang=1&reg=1)

## 2026 status

As of **3 September 2026**, no official Government of India announcement reviewed for this update confirms dates, registration, delegate batches, venues, or a transport plan for another 2026 edition.

- Treat pages carrying **2–15 December 2026** as outdated unless they link to a new government notification.
- Avoid non-refundable event-specific hotel, flight, or transport bookings.
- Check the Press Information Bureau and the official ministries associated with the programme before planning.
- Use ordinary December travel advice for Varanasi until a new edition is formally announced.

## What happened during KTS 4.0

The 2025 programme brought delegates from Tamil Nadu to Kashi for academic sessions, cultural performances, temple and heritage visits, and interaction with local institutions. Banaras Hindu University was an important programme location, but activities were distributed across the city.

## Transport after a future announcement

If a new edition is announced, confirm your delegate batch, reporting point, venue, and government-provided transport before hiring a private vehicle. Event traffic plans can change by batch and by day.
