## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Varanasi to Vindhyachal taxi / cab fare?

The Varanasi to Vindhyachal fare is about ₹1,877 one-way and ₹2,554 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹3,610 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹5,220 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Varanasi to Vindhyachal fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. This short hop has little or no NHAI toll for a car (well under ₹100). There is no separate state tax on this route.

### Is the Varanasi to Vindhyachal fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Varanasi to Vindhyachal trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹3,610 round-trip.

### Is the Varanasi to Vindhyachal fare per car or per person?

Per car. The fare covers the whole vehicle — fuel, tolls and the driver — however many of you travel, up to the seat limit. A Dzire seats 4, an Ertiga 6 and an Innova 7, so a family sharing one car pays once, not per head.

### Do you provide AC cars for Varanasi to Vindhyachal?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Varanasi to Vindhyachal one-way drops?

Yes, a Varanasi to Vindhyachal one-way drop is available (Dzire from ₹1,877). One-way is the cheaper option when you don't need the car to return.
