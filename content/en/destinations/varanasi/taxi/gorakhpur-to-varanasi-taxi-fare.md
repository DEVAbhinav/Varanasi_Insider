## Frequently asked questions

The Gorakhpur to Varanasi fare is about ₹5,253 one-way and ₹7,205 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹10,175 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹14,850 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### What is the Gorakhpur to Varanasi distance and travel time?

Gorakhpur to Varanasi is about 270-280 km. Plan on 5-5.5 hr in normal traffic, or up to 6+ hr when the road is busy.

### Do you provide AC cars for Gorakhpur to Varanasi?
