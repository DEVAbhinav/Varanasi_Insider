## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Varanasi to Gaya taxi / cab fare?

The Varanasi to Gaya fare is about ₹4,813 one-way and ₹6,602 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹9,324 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹13,608 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Varanasi to Gaya fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. Cars cross 4–5 plazas (Daffi, Mohania, Sasaram, Aurangabad/Sherghati, Dobhi) for about ₹400–500 in tolls one-way. Bihar entry + green tax for a private car is about ₹200–250 one-way at the UP–Bihar border, so total toll + tax runs ~₹600–750.

### Is the Varanasi to Gaya fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Varanasi to Gaya trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹9,324 round-trip.

### Is the Varanasi to Gaya fare per car or per person?

Per car. The fare covers the whole vehicle — fuel, tolls and the driver — however many of you travel, up to the seat limit. A Dzire seats 4, an Ertiga 6 and an Innova 7, so a family sharing one car pays once, not per head.

### Do you provide AC cars for Varanasi to Gaya?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Varanasi to Gaya one-way drops?

Yes, a Varanasi to Gaya one-way drop is available (Dzire from ₹4,813). One-way is the cheaper option when you don't need the car to return.

### How do I book a Varanasi to Gaya taxi?

Message +91 99354 74730 on WhatsApp with your date, number of passengers and preferred vehicle — you get a fixed fare back instantly.
