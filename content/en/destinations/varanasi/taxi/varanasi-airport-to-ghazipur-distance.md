## Best Varanasi Airport to Ghazipur route

From the airport, cabs typically:

1. Exit VNS and join **NH-31**
2. Follow NH-31 directly toward Ghazipur, which is the **shortest and smoothest** connection.

The highway is mostly straightforward, with a few slower stretches near toll plazas and local markets. Monsoon showers can briefly slow traffic near low-lying patches by the river.

## Varanasi Airport to Ghazipur taxi fare (2026)

- **Hatchback:** ₹984–₹1,520, ideal for 2–3 budget travellers.
- **Sedan:** ₹1,533–₹1,795, best for families with luggage.
- **Innova:** ₹1,520–₹1,795 for groups needing extra space.
- **Extra km charge:** ₹11.75–₹17.5/km if you go beyond the standard drop zone.

Ask for an itemised quote covering fuel, driver allowance, and expected tolls to keep your budget predictable.

## Places to visit in Ghazipur after you arrive

- **Ghazipur Fort:** Historic riverside fort with views of the Ganga.
- **Lord Cornwallis Tomb:** British-era monument and landscaped garden.
- **Dadri Ghat:** Scenic ghat popular for walks and local rituals.
- **Ganga Das Baba Ashram (Bayepur):** Spiritual centre by the river.
- **Parshurama Temple:** Temple dedicated to Lord Parshurama.
- **Collector Ghat:** Another busy ghat used by locals for daily activities.

## FAQs about Varanasi Airport to Ghazipur taxis

### Are night transfers from VNS to Ghazipur safe?
The highway is active with trucks and buses at night. Book a reputable operator, avoid unnecessary roadside stops, and share live location with family.

### Can I book a round trip for a same-day visit?
Yes, same-day packages are common. Discuss your halt time in Ghazipur and the total km expected so the operator can quote a fair round-trip price.

### Do buses run between Varanasi and Ghazipur?
Buses connect the two cities, but they generally start from city bus stands, not the airport itself. For door-to-door comfort with luggage, a taxi is more practical.
