## Best Varanasi Airport to Ghazipur route

From the airport, cabs typically:

1. Exit VNS and join **NH-31**
2. Follow NH-31 directly toward Ghazipur, which is the **shortest and smoothest** connection.

The highway is mostly straightforward, with a few slower stretches near toll plazas and local markets. Monsoon showers can briefly slow traffic near low-lying patches by the river.

## Places to visit in Ghazipur after you arrive

- **Ghazipur Fort:** Historic riverside fort with views of the Ganga.
- **Lord Cornwallis Tomb:** British-era monument and landscaped garden.
- **Dadri Ghat:** Scenic ghat popular for walks and local rituals.
- **Ganga Das Baba Ashram (Bayepur):** Spiritual centre by the river.
- **Parshurama Temple:** Temple dedicated to Lord Parshurama.
- **Collector Ghat:** Another busy ghat used by locals for daily activities.
