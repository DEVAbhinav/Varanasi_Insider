## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Varanasi to Ayodhya taxi / cab fare?

The Varanasi to Ayodhya fare is about ₹3,820 one-way and ₹5,240 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹7,400 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹10,800 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Varanasi to Ayodhya fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. Two common routings: via NH-31 (Hauj ₹125 + Bainti Khurd ₹125 + Bharatkhund ₹40 ≈ ₹290) or the older NH-28 (Amora Moiddinpur ₹110 + Amdi ₹100 ≈ ₹210) — a car toll of roughly ₹210–290 one-way, included in your fare. There is no separate state tax on this route.

### Is the Varanasi to Ayodhya fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Varanasi to Ayodhya trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹7,400 round-trip.

### Is the Varanasi to Ayodhya fare per car or per person?

Per car. The fare covers the whole vehicle — fuel, tolls and the driver — however many of you travel, up to the seat limit. A Dzire seats 4, an Ertiga 6 and an Innova 7, so a family sharing one car pays once, not per head.

### Do you provide AC cars for Varanasi to Ayodhya?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Varanasi to Ayodhya one-way drops?

Yes, a Varanasi to Ayodhya one-way drop is available (Dzire from ₹3,820). One-way is the cheaper option when you don't need the car to return.

### How do I book a Varanasi to Ayodhya taxi?

Message +91 99354 74730 on WhatsApp with your date, number of passengers and preferred vehicle — you get a fixed fare back instantly.
