## ✈️ Varanasi Airport Snapshot (2026)

- **Code:** VNS / Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport
- **Elevation:** 83 m · Single runway (09/27) with CAT-I ILS
- **Peak hours:** 10:00–13:30 and 17:00–20:30 (expect belt + taxi stand queues)
- **City distance:** 23–27 km depending on the ghat/railhead you choose
- **Security:** CISF controlled, e-boarding gates, dedicated tourist helpdesk near belt 2
- **EV & luggage support:** Golf carts + porter desk available 24x7 (₹250 onwards)

## How Arrival Coordination Works (3-Step Playbook)

1. **Ping dispatch before take-off.** Share your PNR/flight number, landing time, pax count, luggage style, and any temple or hotel priorities. You get a written fare confirmation so there’s no negotiation outside Babatpur arrivals.
2. **Receive your digital docket.** We WhatsApp the driver photo, plate, Fastag balance, GST invoice preference, and the queued barricade recommendation (Godowlia, Assi, NaMo promenade, etc.).
3. **Meet & ride.** Driver waits at Gate 3 with a placard. Luggage assist + in-car water is standard. If you add a second stop (ATM, mithai shop, railhead) we update the docket in real time so billing stays transparent.

> **Need last-minute help?** Reply “SOS” on WhatsApp for an operations desk callback within 90 seconds.

## Kashi Taxi vs Prepaid Booth vs Ola/Uber (VNS arrivals)

| Decision factor | **Kashi Taxi Dispatch** | **Airport Prepaid Booth** | **Ola/Uber Apps** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Headline fare** | ₹690–₹1,380 fixed in writing before wheels-down | Printed slip (₹750–₹1,050) but varies by queue | Dynamic surge ₹650–₹1,400 | 
| **Wait time** | Driver staged at Gate 3 with 15-min standby | 20–35 min queue (longer on Dev Deepawali) | 10–25 min; drivers often cancel long trips |
| **Corridor / ghat access** | Pre-filed manifests + e-rickshaw handoff | Only drops till cantonment/bus stand | Many drivers refuse barricade zones |
| **Support & reroute** | 24x7 ops desk reroutes via Ring Road or Assi lane | Limited to booth duty manager | In-app chat; no local barricade intel |
| **Flight delay protection** | Auto hold + backup driver | Slip expires; must repay | Driver may leave; surge resets |

Result: searchers scanning the SERP instantly see why the ₹690 flash fare is the safer bet versus prepaid or app cabs.

## 🛡️ Permits, Payments & Safety Checklist

- **Receipts & GST:** Standard airport taxis include tolls + parking. For corporate claims, request a GST invoice (Varanasi Taxi – Vinayak Travels, GSTIN 09AAFFV5752L1ZF) before you board.
- **Temple access permits:** Kal Bhairav, Kashi Vishwanath corridor, and certain ghats during Dev Deepawali require pre-submitted manifests. Share ID copies 12 hours in advance to avoid barricade denials.
- **Payment modes:** Cash, UPI, corporate cards, and bank transfer supported. International passengers can prepay via Stripe link to avoid forex hassles.
- **Safety stack:** Police-verified drivers, GPS telematics, in-car panic button, live ride tracking shared with your family. Female travelers can request Pink Shield add-ons (female marshal, body cam, codeword escalation).
- **Support escalations:** Ops desk is reachable 24×7; if you miss a flight, we automatically reassign the vehicle and freeze billing until the new ETA.

## 🛰️ Route Intelligence & Weather Triggers

- **Ring Road chokepoint:** If there’s a blockade near Lahartara, drivers switch via Nadesar–Sigra which adds ~10 minutes but keeps you moving.
- **Festival lockdowns:** Mahashivratri, Dev Deepawali, Kartik Purnima – only vehicles with prior manifests are allowed near Godowlia/Assi.
- **Monsoon alert:** When the Ganga crosses 68 m, lower ghats such as Scindia & Manikarnika reroute to upper lanes; expect a porter handoff.

## ✈️ Airport Facilities

- **Terminals:** Single integrated terminal for domestic & international movements with four aerobridges.
- **Lounges:** "The Lounge" (priority pass / paid) + dedicated VVIP lounge for protocol visits.
- **Dining:** Costa Coffee, Subway, local chai counters, mithai kiosks both landside and airside.
- **Shopping:** Banarasi handloom boutique, brassware studio, wellness teas, travel sim kiosks.
- **Medical & safety:** 24x7 first-aid room, dedicated tourist police & lost-and-found counter near arrival gate 2.

## ❓ Varanasi Airport FAQ

**How long does it take to reach the main ghats from VNS?**
Plan 50–60 minutes for Kashi Vishwanath / Dashashwamedh depending on barricade checks. Add 10–12 minutes buffer if you land during evening aarti. For exact distances and route options, check the tabbed directory above.

**What's the difference between this guide and the price chart?**
This page covers **comprehensive logistics**: distances, routes, permits, arrival timelines, and outstation planning. For **detailed pricing, flash deals, and fare comparisons**, visit our [Airport Taxi Price Chart](/en/varanasi-airport-taxi-price-guide).

**Are app cabs reliable at the airport?**
Ola/Uber work but suffer from surge pricing + last-minute cancellations during peak arrivals. Pre-booked cabs provide written fares, escort help at barricades, and backup vehicles for flight delays.

**Can I book an outstation taxi directly from the airport?**
Yes. Share your multi-city plan for a written itinerary with driver allowances, tolls, state entry permits, and backup hotline numbers.

**Is a night pickup safe?**
Absolutely—drivers are police-verified, vehicles have panic buttons + GPS, and our ops desk tracks the ride till drop. Request a female marshal or porter for late-night ghat entries if needed.
