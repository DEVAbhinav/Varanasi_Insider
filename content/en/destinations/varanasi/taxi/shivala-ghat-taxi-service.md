## Shivala Ghat taxi FAQs

### Can the taxi drop me inside my hotel, or will I need to walk?
It depends on your hotel. Heritage Inn has parking and direct drop. Ghat-side guesthouses usually need a 2-5 minute walk from Sonarpura Crossing.

### Is Shivala Ghat safe for solo female travelers?
Yes. Quieter than the main ghats with steady police presence. Female drivers on request.

### Is Shivala quiet enough to sleep early and meditate?
Yes. Minimal traffic and no cruise horns. Expect early temple bells around 4-5 AM.

### Can I book a taxi from Shivala to do a ghat-hopping tour?
Yes. Half-day ghat tours start from Shivala (3-4 hours): Shivala → Assi → Dashashwamedh (Aarti) → return. Driver waits while you explore.

### How long is the walk from Shivala to the water?
About 5-7 minutes down the steps. Good shoes recommended. Sunrise views are less crowded than Assi or Dashashwamedh.
