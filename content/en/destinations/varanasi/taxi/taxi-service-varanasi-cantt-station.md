## Avoiding taxi scams at Cantt Railway Station
- **“Rickshaw relay”:** Autos move you 1 km then hand you off again, inflating total cost. We provide one private, end-to-end car.
- **“Fake guide”:** Drivers posing as guides push costly boat rides or silk shops. Our drivers skip detours unless you ask.
- **Direct station pickup:** We guide you to the circulating area so you avoid tout-heavy exits and keep luggage close.

## Varanasi Cantt taxi FAQs

### Which exit should I take for the taxi pickup?
Use the Cantt Side (Platform 1) exit—it has better car access than the City Side (Platform 9). The driver will call to coordinate.

### Do you offer pickups for large groups with luggage?
Yes. Innovas and Tempo Travellers are available; pre-book because large-vehicle slots at the station are limited.

### Is there a waiting charge if my train is late?
We include 45 minutes of complimentary waiting from actual arrival. We track PNR/train status to align timing.

### Is late-night pickup at Cantt safe?
Cars are GPS-tracked, drivers are verified, and dispatch stays on-call; share the live link for solo travel.
