## How our station pickup works
- **Book in advance:** Share your **PNR or train number** via WhatsApp/call so dispatch tracks live status.
- **Driver details ahead of time:** Receive driver name, phone, car number, and color **30 minutes before arrival**.
- **The meeting point:** We guide you to the circulating area/"Parao" lane on the Cantt side; if needed, we use the Platform 8 gate for elderly passengers.

## Avoiding taxi scams at Cantt Railway Station
- **“Rickshaw relay”:** Autos move you 1 km then hand you off again, inflating total cost. We provide one private, end-to-end car.
- **“Fake guide”:** Drivers posing as guides push costly boat rides or silk shops. Our drivers skip detours unless you ask.
- **Direct station pickup:** We guide you to the circulating area so you avoid tout-heavy exits and keep luggage close.

## Services available at Cantt Station
- **Station to Temple drop:** Quick drop to Godowlia/Maidagin for darshan.
- **Station to Sarnath:** Direct tour starting at the station with waiting included.
- **Late-night arrivals:** Safe, GPS-tracked cars for 2 AM or 3 AM trains; shareable live ride link.
- **Luggage help:** Porter support arranged on request; driver assists at the cab lane.
