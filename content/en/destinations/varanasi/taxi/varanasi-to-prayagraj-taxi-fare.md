## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Varanasi to Prayagraj taxi / cab fare?

The Varanasi to Prayagraj fare is about ₹2,388 one-way and ₹3,275 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹4,625 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹6,750 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Varanasi to Prayagraj fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. Typical car toll is about ₹230 one-way at the Lalanagar plaza on the Varanasi–Prayagraj highway — already covered in your fixed fare. There is no separate state tax on this route.

### Is the Varanasi to Prayagraj fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Varanasi to Prayagraj trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹4,625 round-trip.

### What is the Varanasi to Prayagraj distance and travel time?

Varanasi to Prayagraj is about 120-125 km. Plan on 2.5-3 hr in normal traffic, or up to 3+ hr (Kumbh/festival) when the road is busy.

### Do you provide AC cars for Varanasi to Prayagraj?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Varanasi to Prayagraj one-way drops?

Yes, a Varanasi to Prayagraj one-way drop is available (Dzire from ₹2,388). One-way is the cheaper option when you don't need the car to return.
