## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Bodhgaya to Varanasi taxi / cab fare?

The Bodhgaya to Varanasi fare is about ₹5,004 one-way and ₹6,864 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹9,694 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹14,148 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Bodhgaya to Varanasi fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. The NH-19 plazas (Daffi, Mohania, Sasaram, Aurangabad, Dobhi) add up to about ₹400–500 in car tolls one-way. Bihar entry + green tax for a car is ~₹200–250 at the border, taking total toll + tax to roughly ₹600–750 one-way.

### Is the Bodhgaya to Varanasi fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Bodhgaya to Varanasi trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹9,694 round-trip.

### Is the Bodhgaya to Varanasi fare per car or per person?

Per car. The fare covers the whole vehicle — fuel, tolls and the driver — however many of you travel, up to the seat limit. A Dzire seats 4, an Ertiga 6 and an Innova 7, so a family sharing one car pays once, not per head.

### Do you provide AC cars for Bodhgaya to Varanasi?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Bodhgaya to Varanasi one-way drops?

Yes, a Bodhgaya to Varanasi one-way drop is available (Dzire from ₹5,004). One-way is the cheaper option when you don't need the car to return.

### How do I book a Bodhgaya to Varanasi taxi?

### Where do you pick up in Bodhgaya?

We pick up from any address you give — home, hotel, railway station or airport. If your train or flight is delayed, the driver waits with a name-board at no extra charge.
