## Full-Day Varanasi City Tour Cab (8 Hours) - ₹2500-3500

### Complete Varanasi Kashi Darshan Itinerary:

**6:00 AM - Sunrise at Dashashwamedh Ghat**
Start your day witnessing the spiritual morning prayers and rituals at Dashashwamedh Ghat. See sadhus meditating, locals bathing in the Ganges, and the golden light illuminating the sacred river.

**7:00 AM - Breakfast Break**
Enjoy authentic Varanasi breakfast at a local café: jalebi, poori, lassi (local favorites)

**8:00 AM - Kashi Vishwanath Temple**
Visit the holiest of all temples dedicated to Lord Shiva. Our driver will help you navigate the temple and explain the religious significance.

**10:00 AM - Annapurna Temple & Ancient Market**
Explore the 18th-century Annapurna Temple and wander through narrow lanes of Varanasi's old market (Thatheri Bazaar). Shop for brass items, wooden toys, and souvenirs.

**12:00 PM - Lunch**
Lunch at a recommended local restaurant or your choice of cuisine.

**1:30 PM - Assi Ghat & Pilgrimage to Vir Mahakal**
Visit Assi Ghat, where the Assi River meets the Ganges. Climb the 1008 steps of the Vir Mahakal Temple for panoramic city views.

**3:00 PM - Manikarnika Ghat**
Understand the spiritual significance of Varanasi's most sacred ghat where cremations take place (respectfully observed from outside).

**4:00 PM - Sarnath (Optional Add-on: +45 min)**
Visit Sarnath, where Buddha gave his first sermon. See the ancient Dhamekh Stupa and Buddhist monastery (12 km away).

**5:30 PM - Evening Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat**
Witness the mesmerizing evening prayer ceremony with lamps, incense, and sacred chants.

**7:30 PM - Drop at Hotel**
Comfortable return to your accommodation
