### On Driver Tipping

Plan to tip ₹200-400 depending on service quality. This isn't hidden—it's standard in India. Drivers make ₹200-300/day salary. Tips matter.

**Good service warrants tip:** Driver was on time, courteous, knew good rest stops, didn't rush.

### On AC Expectations

AC in Indian taxis provides 20-22°C temperature. Don't expect arctic-level cold.

If AC isn't working at pickup, **refuse the vehicle and ask for replacement.** Non-negotiable for a 5-hour journey.

### On Luggage

**Bring minimal luggage.** You're going for 1-3 days, not relocating.

1 large suitcase + 1 carry-on per person = comfortable.

### On Arrival Time

We quote "5 hours 40 minutes" for Varanasi to Gaya.
