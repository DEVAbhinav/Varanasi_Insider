## FAQs about Varanasi Airport to Jalalpur taxis

### Is Jalalpur a good candidate for shared cab bookings?
Because the area is spread across multiple villages, shared cabs can become complicated. A private taxi gives more control over route and drop time.

### Can you arrange late-night pickups to Jalalpur?
Yes, 24/7 cabs are available, but pre-booking is strongly advised so the driver is briefed on your precise village location.
