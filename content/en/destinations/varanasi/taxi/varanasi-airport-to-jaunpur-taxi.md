## Places to visit in Jaunpur after you arrive

If you have a few spare hours in Jaunpur, these heritage and religious stops fit easily into a short itinerary:

- **Jaunpur Fort (Shahi Qila):** A 14th-century fort overlooking the Gomti river—great views and an easy walk.
- **Shahi Bridge:** A 16th-century Mughal-era bridge about 654 feet long, known for its stone arches.
- **Atala Masjid:** 14th-century mosque with impressive Sharqi architecture.
- **Jama Masjid:** Grand mosque blending Sharqi and Islamic styles.
- **Jhinjhari Masjid:** Historic mosque located on the banks of the Gomti.
- **Lal Darwaza Masjid:** Another 15th-century gem with unique local history.
- **Sheetla Chaukiya Dham:** Popular Hindu temple frequented by local devotees.

## When is the best time to travel from VNS to Jaunpur?

- **Morning flights (before 10 AM):** Smoothest highway run and easier check-in at Jaunpur hotels or homes.
- **Afternoon flights:** Slightly busier markets on the Jaunpur side; leave a 15–20 minute buffer.
- **Night transfers:** Safer to take a pre-booked taxi with a known operator and avoid long halts on the highway.

## FAQs about Varanasi Airport to Jaunpur taxis

### Is the road from Varanasi Airport to Jaunpur safe at night?
The main highway is used regularly by buses and taxis. As with any intercity run, book a commercial yellow-plate cab, share your live location with family, and avoid unnecessary roadside stops.

### Can I get a round-trip package for Jaunpur?
Yes, if you plan to return to Varanasi the same day, ask for a fixed-km round-trip plan. It often works cheaper than two separate one-way bookings.

### Are there buses between VNS and Jaunpur?
Buses run between Varanasi and Jaunpur city, but they rarely start directly from the airport. You would first have to reach a city bus stand, which adds time and luggage handling.
