## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Lucknow to Varanasi taxi / cab fare?

The Lucknow to Varanasi fare is about ₹5,883 one-way and ₹8,070 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹11,396 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹16,632 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Lucknow to Varanasi fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. Most of the journey runs on the Purvanchal Expressway; a car pays roughly ₹400–650 in tolls one-way depending on where you join it (the full 340-km length is about ₹675). There is no separate state tax on this route.

### Is the Lucknow to Varanasi fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Lucknow to Varanasi trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹11,396 round-trip.

### What is the Lucknow to Varanasi distance and travel time?

Lucknow to Varanasi is about 300-316 km. Plan on 5-6 hr in normal traffic, or up to 6-7 hr when the road is busy.

### Do you provide AC cars for Lucknow to Varanasi?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Lucknow to Varanasi one-way drops?

### How do I book a Lucknow to Varanasi taxi?

### Where do you pick up in Lucknow?
