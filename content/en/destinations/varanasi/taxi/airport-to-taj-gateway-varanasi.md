## Safety Tips for Airport Transfers to Taj Gateway Varanasi

### Before You Leave Airport

- **Ignore Touts:** Outside arrivals, people will approach offering "cheap taxi" – ignore them
- **Use Official Taxi Booth:** If not pre-booked, use airport's official taxi counter
- **Keep Phone Charged:** Ensure your phone has Indian SIM or roaming activated
- **Share Details:** Message someone the car number and driver name

### During the Journey to Taj Gateway

- **Verify Driver:** Ask for ID; compare name with booking confirmation
- **Confirm Destination:** Say "Taj Gateway, Assi Ghat" clearly
- **Check Route:** Google Maps your route if concerned
- **Keep Valuables:** Keep passport and money in hotel bag, not visible
- **Trust Your Instinct:** If something feels wrong, ask to stop at police station

### Communication Essentials

**Phrases to Know:**
- "Taj Gateway" (pronunciation: "Taj Gateway")
- "Ganga Ghat" (river area)
- "How much?" → "Kitna?"
- "Too expensive" → "Bahut Mehnga"

## What to Expect on Your Route: VNS Airport to Taj Gateway

### Segment 1: Airport → Daraganj Road (10 km, 20 mins)

- Modern highway with good roads
- **View:** IT parks, industrial areas
- **Traffic:** Usually light

### Segment 2: Daraganj Road → Cantt Area (8 km, 15 mins)

- Mixed commercial and residential
- **View:** Local markets, schools, shops
- **Traffic:** Medium (higher during school hours)

### Segment 3: Cantt Road → Assi Ghat / Taj Gateway (7 km, 15-25 mins)

- Enters Varanasi's core tourist area
- Narrow lanes, local traffic mix
- **View:** Temples, shops, narrow galis
- **Traffic:** High (especially midday)

**Note:** Final 2 km involves narrow lanes; small vehicle easier

### Arrival at Taj Gateway:

- Security checkpoint at hotel gate
- Valet takes vehicle to parking
- Check-in desk 50 meters from drop-off
