## Best Varanasi Airport to Gorakhpur route

From VNS, cabs generally:

1. Exit toward highway corridors and join **NH-31**.
2. Follow NH-31 north-east toward Gorakhpur.

This corridor blends high-speed segments with town approaches. Fog in winter and heavy rain in monsoon can slow parts of the ride—plan a wider time buffer in these months.

## Varanasi Airport to Gorakhpur taxi fare (2026)

- **Hatchback:** ₹2,280–₹2,464, suited to 2–3 travellers.
- **Sedan:** ₹3,500–₹3,653 one way; around **₹7,000** for many round-trip quotes.
- **Innova:** ₹3,517–₹3,894 for families and small groups.
- **Extra km charge:** Around **₹17/km** if you do extra city drives or side trips.

Before confirming your booking, ask for a written fare breakup via WhatsApp so there is no confusion at the end of the journey.

**Popular drop spots in Gorakhpur:**
- **Gorakhnath Temple** (around 4 km from railway station).
- **Railway station and bus depots.**
- City hotels, colonies, and business districts.

## Places to visit in Gorakhpur after you arrive

- **Gorakhnath Temple:** Major pilgrimage centre dedicated to Guru Gorakhnath.
- **Vishnu Temple:** 12th-century temple from the Pal Dynasty period.
- **Geeta Vatika:** Devotional spot where 24/7 kirtan for Goddess Radha has run since 1968.
- **Gita Press:** Famous spiritual publishing house.
- **Railway Museum:** Showcases regional railway heritage.
- **Ramgarh Tal:** Scenic lake for evening walks and boating.
