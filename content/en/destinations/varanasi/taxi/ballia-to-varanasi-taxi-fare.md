## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Ballia to Varanasi taxi / cab fare?

The Ballia to Varanasi fare is about ₹2,731 one-way and ₹3,747 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹5,291 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹7,722 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Ballia to Varanasi fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. Cars pay about ₹285 one-way (Kaithi ~₹165 + Kyampur ~₹120); a Purvanchal Expressway variant adds a Kasimabad/Mohammadabad plaza (~₹70) for ~₹355. There is no separate state tax on this route.

### Is the Ballia to Varanasi fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Ballia to Varanasi trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹5,291 round-trip.

### Is the Ballia to Varanasi fare per car or per person?

Per car. The fare covers the whole vehicle — fuel, tolls and the driver — however many of you travel, up to the seat limit. A Dzire seats 4, an Ertiga 6 and an Innova 7, so a family sharing one car pays once, not per head.

### Do you provide AC cars for Ballia to Varanasi?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Ballia to Varanasi one-way drops?

Yes, a Ballia to Varanasi one-way drop is available (Dzire from ₹2,731). One-way is the cheaper option when you don't need the car to return.

### How do I book a Ballia to Varanasi taxi?

### Where do you pick up in Ballia?

We pick up from any address you give — home, hotel, railway station or airport. If your train or flight is delayed, the driver waits with a name-board at no extra charge.
