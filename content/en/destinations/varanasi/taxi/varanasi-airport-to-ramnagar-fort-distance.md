## Best Varanasi Airport to Ramnagar Fort route
Exit the airport on Ring Road toward NH31, continue past the Babatpur toll, and follow the signage for Ramnagar bridge. Cross the Ganga and turn right to reach the fort entrance plaza. During evening office hours, the bridge narrows to a single moving lane—drivers then detour via the new Balua ghat link road, adding roughly eight minutes but avoiding gridlock.

## Varanasi Airport to Ramnagar Fort FAQs

### How far is Ramnagar Fort from VNS airport?
It is 35–36 km away, and the drive takes 55–70 minutes depending on bridge traffic.

### How long does the taxi take during evening hours?
Bridge congestion between 5–7 PM can add 10 minutes; start earlier if you are catching the Ramnagar Ramlila.

### Are Ola or Uber reliable for the fort visit?
They operate but often surge for the return trip after the museum closes. A reserved cab fixes pricing and waits for you during the tour.

### What is the cheapest way to reach Ramnagar Fort?
City buses from Lanka take nearly 90 minutes and still require a rickshaw for the last kilometre. A ₹950 sedan from the airport remains the most efficient option.

### When should I land to explore the museum the same day?
Since the ticket window shuts at 4:30 PM, plan to land before 2 PM. That allows immigration, a 60-minute drive, and time to browse the armoury displays. Dive deeper into festivities with our [Ramnagar Ramlila guide](/en/ramnagar-ramlila-moving-stages-guide).
