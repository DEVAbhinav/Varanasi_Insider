# Varanasi Airport to Patna: Distance, Time, Taxi Fare (2026)

**The distance from Varanasi Airport (VNS) to Patna is approximately 253–280 km via NH-2 and NH-30, and the drive usually takes 5–6.5 hours.**

| Time | 5–6.5 hours |

## Best Varanasi Airport to Patna route

From VNS, cabs typically:

1. Join **NH-2**, heading east out of Varanasi.
2. Continue on highway corridors toward **NH-30**, aligning for Patna.

The corridor passes through stretches near **Arrah–Mohania road** and then into Patna via its outer ring and bridges. Traffic can slow near major junctions and the approach to **Mahatma Gandhi Setu**.

## Varanasi Airport to Patna taxi fare (2026)

- **Hatchback:** About **₹3,072–₹4,950** depending on operator and whether tolls are included.
- **Sedan:** **₹4,603–₹5,059** one way; round trips quoted around **₹10,118** and up.
- **Innova/SUV:** **₹6,590–₹7,674**, better for groups and comfort.
- **Extra km charge:** Around **₹14–₹15/km** if you detour for multiple town halts.

Confirm in advance whether driver allowance, tolls, and state taxes are bundled into the quote.

## Pickup & drop points in Patna

**Pickup at VNS:**
- Airport arrivals terminal at Babatpur with dedicated taxi zone.
- Driver meets you with a placard and helps with luggage.

**Common Patna drop zones:**
- **Mahatma Gandhi Setu approach** and nearby hotels.
- **Railway station and bus stands**.
- Residential areas such as **Kankarbagh, Rajendra Nagar, Boring Road, Danapur**.

## Places to visit in Patna after you arrive

- **Mahatma Gandhi Setu:** One of India’s longest river bridges across the Ganga.
- **Golghar:** Historic 29 m high granary built in 1786 with views over the city.
- **Takht Sri Patna Sahib:** Major Sikh pilgrimage site.
- **Patna Museum:** Collection of regional historical artifacts.
- **Gandhi Maidan:** Large historic ground used for events and fairs.
- **Patna Fort and older quarters** for history enthusiasts.

## FAQs about Varanasi Airport to Patna taxis

### Is an overnight journey recommended?
Given the 5–6.5 hour run, many travellers choose a **daytime** drive for better visibility. If you must travel at night, use a trusted operator and keep rest breaks to well-lit dhabas.

### Can I split the journey with a halt?
Yes, you can break at towns along NH-2/NH-30 for meals or an overnight stay. Discuss this at booking time so the fare and driver allowance reflect the plan.

### Are Ola/Uber available for this route?
App cabs typically cover only intra-city rides. For an intercity leg to Patna, pre-booking an outstation taxi is more reliable.
