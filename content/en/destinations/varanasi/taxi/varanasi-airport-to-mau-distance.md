## Best Varanasi Airport to Mau route

From the airport, taxis typically:

1. Head out toward the main highway and join **NH-31**.
2. Follow NH-31 all the way toward Mau, using bypasses wherever possible.

Highway conditions are generally good, with a few slower town stretches and toll booths along the way. Night transfers are common; still, plan one or two safe dhaba halts rather than frequent random stops.

## FAQs about Varanasi Airport to Mau taxis

### Is this route suitable for overnight journeys?
Yes, but if you are landing late, consider booking a hotel either in Mau or mid-way and avoid very late-night dhaba halts with family.

### Can I use a tempo traveller for groups?
For large families or groups, ask for a 12–17 seater tempo traveller. Pricing depends on exact group size and stay duration; expect higher per-km than Innovas but better per-person value.

### Are buses a good alternative?
Buses run between Varanasi and Mau, but they start from city bus stands, not from the airport. With luggage and kids, a direct taxi is usually simpler.
