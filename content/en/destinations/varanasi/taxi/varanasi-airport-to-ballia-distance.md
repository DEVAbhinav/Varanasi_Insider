## Best Varanasi Airport to Ballia route

From VNS, cabs typically:

1. Exit toward the highway and join **NH-31**.
2. Follow NH-31 past Ghazipur and other towns toward Ballia.

The highway hugs the general Ganga corridor and includes a mix of four-lane and two-lane stretches. Allow extra time during monsoon or major temple festivals.

## Places to visit in Ballia and nearby

- **Bhrigu Temple & Ashram:** Key pilgrimage site associated with Maharishi Bhrigu.
- **Baleshwar Shiva Temple:** Well-known Shiva temple in the main market.
- **Sonadih Bhavani Temple:** Ancient temple dedicated to Goddess Durga.
- **Shankari Devi Temple:** 500-year-old temple with notable Navratri celebrations.
- **Brahmani Devi Temple (Brahmaine village):** Temple linked to the legend of demon Banasura.
- **Surha Tal Bird Sanctuary:** Wetland and birdwatching hotspot near Ballia.
