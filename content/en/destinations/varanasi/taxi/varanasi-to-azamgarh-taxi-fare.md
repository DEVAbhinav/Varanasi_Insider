## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Varanasi to Azamgarh taxi / cab fare?

The Varanasi to Azamgarh fare is about ₹1,967 one-way and ₹2,699 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹3,811 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹5,562 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Varanasi to Azamgarh fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. Expect at least the Hauj Fee Plaza on NH-31 (~₹125 for a car), plus any local gate depending on the exact route. There is no separate state tax on this route.

### Is the Varanasi to Azamgarh fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Varanasi to Azamgarh trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹3,811 round-trip.

### Is the Varanasi to Azamgarh fare per car or per person?

Per car. The fare covers the whole vehicle — fuel, tolls and the driver — however many of you travel, up to the seat limit. A Dzire seats 4, an Ertiga 6 and an Innova 7, so a family sharing one car pays once, not per head.

### Do you provide AC cars for Varanasi to Azamgarh?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Varanasi to Azamgarh one-way drops?

Yes, a Varanasi to Azamgarh one-way drop is available (Dzire from ₹1,967). One-way is the cheaper option when you don't need the car to return.
