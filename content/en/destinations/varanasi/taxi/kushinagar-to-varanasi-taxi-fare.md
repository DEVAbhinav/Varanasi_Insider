## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Kushinagar to Varanasi taxi / cab fare?

The Kushinagar to Varanasi fare is about ₹6,303 one-way and ₹8,646 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹12,210 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹17,820 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Kushinagar to Varanasi fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. Roughly ₹350–500 in car tolls one-way — the Varanasi–Gorakhpur plazas (Kaithi, Kyampur, Bhitaha) plus smaller or no tolls on the Gorakhpur–Kushinagar leg. There is no separate state tax on this route.

### Is the Kushinagar to Varanasi fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Kushinagar to Varanasi trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹12,210 round-trip.

### Is the Kushinagar to Varanasi fare per car or per person?

Per car. The fare covers the whole vehicle — fuel, tolls and the driver — however many of you travel, up to the seat limit. A Dzire seats 4, an Ertiga 6 and an Innova 7, so a family sharing one car pays once, not per head.

### Do you provide AC cars for Kushinagar to Varanasi?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Kushinagar to Varanasi one-way drops?
