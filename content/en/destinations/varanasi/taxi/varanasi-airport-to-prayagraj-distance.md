## Best Varanasi Airport to Prayagraj route
From Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport, cabs join **NH-19** toward Handia. This four-lane highway runs parallel to the Ganga and is the fastest corridor into Prayagraj. Near the outskirts, your driver will choose one of two entries:

- **Naini bridge approach** for Triveni Sangam, Arail, and Old City ghats.
- **Civil Lines approach** for hotels, railway station, and cantonment area.

During Kumbh or Magh Mela, police diversions may send vehicles via alternate bridges. Build a 30-minute cushion into your plan in peak festival season.

## Places to visit after reaching Prayagraj

If you have half a day or more in the city, build a simple circuit around these highlights:

- **Triveni Sangam:** Sacred confluence of the Ganga, Yamuna, and the mythical Saraswati. Take a boat ride and, if time allows, attend a short Ganga aarti.
- **Allahabad Fort (view from outside):** Mughal-era fort built by Emperor Akbar at the riverfront.
- **Akshay Vat:** The legendary "immortal" banyan tree inside the fort complex, accessible on specific routes.
- **Hanuman Mandir (Bade Hanuman Ji):** Famous riverside temple with a reclining Hanuman idol.
- **Khusro Bagh:** Historical garden complex with Mughal tombs and shaded walkways.
- **Anand Bhavan:** Ancestral home of the Nehru family, now a museum.

Plan temple visits early in the morning or post-lunch to avoid heavy queues at peak aarti times.

## When should you start from Varanasi Airport?

- **Morning landings (before 11 AM):** You can comfortably reach Prayagraj by early afternoon and fit in Sangam + 1–2 sights.
- **Afternoon landings (1–4 PM):** Prioritise check-in and either an evening aarti at Sangam or a quick visit to Hanuman Mandir.
- **Late-night landings:** Safer to head straight to your hotel in Prayagraj and keep sightseeing for the next morning.

Factor in fog delays in December–January; highway visibility can occasionally drop, stretching the drive to 3.5 hours.

## FAQs about Varanasi Airport to Prayagraj taxis

### Is one-way taxi cheaper than round trip?
For single-direction travel, a one-way cab is cheaper than paying for a vehicle to idle. If you plan to return to Varanasi the same or next day, a round-trip package with a fixed km cap often works out better.

### Are Ola or Uber available for VNS to Prayagraj?
App cabs are mostly limited to intra-city travel around Varanasi. For an intercity leg like Prayagraj, pre-book an outstation taxi so the fare, driver allowance, and night halt rules are clear.

### Can I stop for food or tea on the way?
Yes, most drivers allow one or two short halts on NH-19 for tea, snacks, or washroom breaks. Mention this in advance so they pick appropriate dhabas.

### Is the route safe for solo travellers or families?
NH-19 is a busy national highway used by buses and outstation taxis round the clock. Choose a commercial yellow-plate vehicle, share trip details with family, and avoid unnecessary roadside stops late at night.
