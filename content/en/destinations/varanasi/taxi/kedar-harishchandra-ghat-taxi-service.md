## FAQs about Kedar & Harishchandra taxi service

### What is the difference between Kedar, Harishchandra, and Manikarnika?
Kedar is a Shiva temple ghat; Harishchandra is a quieter cremation ghat; Manikarnika is the main cremation ghat with heavy crowds.

### Will people here understand South Indian languages?
Yes. Many shopkeepers and priests speak Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, and Malayalam. Our drivers help connect you.

### Is it respectful to observe cremations at Harishchandra?
Yes if you stand aside, stay quiet, dress modestly, and never photograph.

### Do you charge more for pilgrims?
No. Rates are fixed with no pilgrim markup.

### Is this area good for multi-day spiritual stays?
Yes. We offer retreat packages with daily car service for ghats, temples, and ashrams, discounted for 3+ day stays.
