## Frequently asked questions

### How much is the Varanasi to Delhi taxi / cab fare?

The Varanasi to Delhi fare is about ₹16,407 one-way and ₹22,506 round-trip in an AC Swift Dzire. An Innova is from ₹31,783 round-trip and a 12-seater Tempo Traveller from ₹46,386 round-trip. The fare is fixed and already includes fuel, tolls and the driver.

### Is toll included in the Varanasi to Delhi fare?

Yes — our quote includes an estimate for highway tolls. The classic GT Road → Kanpur → Agra → Yamuna Expressway route has ~11 car plazas totalling about ₹1,500 one-way; a newer all-expressway routing is quicker but tolls run higher (~₹2,200). Either way, tolls are already covered in your fixed fare. There is no separate state tax on this route.

### Is the Varanasi to Delhi fare fixed or metered?

You get the full fixed fare in writing before you book — no meter, no surge pricing and no negotiation on the road.

### Which car is best for a family of 5 on the Varanasi to Delhi trip?

For 5-6 people an Ertiga (6-seat) or Innova (7-seat) is ideal — comfortable with luggage. On this route an Innova is from ₹31,783 round-trip.

### What is the Varanasi to Delhi distance and travel time?

Varanasi to Delhi is about ~859 km. Plan on 12.5-13 hr in normal traffic, or up to 14+ hr when the road is busy.

### Do you provide AC cars for Varanasi to Delhi?

Yes, the entire fleet is air-conditioned — Dzire, Ertiga, Innova, Innova Crysta, 12/17/26-seater Tempo Travellers and the Force Urbania.

### Do you offer Varanasi to Delhi one-way drops?

Yes, a Varanasi to Delhi one-way drop is available (Dzire from ₹16,407). One-way is the cheaper option when you don't need the car to return.
