# Varanasi Airport to Deoria: Distance, Time, Taxi Fare (2025)

**The distance from Varanasi Airport (VNS) to Deoria is 193–212 km via NH-28 or NH-31 and the drive generally takes 4.5–6 hours.**

## Best Varanasi Airport to Deoria route

From VNS, drivers typically choose between:

- **NH-28:** The shorter alignment, often preferred when traffic is smooth.
- **NH-31 via Ghazipur–Rasra:** An alternate path that can be useful when one corridor is congested.

Both routes mix highway stretches with town bypasses and some two-lane segments, so realistic planning is important.

## Varanasi Airport to Deoria taxi fare (2025)

- **Hatchback:** Around **₹3,769**, suitable for 2–3 passengers.
- **Sedan:** **₹3,769–₹3,999** one way, roughly **₹7,998** for quoted round trips.
- **Innova:** **₹3,810–₹4,188**, better for joint families or groups with luggage.
- **Extra km:** **₹13–₹13.5/km** for diversions and extra city running.

Before confirming, ask for clear inclusions (toll, state tax, parking, driver allowance) in a WhatsApp message.

## Places to visit in Deoria after you arrive

- **Tirupati Balaji Temple:** South Indian-style temple located on Kasia Road.
- **Devaraha Baba Ashram:** Spiritual ashram on the Saryu riverbanks.
- **Dugdheshwarnath Temple:** Ancient Shiva pilgrimage spot.
- **Mahendranath Temple:** Another prominent Shiva temple.
- **Devarahi Mata Temple:** Old temple linked to Goddess Sati legends.
- **Just Chill Water Park:** Family-friendly water park on Baikunthpur Road.
