## Why a 2-Day Plan Works Best

Deoghar is a serious outstation route (~920 km round trip). A same-day attempt means almost the entire day in the car. The comfortable pilgrimage plan:

- **Day 1:** Depart Varanasi early, drive the GT Road via Sasaram and Dhanbad, reach Deoghar by evening, and rest.
- **Day 2:** Early **Baba Baidyanath darshan** (queues build fast), then Naulakha Mandir and a short trip to Trikut Pahar before returning to Varanasi.

We quote this as a **2-day return (1 night)** so the darshan, not the driving, is the focus.

## What to See in Deoghar

1. **Baba Baidyanath Temple** - the Jyotirlinga; the main shrine connects to the Parvati temple by a sacred thread (a unique feature symbolising Shiva-Shakti union).
2. **Naulakha Mandir** - a striking 146-foot temple modelled on Belur Math.
3. **Trikut Pahar (Trikutachal)** - a three-peaked hill with a ropeway, ~10 km away.
4. **Tapovan Caves** - meditation caves linked to sage Valmiki.
5. **Basukinath** - the "court of Baba", ~45 km away, often visited in the same circuit.

## Best Time to Travel

**October to March** is most comfortable. **Shravan (July-August)** is the grand kanwar season - the town overflows with kanwariyas and road traffic peaks, so plan carefully and start very early. **Mahashivratri** is another major occasion.
