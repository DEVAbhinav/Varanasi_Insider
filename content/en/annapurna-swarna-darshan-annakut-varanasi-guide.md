# Maa Annapurna Swarna Roop Darshan & Annakut 2026: Dates, Khazana, Queue Route & Cab Guide

> **Verified 2026 Festival Calendar:** The golden sanctum of Shri Kashi Annapurna Mandir opens on **Friday, 6 November 2026 (Dhanteras)** at 04:00 AM and closes at midnight on **Wednesday, 11 November 2026 (Bhai Dooj)**. The grand **Annakut Mahotsav (56 Bhog & Laddoo Parvat)** takes place on **Tuesday, 10 November 2026**. This once-a-year darshan occurs only across these five sacred days.

While millions visit Varanasi every year to offer Ganga jal at Kashi Vishwanath, autumn brings a sacred window that draws the most devoted pilgrims across India: the **Swarna Roop Darshan (स्वर्णमयी दर्शन) of Maa Annapurna**.

For **360 days a year**, the ancient, solid pure-gold idol of Goddess Annapurna—along with pure-gold murtis of Maa Lakshmi, Bhumi Devi, and Lord Shiva seeking alms—remains locked inside an iron vault on the first floor of the temple. 

Only once a year, starting on the pre-dawn auspiciousness of **Dhanteras** and ending on **Bhai Dooj**, the heavy vault doors unlock. Pilgrims queue for hours through Vishwanath Gali to behold the golden Mother, receive the legendary **Annapurna Khazana** (blessed coin and consecrated rice), and partake in the monumental **56 Bhog Annakut Mahaprasad**.

Yet for outstation families, navigating Diwali week in Varanasi is fraught with confusion. Police barricades seal the old city into a pedestrian fortress, touts sell fake coins outside the gates, and travelers often wait in the wrong temple queue. 

This guide delivers verifiable ground facts: the 2026 ritual calendar, exact queue routes, the truth about VIP passes, senior-citizen navigation, and fixed-fare perimeter cabs.

## The Spiritual Mystery: The Pure-Gold Idols of Annapurna Mandir

In Banaras theology (*Kashi Khanda*), Lord Shiva sustains the cosmos spiritually, but Maa Annapurna sustains the physical body so that seekers may attain salvation (*Moksha*).

When Lord Shiva, wandering as the cosmic mendicant (*Bhikshatana*), realized that spiritual liberation cannot be achieved by starving living beings, he approached Maa Annapurna in Kashi. Holding out his begging bowl (*kapala*), Shiva accepted food from her divine hands. The Mother granted him a boon: **in Kashi, no living being shall ever sleep with an empty stomach**.

### What Rests Inside the First-Floor Vault?
While the ground-floor brass and black stone shrine remains open daily, the first-floor **Swarna Mandir** houses priceless ancient heritage idols:
1. **Mata Annapurna (Pure Solid Gold):** Seated on an ornate throne, holding a golden ladle (*karchi*) in her right hand and a bowl of consecrated nectar-rice (*katora*) in her left.
2. **Bhagwan Shiva (Solid Gold):** Standing with his alms bowl extended toward the Goddess in a posture of humble reception.
3. **Maa Lakshmi & Bhumi Devi (Solid Gold):** Flanking Goddess Annapurna, symbolizing boundless material wealth and agricultural abundance.

## The Sacred "Annapurna Khazana": Facts, Rituals & Scam Warnings

Every devotee ascending to the Swarna Mandir receives a consecrated paper envelope known throughout India as the **Annapurna Khazana (अन्नपूर्णा खजाना)**.

### What Does the Envelope Contain?
1. **A Blessed Metal Coin:** Imbued with mantras and offered at the feet of the golden Goddess.
2. **Consecrated Unbroken Rice (Akshat):** Sacred raw grains blessed during the festive archana.

### How to Keep the Khazana at Home
Traditional belief holds that the Khazana acts as an inexhaustible reservoir of nourishment and wealth. Pilgrims take it back to their native cities and place it in:
- **Commercial Tijori or Cash Register:** Placed beside account books to bless businesses against insolvency.
- **Home Safes / Bank Lockers:** Preserved in red silk cloth alongside family assets.
- **Kitchen Grain Silos (Anna Bhandar):** Mixed with the household's rice container to ensure uninterrupted food security throughout the year.

> ⚠️ **Critical Scam Alert for Travelers:**  
> Outside the temple gates along Vishwanath Gali, Bansphatak, and Godowlia, street hawkers and unauthorized agents aggressively peddle small plastic packets claiming to be *"Original Annapurna Khazana Coins"* for ₹100 to ₹500. **Do not buy them.**  
> The genuine, sanctified Annapurna Khazana is **distributed completely FREE of charge** inside the first-floor sanctum by the temple priests.

## The Grand Annakut Day: 56 Bhog & The Mountain of Laddoos

On **Tuesday, 10 November 2026**, the temple celebrates its namesake festival: **Annakut (अन्नकूट)**, literally meaning a "mountain of food."

### The Offering
Starting in the early morning hours, the temple trust arrays an astounding culinary offering before the golden altar:
- **Chhappan Bhog (56 Dishes):** Dozens of varieties of regional sweets, dry fruits, seasonal fruits, savories, pickles, vegetable preparations, kheer, and halwa.
- **Laddoo Ka Parvat (The Laddoo Mountain):** Thousands of kilograms of pure-desi-ghee *besan* and *boondi* laddoos stacked into a pyramid resembling Mount Sumeru.

### Mahaprasad Distribution
Following the grand noon *Bhog Aarti* (12:00 PM – 01:00 PM), the Annakut prasad is opened for distribution. Devotees receive packets of blessed laddoos and sit down for the traditional temple *Bhandara* serving hot *Puri, Sabzi, and Kheer*.

## Queue Mechanics: Annapurna Mandir vs. Kashi Vishwanath Corridor

The single biggest mistake outstation pilgrims make during Diwali week is standing in the wrong queue.

### They Are Two Completely Separate Temples
- **Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple:** Governed by the government-appointed Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust (SKVT). Its primary entry gates are Gate 4 (Gyanvapi/Chowk), Gate 1 (Dhundi Raj/Ghats), and Gate 2 (Saraswati Phatak).
- **Shri Kashi Annapurna Mandir:** Governed by the autonomous **Mahant Shankar Puri Trust**. It is an independent historical temple located inside Vishwanath Gali, approximately 50 meters from Kashi Vishwanath's Gate 4.

### Where Does the Annapurna Queue Form?
During the five days of Swarna Darshan, the Annapurna line does **NOT** use the Kashi Vishwanath Corridor plazas. 
1. **The Primary Line:** Forms outside the lane mouth at **Bansphatak Crossing** (on the main road between Godowlia and Chowk).
2. **The Barricade Route:** Pilgrims are routed through pedestrian steel barricades down into Vishwanath Gali, moving directly toward the marble entrance arch of Annapurna Temple.
3. **Exit Route:** After descending from the first-floor sanctum, devotees exit via an alternate back lane leading out toward Gyanvapi or Saraswati Phatak.

### The Truth About VIP Passes & Sugam Darshan
- **Kashi Vishwanath Portal Passes Do NOT Work:** The online ₹300/₹500 "Sugam Darshan" ticket bought on `shrikashivishwanath.org` is strictly for Kashi Vishwanath Temple. It does **not** grant entry into Annapurna Swarna Mandir.
- **No Public Online VIP Booking:** The Annapurna Temple Trust does not run an online ticket counter. Beware of touts or hotel desks claiming: *"I will give you an Annapurna VIP Pass for ₹1,500."* Priority entry is restricted to official protocol holders approved directly by the temple administration.

## Senior Citizens & Physical Accessibility Reality

If you are planning to bring elderly parents or grandparents for Swarna Darshan, keep these ground realities in mind:

1. **The 1st-Floor Marble Staircase:**
   - The golden sanctum is located on the upper floor of the temple. Devotees must climb approximately **25 to 30 narrow, steep marble steps**.
   - There are **no elevators or mechanical lifts** inside this historic temple structure.
2. **Wheelchair Limitations:**
   - Wheelchairs can reach Godowlia or Bansphatak barricades via cycle-rickshaw or foldable chair, but **wheelchairs cannot enter Vishwanath Gali** due to the dense crowd density and stone thresholds.
3. **Actionable Senior Protocol:**
   - Schedule your visit strictly between **04:30 AM and 06:00 AM**.
   - Carry a lightweight folding cane with rubber grips for staircase stability.
   - Keep prescribed blood pressure and sugar medications in a small waist pouch with a water bottle.
   - Have an able-bodied family member walk directly behind seniors on the staircase to prevent pushing from surging queues.

## Security, Mobile Phones & Locker Facilities

Security inside Vishwanath Gali during Diwali week is enforced by Uttar Pradesh Police and Rapid Action Force (RAF).

### Banned Items Inside the Sanctum
- ❌ Mobile phones and digital cameras.
- ❌ Leather belts, leather wallets, and leather footwear.
- ❌ Large backpacks, suitcases, or metal objects.
- ❌ Matchboxes, lighters, and tobacco products.

### Safe Cloakroom & Locker Spots
- **Your Hotel Room (Safest Option):** Leave all phones, smartwatches, and excess cash locked inside your hotel safe. Carry only your government photo ID and loose cash for prasad offerings.
- **Kashi Vishwanath Tourist Facilitation Centre (Gate 4):** Government-operated electronic lockers available on a first-come, first-served basis (nominal charge of ₹20–₹50).
- **Benia Bagh Parking Cloakroom:** Available for travelers parking their vehicles at the multi-level facility.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the daily opening and closing hours of the Swarna Mandir?
During the five days (6–11 November 2026), the Swarna Mandir opens daily at **04:00 AM** following Mangala Aarti and remains open continuously until **11:30 PM** (except for a brief 30-minute curtain closure during the midday Rajbhog Aarti around 12:00 PM – 12:30 PM).

### Is there any dress code for Maa Annapurna Swarna Darshan?
While traditional Indian attire (dhoti-kurta or pyjama-kurta for men; saree, salwar-suit, or long ethnic dress for women) is strongly recommended, there is no mandatory dress code enforcement like the South Indian temples. However, western shorts, sleeveless tops, and short skirts are strictly discouraged.

### Can we bring prasad or bhog from home to offer to Mata Annapurna?
Due to the intense crowd volume during Swarna Darshan, priests do not accept elaborate homemade food vessels at the golden altar. Devotees may offer dry fruits, pre-packaged *pede*, *laddoos*, or unbroken coconuts purchased from verified halwais along Vishwanath Gali.

### How does this event connect with Dev Deepawali?
Maa Annapurna Swarna Roop Darshan takes place during Diwali week (6–11 November 2026). Exactly two weeks later on Kartik Purnima (**Tuesday, 24 November 2026**), the city celebrates [Dev Deepawali](/en/dev-deepawali-2026-varanasi-ultimate-guide). Many outstation pilgrims combine both sacred occasions into a multi-week spiritual stay in Kashi.
