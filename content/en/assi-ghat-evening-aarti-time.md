# Assi Ghat Evening Aarti Time – Sandhya Ceremony Guide

**Tonight’s slot:** 6:00 PM – 7:00 PM (winter). Reach by 5:35 PM to secure second-step seating.

## Crowd & Atmosphere
- 300-500 devotees in Dec-Feb; 150-250 in summer.
- Live classical vocals and brass lamp choreography.
- Smaller than Dashashwamedh but closer to action.

## Arrival Strategy
- Leave hotel 45 minutes before slot; taxis park near Bhadaini Bridge.
- Carry shawl, power bank, and light footwear.
- During festivals, arrive a full hour early and request reserved chairs for seniors.

## Boat Viewing Option
- Private rowboats anchor 30 meters out (₹400-600 per boat).
- Motorboats discouraged during monsoon due to swell.

## Photography Tips
- Shoot at ISO 3200, f/2.8, 1/80s once lamps are lit.
- Capture reflections by positioning near the right parapet.
