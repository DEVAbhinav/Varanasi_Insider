Deciding **where to stay in Varanasi** is the single choice that shapes your whole trip — how easily you reach the Ganga Aarti, how much time you lose in traffic, and how comfortable your parents and kids are at the end of a long day. This 2026 guide compares the main areas and accommodation types, with honest, practical advice for **families and groups**.

## Quick answer

- **First-time visitors and families:** stay **central (Sigra area)** — close to Cantt station, markets and hospitals, with the main ghats 2–3 km away and easy taxis in every direction.
- **Ghat-first travellers who pack light:** stay near **Assi Ghat or Dashashwamedh** to walk to the aarti.

## The main areas to stay in Varanasi

### Central Varanasi (Sigra / Shastri Nagar)
The practical choice for most travellers. Sigra is well connected, close to **Varanasi Cantt Railway Station**, the airport road, big markets (including the IP Sigra Mall), and hospitals. The main ghats and Kashi Vishwanath are a short 2–3 km drive. Roads are wide enough for cars and auto-rickshaws, which matters a lot when you have luggage or elderly family members. This is where our own homestay is based.

### The ghats (Assi, Dashashwamedh, Godowlia)
Staying steps from the river is magical if walking to the [Ganga Aarti](/en/assi-ghat-aarti-timings-2026) at dawn and dusk is your priority. The trade-off: the lanes are narrow and busy, vehicles cannot reach most doorsteps, and dragging suitcases through crowded galis is tiring — a real issue for older parents and young children.

### Near Cantt Railway Station
If you arrive late by train or leave early, being minutes from the station removes stress. Central Sigra gives you this benefit — our homestay is a **5-minute walk from Cantt station** — while still keeping you close to the sights.

### BHU / Lanka (south)
Quieter and greener, good if you have work at Banaras Hindu University or want a calmer base, but it is farther from the central ghats and station.

## Hotel vs homestay in Varanasi

| | Hotel rooms | Whole-flat homestay |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Couples, 1-night stays | Families & groups of 6–10 |
| Privacy | Shared corridors/lobby | Entire flat to yourselves |
| Meals | Restaurant / room service | Own **kitchen** — home-cooked / satvik / veg |
| Cost for 8–10 people | 4–5 rooms add up fast | One booking, lower per head |
| Space to gather | None | Living room to sit together |

For a **joint family, a group of friends, or wedding-guest overflow**, a whole flat almost always wins on comfort and cost. You stay together, cook simple meals for kids and elders, and pay less per person.

## Budgeting: think per head, not per room

A group of 10 in a hotel needs 4–5 rooms; a single 4BHK flat sleeps the same group in **4 bedrooms with 3 bathrooms** for one price. Divide by the number of guests and a whole-flat homestay is often the cheapest comfortable option in the city.

## Safety and comfort tips

- Choose a base with **vehicle access** so taxis can pick you up at the door — important for early aarti mornings and airport runs. See [how to reach Varanasi](/en/how-to-reach-varanasi).
- Confirm the property is on a **known, well-lit street** (a landmark like a mall or main road nearby helps drivers find you).
- Travelling with elders? A **lift or ground-floor access, clean bathrooms, and a kitchen** for medicines/meals make a big difference.
- Varanasi is generally safe for visitors; read our notes on [staying safe in Varanasi](/en/is-varanasi-safe-for-solo-female-travellers).
