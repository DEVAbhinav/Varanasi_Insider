### Key 1-Day Optimization Tips
- Carry offerings only for first darshan; buy additional after assessing queues.
- Do hill (Ashtabhuja) before lunch to avoid post-meal fatigue.
- Keep footwear ticket & receipt safe (frequent mix-ups on busy days).
- Pre-pack two 500ml water bottles + light dry fruits.

> Not required, but highly beneficial during Navratri or if including side excursions.

> **Can seniors do the full Trikon Parikrama in one day?**  
> Yes—use taxi/Tempo Traveller for all temple transfers + avoid mid-day heat.
