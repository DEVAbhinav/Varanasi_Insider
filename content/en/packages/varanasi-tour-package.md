## 2 Day, 3 Day and 4 Day Varanasi Tour Packages

### 2 Day Varanasi Tour Package

Best for a short first visit. The usual plan covers arrival, evening Ganga Aarti, a sunrise boat ride and the main Kashi darshan route. It is compact, so arrival and departure times need to be realistic.

### 3 Day Varanasi Tour Package

Best for most first-time families. The extra day leaves room for Sarnath and a few city temples without rushing every stop into one crowded day.

### 4 Day Varanasi Tour Package

Best when you want a slower pace, an elder-friendly schedule, shopping time or optional Ramnagar and heritage walks. It also gives flexibility if temple access or crowd controls change.

## Practical Planning Tips for Varanasi

### Hotel location

A hotel near the ghats can reduce some walking, but cars may not reach the door. Cantt and wider-road areas are easier for pickups and need planned transfers into the old city. The better choice depends on age, luggage, walking comfort and temple timing.

### Temple and old-city access

Cars cannot enter every lane or reach every ghat-side stay. We agree practical pickup and drop points before confirmation instead of promising door-to-door access where vehicles are restricted.

### Ganga Aarti and boat rides

Aarti timing, crowd controls and boat availability change with season and festival dates. Your quote will say whether the plan uses ghat viewing, a shared boat or a private boat, and what is included.

### Family and senior pacing

Tell us about elders and children before the itinerary is fixed. Rest windows, meal timing, hotel access and the number of temple stops matter more than packing every attraction into the day.
