## The 3-Day Kashi–Gaya Circuit, Day by Day

Gaya (in Bihar, ~5 hours from Varanasi) is considered the **ultimate destination for Pind Daan** — offering pind at the **Vishnupad Temple** and the sacred **Falgu river** is believed to grant *moksha* to ancestors. Many families deliberately pair the two cities:

- **Day 1 — Kashi:** dawn Tarpan and Shradh at Tulsi/Kedar Ghat; rest; optional Kashi Vishwanath darshan.
- **Day 2 — Gaya:** travel to Gaya; Pind Daan at Vishnupad and the Falgu ghats with a Gaya purohit.
- **Day 3 — Return / Prayagraj (optional):** return to Kashi, or extend to **Prayagraj** for Tarpan at the Triveni Sangam.

We handle the inter-city cab, the coordination between the Kashi and Gaya purohits, and stays at both ends.

**September 26 – October 10, 2026.** Book early — this is Kashi's busiest ritual fortnight and morning slots fill fast. Varshik (annual) Shradh can also be arranged year-round.

## Monsoon Preparedness (Jul–Sep)

- **No boat rides** — the Ganga is in flood and boats are suspended; rites are ghat-side/inland.
- **Footwear:** strapped, grippy sandals (not foam clogs) for wet stone.
- **Water:** carry a personal filter bottle (Sawyer-type).
- **Transport:** auto/e-rickshaw to bypass congestion and waterlogging.
