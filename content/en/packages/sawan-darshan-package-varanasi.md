Sawan is one of the hardest times to do Kashi Vishwanath well — huge crowds, monsoon flooding, barricaded lanes. This package is built around those exact problems, so your darshan is calm and your trip stays full even when the river is off-limits.

Everything you've heard about Sawan at Kashi — the six-hour queue, the heat, the crush, the apps that won't reach the temple — is real for people who arrive unplanned. It doesn't have to be your trip. Here's how each fear is already designed out of these packages:

- *"We'll lose hours in the general queue."* → An **official Sugam Darshan pass is built into every package** — no 4–6 hour wait.
- *"We can't even get to the temple."* → **Flood-safe transport** around the barricades, plus a short escorted last stretch where ride-apps fail.
- *"The trip's ruined if the ghats are flooded."* → A ready **inland day** (Sarnath, Ramnagar Fort, BHU) keeps it full.
- *"How will our parents cope?"* → **Wheelchair assistance and a paced, elder-first plan.**
- *"A driver will divert us to a silk shop."* → A **fixed-fare vetted driver** — temple first, no forced detours.

So relax: the crowd is our problem to manage. Your job is the darshan. The two options below are simply how much you'd like us to carry.

## The Inland Day (When the Ghats Flood)

Because boats are suspended and ghats submerged, the 2-day package pivots inland:
- **Sarnath** — where the Buddha gave his first sermon; calm, green, and largely covered/shaded, so it works even in drizzle.
- **Ramnagar Fort** — the Kashi Naresh's 18th-century riverside fort across the Ganga, atmospheric in monsoon.
- **BHU campus** — the sprawling, lush-green Banaras Hindu University grounds, including the marble **New Vishwanath Temple** (a calm, uncrowded alternative darshan).
- **Saray Mohana weavers' village** (optional) — see Banarasi silk woven at the source, away from commission-shop traps.

All reached by reliable auto/e-rickshaw, bypassing the congested riverfront and the junctions (like Godowlia) that gridlock during Sawan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's included in the Sawan darshan package?**
Every Sawan package builds in an official Sugam Darshan pass for Kashi Vishwanath, flood-safe auto/e-rickshaw transport around barricades, curated local food stops, and elder-friendly help. The 2-day option adds an inland sightseeing day (Sarnath, Ramnagar Fort, BHU) and a hotel stay.

**How much does the Sawan darshan package cost?**
A combined 1-day package with darshan, hotel and cab starts from ₹11,000. Any specific pooja (such as a Rudrabhishek) is quoted separately in the ₹5,000–₹11,999 range. The 2-day package is priced by group size and vehicle; we confirm everything in writing.

**Is the Sugam Darshan pass really included in every package?**
Yes. Because Sawan Somwar queues can reach four to six hours, we treat the official Sugam Darshan pass as essential rather than optional. It's built into every Sawan package and avoids ₹1,000+ tout markups.

**Why does the package include an inland sightseeing day?**
In July–August the Ganga floods, boats are suspended and the ghats submerge, so a river-only plan falls apart. The 2-day package pivots to an inland day — Sarnath, Ramnagar Fort and the BHU campus — reached by reliable auto/e-rickshaw.

**Is the package suitable for elderly family members?**
Yes. We prioritise elder-friendly logistics: the Sugam pass to skip the queue, wheelchair assistance on request, drop-offs as close as barricades allow, and rest breaks. Tell us who's travelling and we plan the pacing.

**Are boat rides part of the Sawan package?**
No — during Sawan the river is in flood and boats are suspended, so the package is inland and darshan-focused, and the Aarti is viewed landward. We can add a boat ride once the river is safe post-monsoon.
