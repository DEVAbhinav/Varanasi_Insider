You bring the faith and the fruit-vow. We hold everything else steady. The options below are simply how much you'd like us to carry.

## How to Book in 1-2-3

1. **WhatsApp us** your date, number of people, and whether elderly members are joining.
2. We confirm the **priest, timing and a written quote** with any add-ons.
3. Arrive at dawn; we handle queue, sankalp, transport and waiting — you focus on the ritual.

## Monsoon Preparedness (Late August)

- **No boat rides** — Ganga in flood; plan is ghat-side/inland.
- **Footwear:** strapped, grippy sandals for the 35 wet steps (no foam clogs).
- **Water:** carry a personal filter bottle (Sawyer-type).
- **Transport:** auto/e-rickshaw to bypass waterlogging.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What does the Lolark Shashti package include?**
The core half-day service includes queue navigation at Lolark Kund, a priest for the sankalp and offering, flood-safe transport to a safe drop point, and a calm private waiting spot for family. The upsell adds a Kashi Vishwanath darshan (with a Sugam Darshan pass) or a short inland city tour.

**How much does the Lolark Shashti package cost?**
The pooja/sankalp component runs ₹5,000–₹11,999 depending on the ritual. A combined 1-day package with pooja, hotel and cab starts from ₹11,000. Everything is quoted in writing before booking.

**Is this suitable for elderly parents joining the couple?**
Yes. The 35 steep steps are difficult in monsoon, so we plan the visit for dawn, help family who are not entering the crowd wait calmly, and arrange easy transport. Tell us about any assistance needed.

**Do you guarantee results from the fertility ritual?**
No. This is a faith-based ritual and we make no promises about outcomes. What we guarantee is a respectful, private, well-organised visit — correct sankalp with a priest, safe transport, and no touts or pressure.

**Are boat rides included during the monsoon?**
No — in late August the Ganga is usually in flood and boats are suspended, so the plan is inland with auto/e-rickshaw transport. We can add boat rides once the river is safe post-monsoon.
