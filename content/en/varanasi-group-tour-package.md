## Varanasi Group Tour for Pilgrims

A **Varanasi group tour for pilgrims** is distinct from a casual holiday. It is more temple‑centric, slower‑paced, and focused on the "spiritual journey".

### Pilgrim-Specific Services
*   **Priority on Darshan:** We prioritize the Kashi Vishwanath Temple. Where possible, we assist with **Sugam Darshan** (VIP darshan) booking so your group (especially elders) does not have to stand in queues for hours.
*   **Rituals & Pujas:** We allocate specific time for *Abhishek*, *Rudrabhishek*, or *Sankalp* as requested by your group.
*   **Snana (Holy Dip):** We arrange the holy dip at selected, safer ghats (like Assi or private heritage ghats) where the crowd is manageable and changing facilities are available.
*   **Combined Circuits:** Many pilgrims wish to cover the "Trilok Yatra". We specialize in the **Varanasi Pilgrimage Tour with Prayagraj and Ayodhya**.

### Comfort and Safety for Elders
We know that pilgrim groups often include senior citizens.
*   **Minimal Walking:** Our itineraries and vehicle drop-off points are planned to minimize walking.
*   **Accessibility:** We select hotels with lifts and easy access.
*   **Pacing:** We schedule maximum 2-3 main darshans per day with built-in rest time, avoiding the peak heat of the afternoon.

## Varanasi Group Tour Itinerary (3 Days)

This is the core modular product that most groups prefer. It covers all "must-dos" without rushing and serves as a perfect **Varanasi group tour itinerary 3 days** plan.

### Day 1 – Arrival, First Darshan & Aarti
*   **Arrival:** Pickup from Varanasi Airport or Railway Station by AC Tempo Traveller. Transfer to hotel and check‑in. Rest to recover from travel fatigue.
*   **Afternoon (3:00 PM):** Visit **Kal Bhairav Mandir** first to take "permission" from the Guardian of Kashi.
*   **late Afternoon:** Proceed for darshan at **Kashi Vishwanath Temple** and **Maa Annapurna Mandir**.
*   **Evening:** Head to Dashashwamedh Ghat. Board a reserved hand-row boat to watch the spectacular **Ganga Aarti**.
*   **Dinner & Overnight:** Return to hotel for dinner.

### Day 2 – Sunrise Boat, City Temples & Sarnath
*   **Early Morning (5:00 AM):** **Sunrise Boat Ride** on the Ganga. Witness the ghats coming alive with prayers and chanting. Optional holy dip.
*   **Breakfast:** Back to hotel for breakfast.
*   **Late Morning:** Visit the "City South" temples: **Sankat Mochan**, **Durga Kund**, **Tulsi Manas Mandir**, and the massive **New Vishwanath Temple** inside BHU Campus.
*   **Afternoon:** Drive to **Sarnath** (12km). Visit the Dhamek Stupa, Deer Park, and the Museum (closed Fridays). It is a peaceful, green contrast to the city's hustle.
*   **Evening:** If energy permits, visit a silk weaving center or enjoy street food (Chaat/Lassi) at Godowlia.
*   **Overnight:** Stay in Varanasi.

### Day 3 – Leisure & Departure
*   **Morning:** Relaxed breakfast. Free time for shopping Banarasi sarees, holy threads, or souvenirs.
*   **Optional:** Visit Ramnagar Fort or take a heritage walk in the northern lanes (Pakka Mahal).
*   **Departure:** Transfer to airport/railway station with happy memories.

## Varanasi Ayodhya Prayagraj Group Package (4N/5D)

For a complete yatra, this **Varanasi Ayodhya Prayagraj group package** is the gold standard. It covers Kashi (Shiva), Ayodhya (Ram), and Prayagraj (Sangam).

**Typical Route:** Varanasi → Prayagraj → Ayodhya → Departure.

![Varanasi Ayodhya Prayagraj group package map](/images/infographics/varanasi-ayodhya-prayagraj-map.jpg)

### Day 1 – Arrive Varanasi
*   Pickup from airport/station. Check-in.
*   Evening witness the **Ganga Aarti** at Dashashwamedh Ghat.
*   Overnight in Varanasi.

### Day 2 – Varanasi Sightseeing
*   Early morning boat ride & holy dip.
*   Main Kashi Vishwanath & Annapurna Darshan.
*   Afternoon excursion to Sarnath.
*   Overnight in Varanasi.

### Day 3 – Varanasi → Prayagraj → Ayodhya
*   Morning drive to Prayagraj (120 km / 4 hrs).
*   **Triveni Sangam:** Take a boat to the confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati for a holy dip.
*   **Visits:** Lete Hanuman Ji (Reclining Hanuman) and Anand Bhavan.
*   Drive to Ayodhya (170 km / 4-5 hrs) in the evening.
*   Overnight in Ayodhya.

### Day 4 – Ayodhya Darshan
*   Full day in the holy city of Lord Ram.
*   **Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir:** Darshan of Ram Lalla.
*   **Hanuman Garhi:** Steep steps to the guardian fort of Ayodhya.
*   **Other Sites:** Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, Ram Ki Paidi.
*   **Evening:** Attend the serene **Saryu Aarti**.
*   Overnight in Ayodhya.

### Day 5 – Departure
*   Morning free for final prayers.
*   Drop at Ayodhya Airport (AYJ) / Station *OR* drive back to Varanasi for departure (depending on your flight tickets).
