# Bharat Milap at Nati Imli 2026

> **Correction notice, updated 3 September 2026:** The previous page incorrectly called Nati Imli part of Ramnagar and advertised an invented shuttle timetable, “guided wristbands,” road checkpoints, fixed viewing areas and exact hourly programme. Those claims have been removed.

## Date and location

Kashi Bharat Milap is observed on the day after Vijayadashami. The Government of India's tourism calendar places Dussehra on **Tuesday, 20 October 2026**, and the 2026 Kashi Bharat Milap calendar therefore places the observance on **Wednesday, 21 October 2026**.

- **Expected date:** Wednesday, 21 October 2026
- **Venue:** Nati Imli, Varanasi city
- **Local time and access plan:** Await the organiser's and Varanasi Police's current notices

Sources:

- [Incredible India: Dussehra on 20 October 2026](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/dussehra)
- [Drik Panchang: Kashi Bharat Milap on 21 October 2026](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/bharat-milap/kashi-bharat-milap-date-time.html?year=2026)

## What happens at Bharat Milap?

The leela depicts the reunion of Lord Rama and Bharat after Rama's exile. It is a major Varanasi observance associated with the Nati Imli Ramlila tradition and can draw a very large crowd.

Nati Imli is in **Varanasi city**. It is not a locality in Ramnagar, and visitors should not follow maps that direct them to Ramnagar Fort for the Nati Imli performance.

## Planning safely

- Reconfirm the start time on the event day; this page does not claim a fixed hour.
- Check Varanasi Traffic Police advice for closures, parking and pedestrian entry.
- Use only an officially designated shuttle if one is announced.
- Do not pay for a “VIP wristband” or restricted viewing area without an organiser-issued notice.
- Agree on a pickup point outside the restricted zone and expect to walk.
- Keep children and older travellers close and choose a regroup point in advance.
