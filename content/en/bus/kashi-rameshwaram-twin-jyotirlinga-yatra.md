## 🔀 Canonical Route / Connector Patterns
Three proven patterns—pick during enquiry (we fine‑tune to your dates & seat inventory).

### Pattern A – **Fly‑First / Bus‑Last (Fastest)**
**Flow:** Home City → Fly to Madurai (or Chennai/Bengaluru + connect) → Coach to Rameshwaram → Darshan → Return Madurai → Flight(s) to Varanasi → Kashi circuit → Flight home.

**Pros:** Lowest overland fatigue; tight 6–7 day completion possible.  
**Cons:** More flight segments → higher base cost; needs firm booking early for winter.  
**Comfort Rating:** ★★★★☆

### Pattern B – **Bus + Overnight Coach (Budget/Scenic)**
**Flow:** Depart South India by deluxe coach → (staged halts) → Varanasi first → Kashi darshan → Long staged southbound to Rameshwaram (multi‑day) → Return.

**Pros:** Lowest flight spend; group bonding; open date flexibility.  
**Cons:** High cumulative seat time; physical strain for elders; weather risk mid‑monsoon.  
**Comfort Rating:** ★★☆☆☆ (upgrade rest days recommended).

### Pattern C – **Split (Flight + Rail + Coach)**
**Flow:** Fly to Varanasi → Kashi circuit → Rail / flight to Madurai or Trichy → Coach to Rameshwaram → Return via nearest hub.  
**Pros:** Balances cost + comfort; rail leg adds pilgrimage ambiance.  
**Cons:** Rail PNR confirmation risk in peak; adds scheduling complexity.  
**Comfort Rating:** ★★★☆☆

## ⏱️ Darshan & Ritual Scheduling Strategy
### Kashi Vishwanath (Varanasi)
- **Early Darshan Window:** Pre‑7:00 AM arrival slashes queue dwell → higher temple energy post sunrise.
- **Sugam / Priority Options:** Limited; book early if itinerary is compressed (fees variable; availability seasonal).
- **Ganga Aarti:** Sunset dependent; boat vantage offers continuity but manage expectation on proximity; on‑ghat immersion for high-energy devotees.

### Ramanathaswamy (Rameshwaram)
- **Morning Corridor & Main Darshan:** Early entry (pre large tour influx) keeps flow smoother; allocate unhurried time for pillared corridors.
- **Teerth Snan (Sacred Tanks):** If performing formal sequence, schedule dedicated slot (avoid collapsing into main darshan queue period). Maintain modest attire & footwear plan.
- **Evening Re‑Entry:** Often calmer for reflective second darshan (non-festival days).
