## ⏱️ Darshan & Ritual Scheduling Guidance
### 1. Kashi (Varanasi)
- **Sunrise Boat (Best Window):** ~30–45 min after nautical dawn: calmer water, pastel light.
- **Kashi Vishwanath Darshan:** Early arrival (pre‑7:00 AM) drastically reduces queue dwell; special (Sugam) darshan optional (limited). Expect 45–180 min variance in festival peaks.
- **Ganga Aarti (Dashashwamedh):** Sunset-timed; reserve boat spot early; on-ground crowd compression high after T‑30 min.

### 2. Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam)
- **Snan / Boat:** Target 6:00–9:30 AM window; midday glare + wind increases discomfort and boarding difficulty for elders.
- **Pind Daan / Rituals:** Pre‑book priest (gotra details) to avoid ad hoc negotiation.

### 3. Ayodhya (Ram Mandir)
- **Darshan Slots:** Time-slot allocations vary seasonally; evening (post 6 PM) can offer slightly smoother movement outside festival spikes.
- **Perimeter Security:** Extra screening time: add 20–30 min buffer prior to slot.
