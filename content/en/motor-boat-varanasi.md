## What to expect on a motorboat

A motorboat covers far more river than a rowboat in the same time. You'll board off the ghat steps, and the boat moves briskly between ghats — great for photos of the full skyline, less suited to slow, silent moments.

- **Duration:** 1.5–2.5 hours for a standard ghat tour; up to 4 hours for the full 84-ghats run with Ramnagar Fort.
- **Feel:** faster and steadier in open water, but you'll notice the engine hum and some vibration. Sit mid-boat if anyone is prone to motion sensitivity.
- **Capacity:** most motorboats are rated for 10–15. Always confirm the boat's rated number — a fixed-fare boat has no reason to overload.

## Is a motorboat right for your group?

- **Yes, if** you're 8–15 people, short on time, or want to reach the far ghats and Ramnagar Fort. It's the best per-person value on the river when a full group splits one fixed fare.
- **Maybe not, if** you're a couple or after a quiet, photo-first ride — a [hand rowboat](/en/hand-boat-ride-varanasi) is calmer and lower. And for 15+ or a premium occasion, a [bajra](/en/bajra-boat-ride-varanasi) is more comfortable.

## Getting the best group price

The trick with groups is simple: **one fixed fare for the whole boat, split on the day.**

- Have **one person** WhatsApp the booking with the final headcount and route — it avoids mixed messages and duplicate quotes.
- Confirm the **rated capacity** so no one is left on the ghat.
- Agree the fare **in writing before boarding.** The fare is per boat, not per head, so a fuller boat is cheaper for everyone.

## Season and water conditions

- **October–March** is the ideal window — settled water and clear skies; expect a 20–30% seasonal premium.
- **Monsoon (July–September)** raises the river and its current, and motorboat routes may be shortened for safety. Follow the boatmen's guidance on high-water days.
- Life jackets are provided for every passenger on every ride — keep them on, especially with children aboard.
