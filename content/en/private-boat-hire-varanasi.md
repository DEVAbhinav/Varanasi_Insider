## What's included

Private hire means the **whole boat is yours** — no shared benches, no strangers, and no fixed loop. Every private booking includes an experienced boatman, life jackets for everyone aboard, and a fixed fare agreed before you board. You set the departure time and choose the ghats you want to see.

## Rowboat or motorboat for private hire?

- **Private hand rowboat (₹1,500–₹3,000, 1–6 people):** low, silent and stable — the best choice for photography, proposals and a slow, intimate ride. Sits close to the water for that classic eye-level ghat shot.
- **Private motorboat (₹3,500–₹6,000, up to 15):** better when you want to keep a larger family together and cover more river, including a run toward Ramnagar Fort.

If it's just the two of you or a small family, the rowboat is almost always the nicer experience.

## Photography and proposal tips

- **Book the sunrise or blue-hour slot** — the soft light on the ghats is unbeatable, and the river is at its calmest.
- **Tell us the plan in advance** so the boatman can position you side-on to the ghats for clean, unobstructed frames.
- **A low rowboat beats a motorboat** for stable, reflection-friendly shots.
- **For a proposal,** we'll keep it discreet and let you pick the exact moment and ghat.

## Comfortable for families and elders

Private hire is the calm option for travellers with **children or older parents**: no jostling for a seat, no crowded bench, and the boatman can board you gently off the steps and keep the pace easy. Keep life jackets on throughout and children seated between adults.

## Season and timing

October–March is the ideal window — calm water and clear light, with a 20–30% seasonal premium. During the monsoon (July–September) the river runs high and fast, so routes may be shortened for safety. Reserve your slot ahead for the sunrise and aarti windows, which fill fastest.
