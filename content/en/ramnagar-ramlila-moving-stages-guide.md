The Government of India's tourism portal lists Ramnagar Ramlila in **September 2026** and describes the tradition as spanning 31 days, but it does not publish a complete night-by-night calendar. ## How the moving-stage tradition works

Ramnagar becomes the setting for the Ramayana rather than relying on one conventional theatre. Different outdoor areas represent places in the story, and spectators may move as the narrative advances. The route, walking distance and accessible vehicle point depend on that night's episode.

This means a fixed map copied from an earlier year is not enough for planning. The practical source of truth is the **current daily programme and local directions**.

## Do not confuse Ramnagar with Nati Imli

Nati Imli is a locality in Varanasi city, not a zone of Ramnagar town. The famous Nati Imli Bharat Milap is a separate local Ramlila tradition. A route map describing a procession from Ramnagar Fort to Nati Imli as one moving-stage sequence is misleading and should not be used.

## A reliable planning method

### Before leaving

1. Confirm the night's episode, start time and location from a current organiser, district or tourism notice.
2. Ask whether police diversions change the normal Ramnagar approach.
3. Agree on the last motorable drop point and return pickup point with your driver.
4. Save the pickup details offline in case mobile service becomes congested.

### At Ramnagar

- Follow the official/local direction for the night's performance rather than an old online timetable.
- Expect some walking and wear footwear suitable for uneven ground.
- Establish a group meeting point before the performance begins.
- Respect devotional activity and avoid blocking the moving audience or performers.

## Transport guidance

A driver can take you to the permitted approach point but cannot guarantee access to inner performance areas. Do not accept claims of private vehicle access, reserved viewing, official wristbands or special entry unless they are backed by the current organiser or police notice.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Ramnagar Ramlila have one permanent stage?

No. The town's different spaces become settings in the Ramayana narrative, and the audience may move between them.

### Are the exact 2026 dates confirmed?

The official tourism listing available for this update shows **September 2026**, but not the full daily schedule. Earlier exact dates on this page were not verified.

### Is Nati Imli in Ramnagar?

No. Nati Imli is in Varanasi city and should not be treated as a Ramnagar moving-stage venue.
