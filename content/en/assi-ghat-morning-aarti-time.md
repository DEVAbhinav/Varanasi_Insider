# Assi Ghat Morning Aarti Time – Subah-e-Banaras Guide

**Current slot:** 6:30 AM – 7:00 AM (winter). Arrive 20 minutes early with shawl + socks.

## What Happens at Subah-e-Banaras?
- Yoga session led by instructors from BHU.
- Sanskrit chants, conch shells, and slow lamp choreography.
- Free herbal tea for early arrivals on the second step.

## How to Reach Before Sunrise
- Hotel pickup at 5:45 AM ensures stress-free entry.
- From airport, schedule a 4:30 AM drop with wait service.
- Pre-book via [dispatch](/booking) or call +91-94503-01573.

## Photography & Gear
- Shoot from right staircase for diagonal flame shots.
- Settings: ISO 1600, f/2.8, 1/125s before dawn.
- Tripods allowed if you stand behind the first row.

## Etiquette
- Keep phones on silent.
- Sit during mantras, stand only when priests invite devotees forward.
- Avoid flash; it distracts performers and other pilgrims.
