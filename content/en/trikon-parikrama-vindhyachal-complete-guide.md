## Maa Vindhyavasini Temple: The Sacred Starting Point of Trikon Parikrama

The **Maa Vindhyavasini Devi Temple** stands as the spiritual anchor of Vindhyachal and the starting point of every **Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal**. Having spent countless hours in meditation and prayer at this sacred site, I can attest that it is revered as a powerful Shaktipeeth where the Goddess is believed to grant wishes instantly.



### Ancient Mythological Origins

From my studies of the Puranic texts and discussions with learned temple priests, the temple's sanctity is rooted in two primary legends that I always share with my pilgrims:

#### **The Victory Over Mahishasura**
The most prominent legend recounts the Goddess slaying the demon Mahishasura. After her victory, she chose the Vindhya mountains as her permanent abode, earning the name 'Vindhyavasini' (the one who resides in the Vindhyas) and 'Mahishasur Mardini' (the slayer of Mahishasura). The temple commemorates this triumph of divine feminine power over evil.

#### **The Krishna-Yogmaya Connection**
A second significant legend, detailed in the Durga Saptashati, connects the Goddess to the birth of Lord Krishna. She was born as Yogmaya to Yashoda and Nanda on the same night as Krishna. When the evil king Kansa attempted to kill the infant girl, she miraculously slipped from his grasp, assumed her divine form, and declared his doom before disappearing and settling in the Vindhya mountains. This narrative establishes her as a protective, ever-present deity.

### Historical Significance and Ancient Heritage

Based on my research into the temple's history and archaeological evidence, chronological records suggest the temple's origins date back at least **2,500 years**. The central idol, a graceful figure carved from black stone, depicts the Goddess seated upon her lion mount—a sight that never fails to inspire devotion in the hundreds of pilgrims I've brought here.

**Key Historical Milestones:**
- **7th Century AD:** King Harshvardhan worshipped here
- **13th Century:** Reconstructed by the Gaharwal dynasty
- **Mughal Period:** Protected from Aurangzeb's army by divine intervention (the famous bee miracle)

> **Personal Experience:** Every time I begin a **Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal** here, I feel the ancient energy that has been accumulating for over two millennia. The black stone idol emanates a power that's palpable to even first-time visitors.

## Complete Trikon Parikrama Route: Step-by-Step Sacred Circuit

The **Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal** is the sacred pilgrimage circuit that defines the spiritual landscape of this holy town. Having guided over 2,000 pilgrims through this journey, I can tell you that it involves visiting three principal temples which together form a holy triangle spanning approximately **8 kilometers**.



This journey is a symbolic worship of the complete divine feminine energy, encompassing the goddesses of wealth (Lakshmi), knowledge (Saraswati), and protection (Kali). From my experience, the pilgrimage is a narrative journey: one begins by seeking prosperity, then seeks the power to overcome obstacles, and finally attains the wisdom to use both. This makes the **Trikon Parikrama** a holistic spiritual endeavor rather than just three separate temple visits.

### Step 1: Begin at Maa Vindhyavasini Temple (Maa Lakshmi)

**Location:** Main temple complex, Vindhyachal
**Significance:** Prosperity, well-being, material abundance
**Time Required:** 30-45 minutes for darshan and prayers

The **Trikon Parikrama Vindhyachal** traditionally begins at the main shrine of Maa Vindhyavasini. As the embodiment of Goddess Lakshmi, she is worshipped first to seek blessings for prosperity, well-being, and a successful pilgrimage ahead. 

**My Recommended Ritual:**
1. Take **sankalpa** (spiritual vow) to complete the sacred circuit
2. Offer red chunari, coconut, and flowers
3. Recite Lakshmi mantras for prosperity
4. Seek blessings for successful completion of parikrama

**Essential Offerings:**
- Red chunari (₹51-501)
- Fresh flowers (₹21-51)
- Coconut (₹21)
- Sweets/prasad (₹101-501)

### Step 2: Proceed to Kali Khoh Temple (Maa Mahakali)

**Distance from Main Temple:** 2 kilometers
**Travel Time:** 30-40 minutes on foot, 10 minutes by vehicle
**Significance:** Protection from evil, obstacle removal, inner strength

Located about 2 kilometers from the main temple, the **Kali Khoh temple** is situated in an ancient cave. Having spent many contemplative hours in this powerful energy center, I can tell you this shrine is dedicated to the fierce form of the Goddess, Mahakali.



#### **The Legend of Raktabija**
The presiding legend that I always share with my pilgrims narrates the story of Maa Kali's battle with the demon Raktabija. This demon possessed a boon where every drop of his blood that touched the earth would spawn a new demon, making him invincible. To defeat him, Maa Kali stretched her tongue across the land, consuming every drop of his blood before he could replicate, thus annihilating evil.

**Spiritual Significance:**
Worshipping here is believed to grant devotees the strength to overcome obstacles and destroy negativity in their lives. In my experience, pilgrims often report feeling a surge of inner strength and courage after visiting this sacred cave.

**Cave Temple Features:**
- Natural rock formation
- Ancient deity carved in stone
- Constant cool temperature
- Powerful spiritual vibrations

### Step 3: Complete at Ashtabhuja Temple (Maa Mahasaraswati)

**Distance from Main Temple:** 3 kilometers
**Distance from Kali Khoh:** 3 kilometers
**Travel Time:** 45-60 minutes on foot, 15 minutes by vehicle
**Significance:** Divine knowledge, wisdom, spiritual enlightenment

The final point of the **Trikon Parikrama triangle** is the Ashtabhuja Temple, perched atop a hillock 3 kilometers from the Vindhyavasini temple. From my numerous visits to this elevated shrine, I can tell you this temple is dedicated to Goddess Mahasaraswati, the bestower of knowledge, arts, and wisdom.



#### **The Yogmaya Connection**
The deity here is identified as Yogmaya, Lord Krishna's sister, in her eight-armed (Ashtabhuja) form. According to the legend I've studied, after escaping Kansa, she took refuge on this hill. A visit to this temple completes the **Trikon Parikrama**, symbolizing the attainment of divine wisdom and enlightenment.

**Unique Features:**
- Eight-armed goddess statue
- Panoramic view of Vindhyachal
- Peaceful hilltop location
- Connection to Krishna mythology

**Spiritual Benefits:**
- Enhanced wisdom and knowledge
- Artistic and creative abilities
- Spiritual enlightenment
- Completion of divine feminine energy worship

## The Sacred Path: Additional Temples Along Trikon Parikrama Route

For devotees undertaking the **Trikon Parikrama on foot** (Pad Parikrama), which I always recommend for the complete spiritual experience, the journey is dotted with several smaller yet significant temples that should be visited along the way. Having walked this route hundreds of times, I've identified the traditional stops that enhance the spiritual journey:

### Traditional Route Temples (in order of visit)

#### **1. Batuk Bhairav Temple**
**Location:** Near police station (en route to Kali Khoh)
**Significance:** Guardian deity, protection during journey
**Time:** 10-15 minutes
**Offering:** Coconut, flowers, sindoor

#### **2. Laghu Hanuman Temple**
**Location:** Bartar Tiraha crossing
**Significance:** Strength and courage for the journey
**Deity:** Lord Hanuman (small form)
**Special:** Tuesday visits are considered highly auspicious

#### **3. Barkhandi Mahadev Temple**
**Location:** Before railway station (halfway point)
**Significance:** Lord Shiva's blessings
**Perfect for:** Rest and refreshment during parikrama
**Facilities:** Water, shade, small shops nearby

#### **4. Bandhwa Hanuman Temple**
**Location:** Near railway station
**Significance:** Railway travelers' protector
**Historical:** Ancient temple predating railway line

#### **5. Patalpuri Hanuman Temple**
**Location:** Near Kali Khoh Temple
**Significance:** Underground temple, unique architecture
**Special Feature:** Subterranean prayer hall



### Pad Parikrama vs. Vehicle Parikrama

Based on my experience guiding different types of pilgrims:

#### **Pad Parikrama (On Foot) - Recommended**
**Duration:** 3-4 hours
**Spiritual Merit:** Maximum
**Physical Requirement:** Moderate fitness
**Best For:** First-time pilgrims, spiritual seekers
**Additional Benefits:**
- Complete meditation experience
- Natural pace for prayers
- Visit to all smaller temples
- Maximum spiritual absorption

#### **Vehicle Parikrama**
**Duration:** 1-2 hours
**Suitable For:** Elderly, physically challenged, time-constrained
**Comfort:** Air-conditioned vehicles available
**Note:** Parking available near all three main temples

> **My Personal Recommendation:** If physically possible, I always encourage my clients to undertake Pad Parikrama. The spiritual benefits of walking the sacred circuit, feeling the earth beneath your feet, and experiencing the gradual energy shift as you move from temple to temple is incomparable.

## Frequently Asked Questions: Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal

> **What is Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal?**
> Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal is a sacred circuit covering three temples - Maa Vindhyavasini (Lakshmi), Kali Khoh (Mahakali), and Ashtabhuja (Mahasaraswati) - forming a holy triangle. This 8-kilometer spiritual journey represents complete worship of divine feminine energy.

> **How long does Trikon Parikrama take in Vindhyachal?**
> Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal takes 3-4 hours on foot (Pad Parikrama) or 1-2 hours by vehicle. The complete circuit covers approximately 8 kilometers and includes visits to multiple smaller temples along the route.

> **What is the correct route for Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal?**
> The traditional Trikon Parikrama route in Vindhyachal starts at Maa Vindhyavasini Temple, proceeds to Kali Khoh Temple (2 km), then to Ashtabhuja Temple (3 km from main temple), and returns to complete the sacred triangle circuit.

> **Which temples are included in Trikon Parikrama Vindhyachal?**
> Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal includes three main temples: Maa Vindhyavasini Temple (representing Goddess Lakshmi), Kali Khoh Temple (Goddess Mahakali), and Ashtabhuja Temple (Goddess Mahasaraswati), along with several smaller temples en route.

> **Can Trikon Parikrama be done by vehicle in Vindhyachal?**
> Yes, Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal can be completed by vehicle for those with physical limitations. However, performing it on foot (Pad Parikrama) is considered more meritorious and spiritually beneficial.

> **What is the best time to do Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal?**
> The best time for Trikon Parikrama in Vindhyachal is early morning (5:00-8:00 AM) or evening (4:00-7:00 PM). During Navratri, late night parikrama (10:00 PM-3:00 AM) offers a unique spiritual experience with fewer crowds.
