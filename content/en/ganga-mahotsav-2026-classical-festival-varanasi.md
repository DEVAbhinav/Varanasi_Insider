# Ganga Mahotsav 2026: latest dates and planning status

> **Current planning window:** **20–23 November 2026**, followed by Dev Deepawali on **24 November 2026**. These dates are based on the latest district-administration schedule reported on 19 August 2026. Programme, venue, entry and traffic details should still be treated as provisional until the administration publishes its final notice.

**Source:** [District-administration schedule reported by The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/varanasi/kashi-gears-up-for-ganga-mahotsav-dev-deepawali/amp_articleshow/133357054.cms)

## Correction to the earlier guide

This page previously stated that Ganga Mahotsav would run **6–8 November**, immediately after Dev Deepawali. That was incorrect. Dev Deepawali falls on **Tuesday, 24 November 2026**, and the latest reported Ganga Mahotsav plan places the cultural programme before it.

The earlier day-by-day artist programme, ticket prices and booking channels were not supported by an official 2026 announcement, so they have been removed.

## What is currently known

Ganga Mahotsav is a Varanasi cultural programme associated with music, dance, crafts and the city's riverfront heritage. For 2026, the reported administrative plan is:

| Date | Planning status |
| --- | --- |
| 20–23 November | Ganga Mahotsav programme window |
| 24 November | Dev Deepawali / Kartik Purnima |

Do not assume a specific ghat, artist, seat category, ticket price or nightly schedule until it appears in an official programme.

## Before booking

- Ask the hotel or operator whether the booking remains refundable if the official programme changes.
- Verify venues and road diversions shortly before travel.
- Do not buy a “VIP pass” from an unofficial seller.
- Keep 24 November logistics separate: Dev Deepawali has much heavier riverfront restrictions.
- If combining both events, plan a stay covering 20–25 November.

## Transport notes

Normal taxis cannot reach every riverfront road during major events. Once the traffic plan is released, confirm the vehicle drop point, walking distance and return pickup area in writing.

Kashi Taxi can arrange ordinary airport, station and hotel transfers. Event-specific access or passes will only be quoted after an official 2026 notification confirms them.
