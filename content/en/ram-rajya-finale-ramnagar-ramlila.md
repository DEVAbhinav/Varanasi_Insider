# Ramnagar Ramlila Ram Rajya Finale 2026

## Current 2026 status

The Government of India's tourism portal lists the **Ramlila of Ramnagar in September 2026** and describes it as a 31-day tradition. It does not provide the day-by-day 2026 programme or a confirmed Ram Rajya finale date.

- [Official Incredible India listing: The Ramlila of Ramnagar in Varanasi](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/uttar-pradesh/the-ramlila-of-ramnagar-in-varanasi)
- [UNESCO: Ramlila, the traditional performance of the Ramayana](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ramlila-the-traditional-performance-of-the-ramayana-00110)

Treat the finale date, starting time, entry arrangements and traffic plan as **pending local confirmation**. Do not buy a “VIP pass” or date-specific transfer merely because a commercial page presents an exact schedule.

## What is the Ram Rajya finale?

Ram Rajya is the concluding coronation episode in the Ramayana narrative. In the Ramnagar tradition it follows the long sequence of performances staged across the town. Visitors should distinguish this finale from **Nati Imli Bharat Milap**, a separate Varanasi observance held at Nati Imli in the city.

## How to verify the finale

Close to your visit:

1. Check the latest district-administration and Uttar Pradesh Tourism notices.
2. Confirm the night's episode and location locally at Ramnagar before leaving your hotel.
3. Ask your accommodation or driver to verify police diversions and the nearest permitted drop point.
4. Keep bookings flexible until the organiser's daily programme is available.

## Practical visitor guidance

- Expect walking and standing on uneven ground.
- Carry water, a charged phone and only a small bag.
- Arrange a regroup point before entering a crowd.
- Follow police instructions; vehicle access can change during the event.
- Avoid flash photography during devotional scenes and do not obstruct performers or devotees.

### Is Nati Imli part of Ramnagar town?

No. Nati Imli is in Varanasi city. Its well-known Bharat Milap is not the same event as the Ram Rajya finale at Ramnagar.
