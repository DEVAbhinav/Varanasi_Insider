## Why choose a bajra

- **Space & comfort.** Walk around, cushioned seating, room for elders and kids.
- **Elevation.** The best sightlines for aarti, fireworks and Dev Deepawali lamps.
- **Occasion-ready.** Ideal for birthdays, anniversaries, corporate groups and weddings on the water.
- **Group value.** Split across 20+ guests, a premium boat becomes surprisingly affordable per head.

## What's included — and what you can add

A standard bajra hire gives you the boat, an experienced crew and life jackets for the whole ride. Because it's the event boat of the river, most extras are available on request:

- **Catering** — from tea and snacks to a full meal, arranged in advance.
- **Décor** — flowers, lighting or a banner for birthdays, anniversaries and weddings.
- **Guide or commentary** — a local guide to narrate the ghats for a delegation or corporate group.
- **Sound** — a small PA for speeches or music at private events.

Tell us the occasion when you enquire and we'll build it into a single written quote.

## Planning an event on a bajra

- **Confirm your headcount early.** Bajras seat 15–30; the fare and boat are matched to your final number.
- **Book the boat well ahead.** There are far fewer bajras than small boats, and they go first for festivals and weddings.
- **Time it around the light.** Late afternoon into sunset is the most photogenic window; aarti evenings are the most atmospheric.
- **Allow a buffer.** For catering or décor, give us a few days' notice so nothing is rushed.

## Dev Deepawali on a bajra

On [Dev Deepawali](/en/dev-deepawali-boat-ride-pricing-guide-2026) — the biggest night of the Varanasi year — a bajra is simply the best seat on the water. Its elevated deck rises above the crowds for an unobstructed view of the lamps and fireworks along the ghats. Demand is extraordinary and boats sell out **weeks** ahead, so this is the one booking you should never leave late. Festival pricing is quoted separately.

## Safety and comfort

Every bajra comes with life jackets for all guests and a crew who work the river daily. The elevated, stable deck is the most comfortable option for **elders, children and anyone nervous about small boats** — there's room to sit properly, walk around, and stay well away from the water's edge.
