## How Delhi-Origin Trips Usually Work Best

### Option 1: Delhi -> Varanasi -> Delhi

Best for:

- short leave windows
- elders who should not change hotels too often
- travelers whose main purpose is Kashi Vishwanath, Ganga Aarti, and city darshan

### Option 2: Delhi -> Varanasi -> Ayodhya -> Delhi / Lucknow

Best for:

- Ram and Shiva circuit
- family groups with 4 days available
- pilgrims who do not want to rush Prayagraj into the same trip

### Option 3: Delhi -> Varanasi -> Prayagraj -> Ayodhya -> Delhi / Lucknow

Best for:

- stronger devotional intent
- temple committees and satsang groups
- organizers who want the sacred triangle in one properly sequenced package

This is usually the best commercial fit for larger groups because the package becomes easier to justify to the whole batch.

## Recommended 4N/5D Circuit for Delhi Groups

### Day 1: Delhi to Varanasi, Light Arrival Day

- arrival in Varanasi
- hotel check-in
- rest and reset
- evening Aarti if the batch is on time and not fatigued

We prefer not to overload arrival day for Delhi groups. Late arrivals and tired elders can ruin the rest of the trip if day one is badly planned.

### Day 2: Main Kashi Day

- Kashi Vishwanath
- Kal Bhairav
- Annapurna / city temple sequence
- optional sunrise or evening river experience
- group meal and recovery window

### Day 3: Prayagraj or Ayodhya Transition Day

This depends on chosen package:

- **2-city package:** Varanasi to Ayodhya
- **3-city package:** Varanasi to Prayagraj, then onward or overnight depending group energy

### Day 4: Ayodhya Main Darshan Day

- Ram Janmabhoomi
- Hanuman Garhi
- Kanak Bhawan
- Saryu Aarti

### Day 5: Departure

The cleanest departures are often:

- Ayodhya out
- Lucknow out
- Varanasi return only if required by tickets

## Family vs Group Decision

For a **family from Delhi**, the usual mistake is booking a big sacred circuit just because it sounds complete.

Use the shorter format if:

- elders are central to the trip
- there are children in the group
- the leave window is tight
- the family wants more prayer and less road time

Use the full circuit if:

- the group is disciplined
- headcount is healthy enough to justify better transport economics
- everyone agrees on the pilgrimage priority
- the organizer wants a stronger devotional package, not just a city break

## FAQ: Varanasi Tour Package from Delhi

### Is 3 days enough for a Delhi to Varanasi pilgrimage?

Yes for a city-focused Kashi trip. No if you also want Ayodhya and Prayagraj done properly.

### What is the best package for a Delhi family with elders?

Usually either a Varanasi-only stay or a shorter Ayodhya plus Varanasi circuit. The full triangle needs better stamina and more time.

### Should Delhi groups arrive in Varanasi and depart from Ayodhya?

Often yes. That reduces backtracking and can make the itinerary much cleaner.

### Can train groups use this package?

Yes. The package can be aligned to rail arrival as long as the batch timing is clear.

### Is this page only for families?

No. It is also for temple committees, satsang groups, and larger pilgrim batches starting in Delhi.
