## Ram Janmabhoomi Security Do's & Don'ts

- **Allowed:** Mobile phone (silent), small wallet, cloth bag with sealed sweets, printed darshan pass, folded flag (no pole).

- **Dress Code:** Modest attire recommended; avoid metal-heavy belts. Provide elders with slip-on footwear.
