## 1. Varanasi City Staples

### 1.1 Same-Day Varanasi Highlights (8–10 hrs)
- **Profile:** Duration 8–10 hrs · Ideal for first-timers, business travelers, pilgrims in transit.
- **Pricing:** ₹7,000–₹11,000 per person all-in (guide+boat) _or_ ₹2,200 sedan self-guided. Innova ₹3,700+, Tempo ₹4,250 day slab.
- **Vehicle slots:** Sedan 4+1, Innova 6+1, upgrade to Pink Shield sedan for solo women (₹200 base + ₹14/km).
- **Lead time:** 48 hrs regular, 5 days during festivals.
- **Itinerary spine:**
  - _Morning_ (9 AM–1 PM): Kashi Vishwanath + Annapurna + Vishalakshi, Sankat Mochan, Durga Kund, BHU campus, Bharat Mata Temple.
  - _Afternoon_ (1–5 PM): Sarnath circuit (Dhamek, Chaukhandi, Mulagandhakuti, 80-ft Buddha, museum closed Fri), BHU Birla Temple.
  - _Evening_ (5–8:30 PM): Private boat ride, Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti, Vishwanath Gali + NaMo Ghat walk.
- **Inclusions:** Private AC cab, licensed guide, Sarnath transfer, evening boat, parking/toll/driver, water.
- **Exclusions:** Meals, temple donations, Sugam Darshan ticket (₹300), museum entry (₹20–₹50), tips.
- **Upsells:** VIP darshan ₹1,000, photographer ₹800, food crawl ₹600, yoga-on-ghat ₹990, Pink Shield upgrade ₹500 flat.
- **Booking cues:** Suggest 7 AM start Apr–Sep. Remind guests to carry ID, modest attire, and note that Dashashwamedh Aarti overcrowds—boat seat solves this.

### 1.2 1 Night / 2 Days Kashi Immersion
- **Profile:** Duration 2D/1N · Ideal for weekend travelers, couples, families wanting both sunrise and evening rituals.
- **Price band (per person, double sharing):** Budget ₹3,999–₹6,100; Standard ₹8,000–₹10,000; Deluxe ₹12,000–₹15,000; Luxury ₹20,000+ (hotel separate or bundled as CP/MAP/AP).
- **Vehicles:** Sedan (2–3 pax), Innova (4–5 pax), Tempo (6–12 pax). Add ₹3,000 sedan / ₹4,000 SUV per extra day.
- **Itinerary spine:**
  - _Day 1:_ Arrival, Kaal Bhairav, Kashi Vishwanath complex, old-city silk walk, sunset boat & Dashashwamedh Aarti, dinner.
  - _Day 2 Early:_ Subah-e-Banaras sunrise boat + Assi Aarti, morning walk, temple trio (Sankat Mochan, Durga, Tulsi Manas), BHU, Sarnath immersion, silk workshop, shopping.
- **Inclusions:** 1-night verified 3★ stay (upgradeable), breakfast (MAP/AP available), private AC cab entire tour, sunrise + evening boat rides, English-speaking guide, toll/parking.
- **Exclusions:** Lunch unless MAP/AP, Sugam tickets, museum entry, tips, insurance.
- **Upsells:** Heritage haveli walk ₹1,200, Malaiyyo tasting ₹400 (Nov–Feb), private classical performance ₹2,000–₹5,000 group, yoga session ₹1,000.
- **Lead time:** 7–10 days peak (Oct–Mar), 48 hrs shoulder, 24 hrs off-season.

### 1.3 2 Nights / 3 Days Classic Kashi
- **Profile:** Duration 3D/2N · Ideal for deep cultural seekers, photographers, families wanting unhurried pace.
- **Price band:** ₹15,000–₹22,000 pp standard; Deluxe ₹22,000–₹27,000; Luxury ₹35,000–₹50,000+ depending on hotel tier.
- **Key differentiators:** Adds Ramnagar Fort, cultural add-ons (music, silk workshop), multiple boat experiences.
- **Itinerary spine:**
  - _Day 1:_ Arrival, Kaal Bhairav + Vishwanath complex, walking tour, sunset boat & Ganga Aarti.
  - _Day 2:_ Sunrise boat, temple circuit, BHU, Sarnath deep dive, cultural sessions (music, weaving), Assi evening.
  - _Day 3:_ Ramnagar Fort / extra temple options / yoga or photo tour, shopping conclave, departure.
- **Inclusions & upsells:** Same as 1N/2D plus dedicated cultural host, silk workshop, optional VIP balconies for Aarti, curated food trail.

### 1.4 3 Nights / 4 Days Premium Varanasi
- **Profile:** Duration 4D/3N · Ideal for luxury travelers, festival seekers (Dev Deepawali/Mahashivratri alignment), NRIs.
- **Price band:** ₹25,000–₹38,000 pp standard luxury, ₹1,00,000+ for ghat-facing heritage suites + concierge.
- **Highlights:** Combines cooking class, yoga-on-ghat, bespoke shopping concierge, Dev Deepawali or Mahashivratri vigil, curated dining.
- **Booking cues:** Push 60–90 day advance for festival dates; require 50% deposit. Suggest Taj Ganges / Brijrama / Suryauday Haveli for premium stays.

## 2. Ayodhya & Ramayan Circuit

### 2.1 Varanasi ↔ Ayodhya Taxi (Sedan/SUV)
- **Distance:** 219 km one way · Drive time 4.5–5 hrs.
- **Pricing:** Sedan ₹2,500 one-way / ₹5,000 same-day return / ₹8,000–₹13,000 for 2D/1N; Innova +₹1,800 per leg.
- **Itinerary suggestions:** Ram Janmabhoomi, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, Saryu Aarti.
- **Add-ons:** VIP queue assist ₹1,000, packed sattvic meals ₹400, QR-code prep for checkpoints.

### 2.2 Ayodhya Tempo Traveller (1-Day)
- **Rates:** 12-seater ₹14,500; 17-seater ₹15,500 (5 AM–9 PM, fuel/toll/parking included).
- **Use case:** Community pilgrimages, small parivars, corporate darshan groups.
- **Notes:** Encourage 04:30 AM dispatch; share mandatory ID list for security gates.

### 2.3 Ayodhya 1N/2D Immersion
- **Pricing:** Sedan transport ₹8,000–₹10,000; SUV ₹11,000–₹13,000; hotels extra (₹2k–₹6k per room). Package PP ₹4,000–₹10,000 depending on hotel tier.
- **Itinerary:**
  - _Day 1:_ Arrival, Ram Janmabhoomi darshan, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan + Sita Rasoi, Dashrath Mahal, evening Saryu Aarti + Light & Sound show.
  - _Day 2:_ Nageshwarnath, Guptar Ghat, Ramkatha Park, Tulsi Smarak, local markets, departure.
- **Inclusions:** Private AC cab, guide on request, sunrise/sunset ghat runs, breakfast.
- **Lead time:** 7 days regular, 15 days for Ram Navami/Deepotsav.

### 2.4 Ayodhya–Varanasi 3-Day Twin City Circuit
- **Pricing:** Transport-only ₹12,000–₹18,000 (sedan/SUV) + hotels; Package PP ₹15,000–₹29,000 depending on tier.
- **Routing (popular “Varanasi first”):**
  - Day 1 Varanasi arrival + sunset cruise.
  - Day 2 Sunrise boat, temple run, drive to Ayodhya, Saryu Aarti.
  - Day 3 Ayodhya temple marathon, depart from Ayodhya or drive back to VNS/Prayagraj.
- **Notes:** Add ₹3,000 sedan / ₹4,000 SUV per extra day if tacking Prayagraj/Vindhyachal.

### 4.4 Kushinagar Charter

## 9. Quote Builders & Policies
- **Sedan multi-day:** (₹3,000 base × days) + (night hits × ₹150) + add-ons.
- **SUV multi-day:** (₹4,200 base × days) + (night hits × ₹250) + add-ons.
- **Tempo Traveller:** (₹4,500–₹5,000 base × days depending on route) + driver night ₹500 if >22:00 wraps.
- **Per-person package math:** (Transport ÷ pax) + (hotel tier) + (activities). Round up to nearest ₹500 for negotiation buffer.
- **Festival buffer:** Add ₹2,000 per vehicle per day for Dev Deepawali/Mahashivratri closures + security lockdowns.
- **Cancellation cues:** 72+ hrs full refund minus ₹1,000 admin; 48 hrs 50%; <24 hrs no refund (unless medical proof). Always mention up front.
