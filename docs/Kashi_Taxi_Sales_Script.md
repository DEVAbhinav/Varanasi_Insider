# Kashi Taxi — Executive Calling Script (Conversion Focused)
## Simple English • High-Intent Framework

---

## OPENING (20 seconds)

**Script:**

"Hello [Name]! This is [Your Name] from Kashi Taxi. We help people like you experience Varanasi and nearby pilgrimage sites. How are you today? Do you have 2 minutes?"

**Why it works:**
- Warm greeting + quick identity
- Signals value immediately
- Permission-based (respects time)
- Assumes interest gently

**If YES:** Move to Need Discovery.  
**If NO:** "Perfect—I'll be quick. Are you planning a trip in the next 30 days?"

---

## NEED DISCOVERY (1 minute)

**Key Questions (pick 2–3 based on caller mood):**

1. **"What brings you to Varanasi?"**
   - *Listen for:* First-time vs. return, solo/family, spiritual vs. tourist

2. **"How many days do you have?"**
   - *Listen for:* Same-day, 1–2 days, 3+ days (drives package match)

3. **"Traveling with family, friends, or solo?"**
   - *Listen for:* Group size = vehicle type + price tier

4. **"Have you been before, or is this your first time?"**
   - *Listen for:* First-timers want guided highlights; repeats want depth/festivals

**Example response script:**
- *If same-day:* "Great! Perfect for the Highlights tour."
- *If 1–2 days:* "Ideal—you'll catch sunrise, rituals, AND evening Aarti."
- *If 3+ days:* "Amazing! You can go deep—temples, workshops, boat rides, everything."

## PACKAGE PITCH (1.5 minutes)

### For Same-Day Visitors (8–10 hours)

**Script:**

"Our Most Popular tour is ₹2,200 per person for a car with a driver. Includes Kashi Vishwanath, boat ride, and the evening Ganga Aarti—the most famous ritual in India. We also offer a deluxe version with a guide for ₹7,000–₹11,000 per person—guide handles temples, history, everything. What sounds better for you?"

**Why it works:**
- Two options = feels like choice
- Price low-anchor first (₹2,200) then high-ticket
- Mentions Aarti (FOMO trigger—everyone wants to see it)

**Closing line:**
"Most people who come same-day regret not staying overnight. Have you thought about that?"

---

### For 1–2 Night Travelers

**Script:**

"For 1 night, 2 days—we do a complete package. You get a hotel, breakfast, private car, guide, sunrise boat ride, AND evening Aarti. Budget options start at ₹3,999 per person. Standard comfort is ₹8,000–₹10,000. Luxury is ₹12,000–₹15,000. What's your comfort level?"

**Why it works:**
- All-inclusive feels simpler than itemizing
- Three tiers let customer self-segment
- Hotels bundled (takes friction out)

**Closing line:**
"Most guests add a boat photographer for ₹800—captures the sunrise perfectly for Instagram. Worth it?"

---

### For 3+ Day Travelers (Deep Dive)

**Script:**

**Why it works:**

- Emotional hook (understand vs. tick boxes)
- Luxury option available (no ceiling)

## OBJECTION HANDLING

### "It's too expensive."

**Script:**

"I understand. Let me ask—how many days are you here? Because if it's 1 night, the budget option is ₹3,999. That's less than a decent dinner in a city. And it includes a hotel, car, guide, and two boat rides. What if we started with the car-only option at ₹2,200, and you see if you want to add a guide later? How does that sound?"

**Why it works:**
- Reframes price as per-night
- Compares to familiar anchor (dinner)
- Offers a lower entry point (car-only)

---

### "I can book a tour online cheaper."

**Script:**

"You absolutely can. But here's the difference—we're locals. Our guide actually knows the priest at Kashi Vishwanath. If there's a line 2 hours long, we can skip it with a VIP pass for ₹1,000. Our boat is private—you're not squished with 50 tourists. And if you have any issue, you call us directly—not an app. Most people find they save money by avoiding tourist traps once they're here. Fair?"

**Why it works:**
- Admits online exists (credible)
- Cites insider advantage (skip lines = time = value)
- Local-ness overcomes aggregator price

---

### "I need to check with my [spouse/family/boss]."

**Script:**

"Sure—totally fair. Here's what I'll do. I'll send you a WhatsApp message right now with the package details, prices, and what's included. You can share it with them. Then just reply back, or I'll call you tomorrow at this time. Sound good? What's your WhatsApp number?"

**Why it works:**
- Unblocks decision (written summary > memory)
- Maintains momentum (tomorrow callback)
- Personal touch (WhatsApp vs. email)

---

### "I'm not sure about dates yet."

**Script:**

"No problem. But here's what you should know—during Diwali, Mahashivratri, and Dev Deepawali, prices go up and slots fill fast. If you're even *thinking* of those seasons, let me hold a spot for 48 hours. It's just a 30% deposit—about ₹2,000–₹3,000. No risk—if dates change, we move it or refund you. Want me to reserve it?"

**Why it works:**
- Scarcity + FOMO (festivals fill up)
- Low deposit barrier (₹2,000 easy)
- Removes risk (refund promise)

## TONE TIPS FOR HIGH CONVERSION

1. **Be a friend, not a salesman.** Share insider secrets (skip lines, local food spots, safe routes). They feel *guided*, not sold.

2. **Use their pace.** If they're fast talkers, match it. If they're thoughtful, slow down. Mirror builds trust.

3. **Specificity wins.** Don't say "nice temples." Say "Kashi Vishwanath has 3,000 years of history and you can see the Ganges from the roof—most tourists miss that."

4. **Silence is your friend.** After you quote a price, STOP talking. Let them respond. Silence creates discomfort that pushes them to decide.

5. **Always have a next step.** Never end without: deposit, follow-up time, or referral ask. Dead air = dead lead.

## Common Mistakes to AVOID

❌ **Don't oversell.** List too many packages = confusion = no decision.  
❌ **Don't discount immediately.** Hold firm for 2 objections. Discount only on 3rd.  
❌ **Don't forget the deposit.** Always close with 30% advance. No deposit = no commitment.  
❌ **Don't leave without a next step.** Always say: "I'll call tomorrow" or "You WhatsApp me."  
❌ **Don't use jargon.** Avoid "itinerary spine," "CP/MAP/AP." Use plain words.  
❌ **Don't compare competitors.** Never say "Airbnb is cheaper." Just say "We're local—that's different."

## Final Pro Tip: The 3-Call Rule

If a lead says "let me think":

1. **Call 1 (Today):** Pitch + send WhatsApp summary
2. **Call 2 (Tomorrow):** Light check-in: "Any questions after looking at the package?"
3. **Call 3 (Day after):** Soft push: "Dev Deepawali slots filling—want me to hold yours?"

Most leads convert by Call 2 or 3. Don't give up after one "no."
