## 1. Context & Religious Significance

While millions visit Varanasi for Ganga Aarti, Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga, and the ghats, **Shardiya Navratri** represents one of the city's oldest, most revered feminine pilgrimage traditions: the **Kashi Nava Durga Yatra** (alongside the parallel **Kashi Nava Gauri Yatra**).

Mentioned in the *Kashi Khand* of the *Skanda Purana*, the 9 forms of Goddess Durga are consecrated in 9 specific, ancient temples across Varanasi. Unlike conventional cities where Navratri is celebrated only inside marquee temporary pandals, in Varanasi the core ritual is a physical pilgrimage (parikrama) across these 9 stone shrines.

Local belief holds that establishing the 9 Durgas protects the 8 directions and the sacred center of Kashi.

## 3. The 9 Temples: Exact Localities & Last-Mile Ground Realities

A major pitfall for outstation travellers is believing a taxi can drive "temple-to-temple". In the historic Pucca Mahal (old city), five of the nine temples are located inside narrow medieval pedestrian alleys (*galis*).

### 1. Maa Shailputri Temple (Day 1)
- **Locality:** Marhia Ghat, Jalalipura (near Alipura / Kashi Railway Station area, northern Varanasi, on the banks of River Varuna).
- **Vehicular Access:** 4-wheelers and autos can reach within 200–300 metres up to Marhia Ghat road.
- **Walking Required:** 3–5 minutes flat walk.
- **Crowd Expectation:** Extreme on Pratipada (Day 1). Hundreds of thousands queue starting 3:30 AM.
- **Best Time:** 4:30 AM – 6:30 AM, or late afternoon (2:00 PM – 4:00 PM).

### 2. Maa Brahmacharini Temple (Day 2)
- **Locality:** Ghasi Tola, directly above Balaji Ghat and Panchganga Ghat.
- **Vehicular Access:** Cars cannot reach directly. Nearest road drop is Machhodari Park / Kashi Station road (800m walk through alleys) OR Maidagin.
- **The Insider Riverboat Hack:** Take a wooden boat or motorboat from Assi or Dashashwamedh straight to Panchganga Ghat. Climb the stone steps directly to the temple. This avoids the entire road traffic jam of northern Varanasi!
- **Walking Required:** 5 minutes if coming by boat; 15 minutes through lanes if coming by road.

### 3. Maa Chandraghanta Temple (Day 3)
- **Locality:** Chandraghanta Gali, near Chowk Police Station.
- **Vehicular Access:** Absolutely no cars. During Navratri, Godowlia to Chowk and Maidagin to Chowk are strictly pedestrian-only zones.
- **Nearest Drop:** Maidagin Crossing (from north) or Godowlia / Benia Bagh (from south).
- **Walking Required:** 500–700 metres inside Chowk galis (approx. 10–12 minutes).

### 4. Maa Kushmanda Temple (Day 4)
- **Locality:** Durga Kund, Sankat Mochan Road, southern Varanasi.
- **Vehicular Access:** High. Broad roads connect from Assi, Lanka, and Bhelupur.
- **Nearest Drop:** Kabir Nagar crossing or temple outer parking zone (approx. 100 metres).
- **Crowd Expectation:** Massive year-round, but exceptionally packed on Chaturthi and during the Navratri fair. Expect barricaded queue lines.

### 5. Maa Skandamata Temple (Day 5)
- **Locality:** Jaitpura, near Jaitpura Police Station and Bageshwari Devi Mandir.
- **Vehicular Access:** Cabs and auto-rickshaws can reach within 150 metres via Jaitpura main road.
- **Walking Required:** 2–3 minutes through local mohalla lane.

### 6. Maa Katyayani Temple (Day 6)
- **Locality:** Atmaveereshwar Mahadev Temple complex, D.22/37 Chhatta Dwar / Scindia Ghat.
- **Vehicular Access:** None. Steep river steps or narrow ancient galis.
- **Nearest Access:** Either arrive by boat at Scindia Ghat / Manikarnika side and climb the steps (approx 100 steps), or walk 15 minutes from Chowk / Brahma Nal.
- **Insider Note:** The temple shares premises with the ancient Atmaveereshwar Lingam, one of Kashi's most powerful Shiva shrines.

### 7. Maa Kaalratri Temple (Day 7)
- **Locality:** D.8/17 Kalika Gali, situated between Dashashwamedh Ghat and Tripura Bhairavi / Meer Ghat.
- **Vehicular Access:** None.
- **Nearest Drop:** Girjaghar Crossing or Benia Bagh parking (1.2 km walk through Godowlia), or Dashashwamedh Ghat via boat.
- **Walking Required:** 8–10 minutes from Dashashwamedh Ghat; 15–20 minutes from Girjaghar.
- **Crowd Expectation:** Massive on Saptami (Day 7) night. Believed to dissolve planetary afflictions (especially Saturn/Rahu).

### 8. Maa Mahagauri Temple (Day 8)
- **Locality:** Maa Annapurna Temple, inside the Kashi Vishwanath Corridor complex (Gate 4, Dhundhiraj Gali / Vishwanath Gali).
- **Vehicular Access:** None. Pedestrian corridor only.
- **Nearest Drop:** Maidagin (via Gate 4 entrance) or Godowlia.
- **Crowd Expectation:** Phenomenal on Ashtami (Day 8). Annapurna Temple also holds special Suvarna (Golden) Annapurna darshan during Dhanteras/Diwali, but on Mahagauri day during Navratri, local and outstation crowds converge simultaneously.
- **Best Time:** 5:00 AM – 7:00 AM.

### 9. Maa Siddhidatri Temple (Day 9)
- **Locality:** Siddhamata Gali, Golghar, near Maidagin Crossing.
- **Vehicular Access:** Auto or cab drop at Maidagin roundabout.
- **Walking Required:** 150–200 metres (3–4 minutes walk into Golghar lane).
- **Crowd Expectation:** High on Navami (Day 9). Concludes the Navratri parikrama.

## 4. Key Visitor Doubts & Logistics Solutions

### Dilemma 1: "I only have 1 or 2 days in Varanasi. Can I complete the Nava Durga Yatra?"
**Solution:** Yes. While local devotees visit one temple per calendar day according to the Tithi, visiting pilgrims traditionally perform the **Sampoorna Nava Durga Parikrama** in a 1-day (intense 8–10 hour) or 2-day (relaxed) circuit.

**Optimized 1-Day Circuit:**
1. **05:00 AM – 06:30 AM (Outer North by Cab):** Maa Shailputri (Jalalipura) + Maa Skandamata (Jaitpura).
2. **07:00 AM – 09:30 AM (Old City / Ghats by Boat + Walk):** Drop at Maidagin or Ghat; take boat to Panchganga Ghat for Maa Brahmacharini, then walk to Scindia Ghat for Maa Katyayani.
3. **10:00 AM – 12:30 PM (Core Gali Cluster):** Walk to Chowk for Maa Chandraghanta, Annapurna for Maa Mahagauri, Kalika Gali for Maa Kaalratri, and Golghar for Maa Siddhidatri.
4. **01:00 PM – 03:00 PM:** Lunch & rest (Phalahari meal).
5. **04:30 PM – 06:00 PM (South by Cab):** Maa Kushmanda at Durga Kund, followed by evening Aarti at Assi or Dashashwamedh.

### Dilemma 2: "What are the police traffic barricades during Navratri?"
- Between 09:00 AM and 11:00 PM throughout Navratri:
  - **Godowlia Crossing** is closed to all 4-wheelers and three-wheelers.
  - Cabs from Cantt or Airport are stopped at **Girjaghar** or **Benia Bagh Multilevel Parking**.
  - **Maidagin Crossing** is restricted to one-way or auto-drops only; no tourist cars allowed towards Chowk.
  - **Durga Kund Road** has barricades restricting curb parking.

### Dilemma 3: "How to avoid 4-hour queues?"
- **The Pre-Dawn Window (4:30 AM – 7:00 AM):** Temples open at 4:00 AM for Mangala Aarti. Entering between 4:30 AM and 6:30 AM cuts waiting from 3 hours to 20–30 minutes.
- **Afternoon Lull (1:30 PM – 3:30 PM):** While some inner sanctums close briefly for bhog (12:00 PM – 1:00 PM or 2:00 PM), early afternoon offers much thinner crowds than evenings.

### Dilemma 4: "Senior Citizen & Wheelchair Accessibility"
- **Temples with decent accessibility:** Durga Kund (Maa Kushmanda) and Jaitpura (Maa Skandamata) have relatively flat approach roads where wheelchairs can be used up to the outer gate.
- **Temples with high step/gali barriers:** Maa Brahmacharini and Maa Katyayani require navigating ghat steps or uneven stone galis. For seniors, using an experienced local guide and cycle-rickshaw up to the nearest entry point, accompanied by gentle pacing, is essential.

### Dilemma 5: "Where to stay during Navratri?"
- **Do NOT book inside deep Godowlia lanes if carrying heavy luggage:** You will be forced to drag wheeled bags 1 km through barricaded streets and crowds.
- **Recommended zones:**
  - **Assi Ghat / Bhelupur:** Accessible by car, close to Durga Kund, peaceful cafes, easy boat access to northern ghats.
  - **Maidagin / Visheshwarganj:** Strategic for old city entry without the Godowlia gridlock.
  - **Varanasi Cantonment (Cantt):** Ideal for seniors who want spacious 4-star/5-star hotels with secure parking and reliable cab pickup.

### Dilemma 6: "Where to get Vrat / Phalahari Food?"
- **Shivpur / Sigra / Bhelupur:** Several restaurants offer dedicated *Navratri Vrat Thalis* (Kuttu atta puris, Sabudana khichdi, Makhana kheer, Sendha namak aloo).
- **Keshari Ruchikar Byanjan (near Dashashwamedh):** Popular for sattvic options.
- **Assi Ghat bakeries & cafes (Vaatika, Pizzeria, Terra Cotta):** Fresh fruit bowls, lassi, tender coconut, and sattvic dishes.

## 5. Parallel Highlights: Durga Puja Pandals in Varanasi

Varanasi has a 200+ year-old Bengali diaspora centered in **Bengali Tola, Sonarpura, and Bhelupur**. During Shasthi to Navami (Oct 16–19, 2026), the city transforms with artistic clay idols and Dhunuchi dance:
- **Sanatan Dharm Inter College (Nai Sarak):** One of Kashi's oldest and grandest traditional Durga Puja pandals.
- **Premier Club (Sigra):** Renowned for innovative theme pandals.
- **Bengali Tola & Pandey Ghat:** Intimate traditional community pujas with authentic Bengali rituals.
