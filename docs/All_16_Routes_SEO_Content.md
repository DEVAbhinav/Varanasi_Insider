### Route Overview Section

**Heading**: "Varanasi to Prayagraj Cab Service - Distance, Time & Route Details"

The spiritual journey from Varanasi to Prayagraj (formerly known as Allahabad) is one of the most popular short-distance routes for pilgrims and travelers. Located approximately **120-125 kilometers apart**, the drive takes around **2.5 to 3 hours** of comfortable travel via National Highway 19 (NH19). This route is known for its smooth, well-maintained roads and minimal traffic interruptions, making it an ideal choice for quick pilgrimages or day trips.

**Journey Distance**: 120-125 km (by road)
**Travel Time**: 2.5-3 hours (typical, without traffic)
**Best Route**: Via NH19 through Handia (fastest and most popular)
**Road Condition**: Excellent - well-maintained highways, minimal traffic
**Toll Gates**: 1-2 toll plazas (approximately ₹100-150 total)

**Seasonal Considerations**: The Varanasi to Prayagraj route remains open year-round. However, during monsoon (July-September), occasional waterlogging in low-lying areas may add 15-20 minutes to travel time. Winter months (October-February) offer the most pleasant traveling conditions with clear skies and comfortable temperatures.

**Best Time to Travel**: Depart between 6:00 AM - 10:00 AM to avoid evening traffic congestion as you approach Prayagraj city. Early morning starts allow you to spend more time at Triveni Sangam and other temples.
